How to Get There

From Louisville, KY (70 mi): Take I-64 W to IN-37 N in English, then follow IN-145 N to IN-56 W into French Lick.

Overview

French Lick is a small resort town in the hills of southern Indiana, about 100 miles south of Indianapolis, and it is known for its grand historic hotels, mineral springs, golf, and a casino. Together with neighboring West Baden Springs, it is home to the French Lick Springs Hotel and the West Baden Springs Hotel, whose enormous domed atrium was once called the "Eighth Wonder of the World." French Lick is also the hometown of basketball legend Larry Bird.

Visitors come to French Lick for a resort getaway with golf courses designed by Donald Ross and Pete Dye, spas, and gaming, as well as for the French Lick Scenic Railway, the nearby Patoka Lake, and the hiking and fall color of the Hoosier National Forest. The town has been a retreat for travelers for more than 150 years, and it offers a mix of elegance and small-town charm in the forested hills of Orange County.

History

The town gets its name from a French trading post built near a salt lick where animals gathered, and in the nineteenth century its mineral springs drew visitors looking for health cures. The French Lick Springs Hotel opened in 1845 and was expanded by Thomas Taggart, a Democratic Party leader, who also marketed "Pluto Water," a bottled mineral water used as a laxative. The nearby West Baden Springs Hotel opened its domed atrium in 1902, and by the early twentieth century the two resorts attracted celebrities, politicians, and gangsters. Both declined after the Great Depression, and after decades of neglect they were restored and reopened in the early 2000s, with the West Baden Springs Hotel designated a National Historic Landmark.

Sightseeing and Popular Landmarks

The West Baden Springs Hotel's 200-foot domed atrium was the largest free-spanning dome in the world when it was built, and the French Lick Springs Hotel has a grand lobby, a mineral springs pavilion, and a historic casino. The French Lick Scenic Railway offers excursions in restored vintage train cars through the countryside and a tunnel, and the Indiana Railway Museum is nearby. Other sights include Larry Bird's Boston Connection, the Wilstem Wildlife Park, and the Springs Valley area, which is known for its antique shops and wineries.

Culture and Museums

The French Lick Springs Hotel has a collection of historic photographs and memorabilia, and the Indiana Railway Museum has a collection of vintage locomotives and train cars. Fans of basketball can visit the Larry Bird statue and exhibit at the French Lick Resort, and the resort's casino, spas, and golf courses are among the area's most popular activities. The nearby town of Jasper has a German heritage, and Santa Claus, Indiana, is a short drive away.

Parks

The hills around French Lick are part of the Hoosier National Forest, which has hiking, horseback riding, and camping, and Patoka Lake, the second-largest lake in Indiana, is about 20 miles away and has boating, fishing, and wildlife viewing. The Buffalo Trace Trail and the grounds of the resorts are good for walks, and the Pete Dye Course at French Lick is ranked among the best golf courses in the country. Springs Valley Park and the area's state forests round out the options.

Seasons and Weather

French Lick has a humid subtropical climate with hot, humid summers and mild to cool winters. Summer highs are in the upper 80s°F, winter highs are in the 40s°F, and snowfall averages about 12 inches a year. Spring and fall are the best times for golf and hiking, and the fall foliage in the surrounding hills is a highlight. The resorts are busiest on summer weekends and holidays, so reservations are recommended.

Things to Do

Experience Indiana's Historic Resort Town

French Lick is a charming resort community in southern Indiana known for its mineral springs, historic hotels, scenic hills, and recreational attractions. Nestled in the rolling landscapes of the Hoosier National Forest region, the town has attracted visitors for more than a century seeking relaxation, outdoor adventure, and historic charm.

Visit the French Lick Resort

The world-famous French Lick Resort features two historic luxury hotels, the French Lick Springs Hotel and West Baden Springs Hotel. Visitors can tour the magnificent properties, enjoy fine dining, relax at the spa, play championship golf courses, and admire the iconic six-story dome of the West Baden Springs Hotel.

Ride the French Lick Scenic Railway

This historic railroad offers scenic excursions through the hills of southern Indiana aboard restored passenger trains. Special seasonal events and themed train rides provide visitors with a unique way to experience the area's history and natural beauty.

Explore Patoka Lake and Outdoor Recreation

Nearby Patoka Lake is one of Indiana's largest reservoirs and offers boating, fishing, kayaking, swimming, hiking, wildlife viewing, and camping opportunities. The lake's scenic shoreline and surrounding forests make it a popular destination for outdoor enthusiasts.

Enjoy Gaming, Golf, and Entertainment

French Lick offers a variety of recreational opportunities, including casino gaming, championship golf courses, live entertainment, horseback riding, and shopping. The area has become one of Indiana's premier destination resorts while maintaining its historic character.

Top Trails

Buffalo Trace Trail

Length: Approximately 1.5 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Located within the French Lick Resort area, this scenic trail travels through wooded hills and provides opportunities to observe wildlife, native plants, and beautiful views of the surrounding countryside.

Hoosier National Forest Trails

Length: Variable distances available
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Easy to Strenuous

The nearby Hoosier National Forest offers numerous hiking opportunities through hardwood forests, ridges, valleys, and scenic overlooks. Visitors can choose from short nature walks to longer backcountry adventures.

Patoka Lake Trails

Length: More than 20 miles of trails
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Multiple trail systems around Patoka Lake provide access to forests, lake views, wildlife habitats, and recreation areas. Hiking, birdwatching, and photography are popular activities throughout the year.

French Lick Scenic Walking Routes

Length: Variable distances available
Elevation Gain: Minimal to Moderate
Difficulty: Easy

Visitors can enjoy walking through the historic downtown, resort grounds, and surrounding neighborhoods while exploring local shops, historic architecture, public art, and the town's unique resort atmosphere.
