How to Get There

From Duluth, MN (27 mi): Take MN-61 E (North Shore Scenic Drive) northeast from Duluth approximately 27 miles to Two Harbors.

Overview

Two Harbors is a city along the shore of Lake Superior. Minnesota State Highway 61 serves as a main route in Two Harbors. Gooseberry Falls State Park is 13 miles (21 km) to the northeast.

Two Harbors is the gateway city to the North Shore of Minnesota. Highway 61 is the only major highway heading north/northeast along Lake Superior's shore and is the fastest way to the North Shore's eight state parks, as well as wayside rests and lakeshore access. The parks are Gooseberry Falls, Split Rock Lighthouse, Tettegouche, George H. Crosby Manitou State Park, Temperance River State Park, Cascade River State Park, Judge C.R. Magney State Park, and Grand Portage State Park.

Things to Do

Explore Minnesota's Historic Lake Superior Harbor Town

Two Harbors is a scenic community located on the North Shore of Lake Superior, approximately 25 miles northeast of Duluth. Known as the birthplace of Minnesota's iron ore shipping industry, the city combines rich maritime history, stunning Lake Superior landscapes, outdoor recreation, and easy access to many of the North Shore's most popular attractions.

Visit Split Rock Lighthouse and North Shore Attractions

Two Harbors serves as an excellent base for exploring nearby destinations such as Split Rock Lighthouse State Park, Gooseberry Falls State Park, Tettegouche State Park, and the Superior Hiking Trail. Visitors can enjoy waterfalls, rugged shorelines, scenic overlooks, and some of Minnesota's finest outdoor recreation opportunities.

Discover Maritime and Railroad History

The city played a major role in the development of the Mesabi Iron Range and Great Lakes shipping industry. Visitors can explore the Lake County Historical Depot Museum, view historic ore docks, and learn about the region's railroad and shipping heritage.

See the Two Harbors Lighthouse

Built in 1892, the Two Harbors Lighthouse is the oldest continuously operating lighthouse in Minnesota. Located on Agate Bay, the lighthouse offers beautiful views of Lake Superior and provides insight into the maritime history that helped shape the North Shore.

Watch Great Lakes Freighters

Two Harbors is one of the best places on Lake Superior to observe massive ore boats and commercial freighters arriving and departing from the harbor. Waterfront viewing areas provide excellent opportunities for photography and ship watching throughout the year.

Top Trails

Superior Hiking Trail

Length: Variable distances available
Elevation Gain: Moderate to Strenuous
Difficulty: Moderate to Strenuous

Numerous access points near Two Harbors allow hikers to experience the renowned Superior Hiking Trail. Routes feature waterfalls, forested ridges, river crossings, and spectacular views of Lake Superior.

Sonju Trail

Length: Approximately 1.5 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

Located within the city, this family-friendly trail passes through forested areas and offers opportunities for walking, birdwatching, and enjoying nature close to downtown Two Harbors.

Gooseberry Falls Trail System

Length: Multiple trail options
Elevation Gain: Minimal to Moderate
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Just a short drive northeast of Two Harbors, Gooseberry Falls State Park offers scenic trails to waterfalls, river gorges, Lake Superior overlooks, and connections to the Gitchi-Gami State Trail.

Gitchi-Gami State Trail

Length: Variable distances available
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

This paved multi-use trail follows portions of Minnesota's North Shore and provides beautiful views of Lake Superior. It is popular for walking, running, cycling, and sightseeing.
