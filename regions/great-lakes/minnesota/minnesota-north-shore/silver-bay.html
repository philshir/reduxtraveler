How to Get There

From Duluth, MN (54 mi): Take MN-61 E (North Shore Scenic Drive) northeast from Duluth approximately 54 miles to Silver Bay.

Overview

Silver Bay is a city in Lake County, Minnesota. Silver Bay is located 28 miles northeast of Two Harbors, 54 miles northeast of Duluth, and 55 miles southwest of Grand Marais. It is about half way between Duluth and Grand Marais, along the North Shore of Lake Superior.

Tettegouche State Park, the Baptism River, and the Palisade Head rock formation are all nearby.

Summer hiking trails, winter cross country skiing, and maintained snowmobile trails are located within Silver Bay.

Things to Do

Discover Minnesota's Scenic North Shore Community

Silver Bay is a picturesque city on the North Shore of Lake Superior in northeastern Minnesota. Surrounded by rugged cliffs, boreal forests, waterfalls, and inland lakes, Silver Bay serves as an excellent base for exploring some of Minnesota's most spectacular natural attractions. The city offers abundant opportunities for hiking, sightseeing, photography, wildlife viewing, and outdoor recreation throughout the year.

Visit Tettegouche State Park

Located just north of Silver Bay, Tettegouche State Park is one of Minnesota's premier outdoor destinations. Visitors can explore dramatic Lake Superior cliffs, inland lakes, forests, waterfalls, and scenic overlooks. Popular attractions include Shovel Point, Palisade Head, High Falls of the Baptism River, and numerous hiking trails.

See the Palisade Head Overlook

Palisade Head is a towering cliff rising nearly 300 feet above Lake Superior and provides one of the most breathtaking viewpoints on the North Shore. Visitors can enjoy panoramic views of the lake, watch peregrine falcons, and photograph the rugged shoreline below.

Enjoy Lake Superior Recreation

Silver Bay offers access to boating, kayaking, fishing, swimming, photography, and shoreline exploration along Lake Superior. Black Beach, known for its unique dark-colored sand created by historical mining activities, has become one of the area's most popular attractions.

Observe Wildlife and North Woods Scenery

The forests and coastal habitats surrounding Silver Bay support white-tailed deer, black bears, foxes, moose, bald eagles, loons, and numerous migratory bird species. The area is particularly beautiful during fall color season and offers excellent opportunities for wildlife photography and birdwatching.

Top Trails

Shovel Point Trail

Length: Approximately 1.2 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Moderate

Located in nearby Tettegouche State Park, this scenic trail follows rocky cliffs above Lake Superior and provides some of the most dramatic coastal views on Minnesota's North Shore. Visitors enjoy breathtaking overlooks, rugged shoreline scenery, and excellent photography opportunities.

High Falls Trail

Length: Approximately 3 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Moderate

Following the Baptism River through Tettegouche State Park, this popular trail leads to High Falls, Minnesota's tallest waterfall entirely within the state. The route features river views, forest scenery, and striking rock formations.

Palisade Head Trail Area

Length: Variable short walks available
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

Short walking paths near Palisade Head provide access to dramatic overlooks above Lake Superior. The area is a favorite destination for sightseeing, photography, and viewing the rugged North Shore landscape.

Superior Hiking Trail

Length: Variable distances available
Elevation Gain: Moderate to Strenuous
Difficulty: Moderate to Strenuous

Several segments of the Superior Hiking Trail pass through the Silver Bay area. These trails traverse forests, ridges, rivers, waterfalls, and scenic overlooks while offering some of the finest hiking opportunities in the Midwest.
