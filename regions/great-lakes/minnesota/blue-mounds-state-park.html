Blue Mounds State Park

★5.0

State park protects an American bison herd which grazes on one of the state's largest prairie remnants

Pets allowed (6 ft max); not permitted in buildings
Closest cities with major hotel chains:
Sioux Falls, SD, 30 miles
Seasons:
All year
Location:
1410 161st St, Luverne, MN 56156
Website:
dnr.state.mn.us

How to Get There

From Sioux Falls, SD (30 mi): Take I-90 E from Sioux Falls approximately 25 miles to Exit 12 for US-75 N, then head north on US-75 and follow signs to the park entrance at 1410 161st St.

Overview

Blue Mounds State Park in southwestern Minnesota protects a mile-long Sioux quartzite cliff rising above tallgrass prairie, with a herd of bison that roams the park's grasslands. The cliff, about 1,600 million years old, was named by early settlers who mistook its bluish color for a mound. The park also has a prehistoric alignment of rocks that points to the sun on the equinox, and prairie wildflowers, camping and trails. Visitors come for the bison, the cliffs, the prairie and a taste of the Great Plains in Minnesota.

Access

The park is about 5 miles north of Luverne, in Rock County, reached by US-75 and County Road 20. It is about 30 miles east of Sioux Falls, South Dakota, and the route from I-90 is simple. The drive from Minneapolis takes about 3.5 hours. Most visitors arrive by car, and the park has several parking areas for trails.

Seasons

The park is open year-round. Summer is warm, with wildflowers in bloom and bison calves in spring. Fall brings golden grasses and good hiking, and winter is cold with snowshoeing and skiing. Spring is muddy, and the prairie begins to green. Mosquitoes can be intense in early summer. Bison viewing is good in any season, and the equinox sunrise is a special event.

Things to Do

Hike Along the Quartzite Cliff

Blue Mounds State Park's Upper Cliff Trail follows the top of the cliff, with views across the prairie. It is the park's best-known walk.

See the Bison Herd

A herd of bison roams a fenced portion of the park, and an overlook allows viewing. Visitors should stay at a safe distance.

Visit the Sun Alignment

A line of stones on the prairie points to sunrise on the equinox. It may be an ancient observatory.

Camp Beside the Prairie

The campground has sites near the cliff, and the night sky is dark. Cabins and camper cabins are sometimes available.

Climb the Rock

The cliff offers rock climbing with permits, and rappelling is also popular. Check regulations.

Top Trails

Mound Trail

Length: Approximately 1.5 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Approximately 100 feet
Difficulty: Easy

This trail climbs the cliff to a view of the prairie. It is a good option for families.

Upper Cliff Trail

Length: Approximately 2 miles one way
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

A trail along the top of the quartzite cliff gives broad views. It can be windy.

Lower Cliff Trail

Length: Approximately 1 mile loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A shorter path at the base of the cliff passes tallgrass and boulders. It is flat and good for birds.

Visitor Centers

The Blue Mounds State Park Interpretive Center, near the Mound Creek area, has exhibits on the prairie, the bison and the cliff, along with restrooms and staff who give programs. Hours are seasonal and weekdays may differ, so check the Minnesota DNR website. A park office near the entrance sells permits.

Camping and Lodging

The park's campground has about 73 sites, some with electric hookups, plus a camper cabin and group camp. Luverne, only minutes away, has hotels such as the Super 8 and local inns, and Sioux Falls, about 40 minutes west, has many hotels, including the Hotel on Phillips. Pipestone, about 45 minutes north, also has lodging. Check each property's website for current availability.
