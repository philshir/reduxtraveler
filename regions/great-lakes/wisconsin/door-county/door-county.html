Overview

Door County is the easternmost county in Wisconsin, covering most of the scenic Door Peninsula between Lake Michigan and Green Bay. One of the Upper Midwest's most popular vacation destinations, the county has nearly 300 miles of shoreline and a reputation as "the Cape Cod of the Midwest," built on its beaches, orchards, and small harbor towns.

State Parks and Attractions

Door County is home to six state parks, including Peninsula State Park near Fish Creek, Potawatomi State Park along Sturgeon Bay, and Whitefish Dunes State Park, home to Old Baldy, the state's tallest sand dune. Other natural highlights include the limestone cliffs of Cave Point County Park and the Ridges Sanctuary, among several protected areas established through decades of local preservation efforts.

Beaches

With more than 50 public beaches and boat launches along both its Lake Michigan and Green Bay shores, the county is also a popular destination for kayaking, boating, and swimming.
