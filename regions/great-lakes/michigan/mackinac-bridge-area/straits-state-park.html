How to Get There

From Best Western Harbour Pointe Lakefront (1 mi): Follow North State Street and Church Street west through St. Ignace toward the shoreline entrance of Straits State Park overlooking the Mackinac Bridge and the Straits of Mackinac.

Overview

Straits State Park sits on a scenic stretch of shoreline in St. Ignace, where Lakes Michigan and Huron meet beneath the towering Mackinac Bridge. It is one of Michigan's oldest state parks, with forested campsites, peaceful walking trails and panoramic overlooks of the bridge and the passing freighters. The park is a prime spot for sunsets and for summer fireworks and bridge events. People come to camp, picnic and enjoy views of one of the most recognizable landmarks in the Great Lakes while staying close to St. Ignace, Mackinac Island ferries and the Upper Peninsula's attractions.

Access

The park is at 720 Church Street in St. Ignace, just off I-75 at the northern end of the Mackinac Bridge, about 1 mile from downtown St. Ignace. It is about 5 hours from Detroit, 6 hours from Chicago and 50 miles from Sault Ste. Marie. Visitors arrive by car, and a Michigan Recreation Passport is required for vehicle entry. The park is a short drive from the ferry docks for Mackinac Island.

Seasons

The park is open daily year-round from 8 a.m. to 10 p.m., and the campground and visitor facilities operate mainly from late spring through fall. Summer is the busiest season, with warm days and big crowds for the Fourth of July fireworks. Fall brings foliage and quieter evenings, and winter is cold and snowy, with limited services. Always check the camping season before visiting.

Things to Do

See the Mackinac Bridge

Overlooks and shoreline spots give clear views of the five-mile bridge, especially at sunset and when lit at night.

Watch Freighters and Boat Traffic

Large lake freighters and ships regularly pass through the straits, making a good setting for picnics.

Camp in the Woods

Wooded campsites offer a convenient base for a stay in the Mackinac region.

Take the Ferry to Mackinac Island

Ferries leave from St. Ignace and nearby Mackinaw City for the car-free island.

Explore St. Ignace

The town has historic sites, the Castle Rock lookout, and waterfront restaurants and shops.

Top Trails

Straits Shoreline Trail

Length: Approximately 1 mile round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A short path follows the shoreline with views of the bridge and the straits. It is flat and well suited for families.

Straits Woodland Trail

Length: Approximately 2 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal to moderate
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

This trail winds through the pine and hardwood forest behind the shoreline and connects the campground with viewpoints.

Visitor Centers

The park has a visitor center and park office near the entrance, with exhibits about the Mackinac Bridge and the straits and staff who can provide camping details, trail maps and Michigan Recreation Passport information. The St. Ignace area visitor bureau also offers information on ferries, lodging and attractions.

Camping and Lodging

Straits State Park has a modern campground with electric sites and a number of wooded sites, as well as a camper cabin, reserved through the Michigan DNR. Hotels and motels are in St. Ignace, about 1 mile away, and in Mackinaw City, about 6 miles across the bridge, which have waterfront and budget options. Mackinac Island has historic hotels, though they are more expensive, and Sault Ste. Marie is about an hour north.
