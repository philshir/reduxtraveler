Lansing

★5.0

Capital city featuring government landmarks, university campuses, museums, riverfront parks, historic neighborhoods, and a strong automotive and cultural heritage.

General Information
Seasons:
All year, with especially pleasant visits during spring, summer, and autumn
Location:
Lansing, MI
Website:
lansing.org

How to Get There

From Hyatt Place Lansing/Eastwood Towne Center (5 mi): Follow US-127 S and downtown surface streets toward the Capitol district where museums, government buildings, riverfront parks, and major cultural attractions are concentrated throughout the city center.

Overview

Lansing is the capital of Michigan, in the south-central part of the Lower Peninsula along the Grand River, and it is the center of a metropolitan area that also includes East Lansing, home of Michigan State University. It is known for the Michigan State Capitol, for its history as the home of Oldsmobile and REO, and for the Spartans of Michigan State. The city is a government, education, and manufacturing center with a relaxed Midwestern feel.

Visitors come to Lansing for the Capitol, which offers free tours, for the museums such as the Michigan History Museum, the Eli and Edythe Broad Art Museum, the MSU Museum, and the R. E. Olds Transportation Museum. They also come for Spartan football and basketball games and for the riverfront and parks. Lansing is about 90 minutes from Detroit and Grand Rapids and is a convenient stop for travelers in mid-Michigan.

History

Lansing was chosen as the state capital in 1847 as a compromise because Detroit was considered too close to Canada and vulnerable to attack, and the site, a small settlement in a forest, was thought to be a remote and unimportant location. The state's capitol building was completed in 1879, and the city grew after the arrival of the railroads and the founding of the Michigan Agricultural College, the forerunner of Michigan State University, in 1855. Ransom E. Olds founded Oldsmobile in Lansing in 1897 and the REO Motor Car Company in 1905, making the city an important center for the early auto industry, and General Motors continues to operate plants in the area. Government and education now anchor the economy.

Sightseeing and Popular Landmarks

The Michigan State Capitol, completed in 1879 and designed by Elijah E. Myers, is a National Historic Landmark with a grand rotunda, a cast-iron dome, and restored interiors, and it offers free guided tours. The Michigan History Museum, the Library of Michigan, and the Michigan Hall of Justice are all located in the Capitol complex, and the Grand River Riverwalk runs through downtown. The Impression 5 Science Center, the Potter Park Zoo, and the Michigan Women's Historical Center are other attractions, and in East Lansing the Michigan State University campus has the Beal Botanical Garden.

Culture and Museums

The Michigan History Museum covers the state's past from prehistory to the present, and the Eli and Edythe Broad Art Museum, designed by Zaha Hadid, shows contemporary art on the MSU campus. The MSU Museum has natural history and cultural exhibits, and the R. E. Olds Transportation Museum has a collection of Oldsmobiles and REOs. The Wharton Center for Performing Arts, the Lansing Symphony Orchestra, and the Old Town district with its festivals and galleries provide entertainment.

Parks

Lansing has a large park system, including the Riverfront Trail, which runs for miles along the Grand River and connects with the Lansing River Trail, and Hawk Island Park, which has a lake, a beach, and a snow tubing hill. Potter Park Zoo is in a large park on the Red Cedar River, and Fenner Nature Center has trails and a nature center. The MSU campus is also a good place for walking, with the Red Cedar River, gardens, with the W. J. Beal Botanical Garden.

Seasons and Weather

Lansing has a humid continental climate with four distinct seasons. Summers are warm and humid, with highs in the 80s°F, and winters are cold and cloudy, with highs in the 30s°F and about 50 inches of snow. Spring and fall are mild, and fall color and football season make autumn a popular time to visit.
