How to Get There

Take US-61 North from Natchez for 4 miles, turn right onto State Park Road, then left onto Wickcliff Road and follow signs to the park entrance.

Overview

Bob M. Dearing Natchez State Park, commonly known as Natchez State Park, is a Mississippi state park managed by the Mississippi Department of Wildlife, Fisheries, and Parks. It lies in the pine and hardwood woodlands just north of historic Natchez and is built around Natchez Lake, a fishing lake known for largemouth bass, crappie and bream. The park provides residents and visitors a place for fishing, camping and outdoor recreation close to the historic river town. It is popular with families and anglers for its lakeside cabins, campsites, nature trails, picnic areas, playground and disc golf course. Many visitors combine a night or two here with sightseeing in Natchez and along the Natchez Trace.

Access

The park is on Wickcliff Road a short drive north of Natchez, reached from US-61 and State Park Road. Natchez is the nearest city, with Vicksburg to the north and Baton Rouge, Louisiana, to the south, each about one to two hours away. A car is needed, and there is no public transportation to the park. The entrance is staffed during the day, so check the park's hours before arriving late for cabin or campsite check-in. Reservations for cabins and campsites are available in advance and are recommended on weekends and in spring.

Seasons

Spring and fall are the most pleasant seasons, with mild weather for hiking, fishing and camping, and spring brings wildflowers and the busy Natchez pilgrimage season in town. Summers are hot, humid and buggy, with afternoon thunderstorms, though the lake is a popular place to cool off with a line in the water. Fall has comfortable temperatures and some leaf color. Winters are generally mild with occasional cold snaps, and the park stays open year-round with fewer visitors.

Things to Do

Fish on Natchez Lake

Anglers fish from the bank and from boats for bass, crappie, bream and catfish. A boat launch provides access to the lake, and fishing is the park's signature activity.

Play a Round of Disc Golf

The park has a nine-hole disc golf course set among the trees, a fun option for families and casual players.

Hike the Nature Trails

Trails wind through hardwood forest and along the lake, offering easy walks and chances to spot birds, deer and other wildlife.

Camp or Stay in a Cabin

Lakeside cabins and campsites make the park a good base for a weekend stay, with a playground and picnic areas for children and groups.

Visit Historic Natchez

The park is a short drive from the antebellum homes, bluffs and river views of Natchez and the southern end of the Natchez Trace Parkway.

Visitor Centers

Natchez State Park is a small, easygoing park without a large visitor center complex. The park office near the entrance is the main place to check in, ask about conditions and get information on trails, fishing and activities. For history and sightseeing in the area, the National Park Service visitor center in Natchez is a good stop.

Camping and Lodging

The park offers lodging and camping on site, including about 50 developed RV campsites, eight primitive tent sites and 10 lakeside cabins, according to the park's listing. Reservations are available well in advance. In Natchez, visitors will find chain hotels, historic inns and bed-and-breakfasts, and across the river Vidalia, Louisiana, has additional motels. Within one to two hours, Vicksburg, McComb and Baton Rouge offer more hotels and restaurants, and the Natchez Trace Parkway has primitive camping at a few locations along its route.
