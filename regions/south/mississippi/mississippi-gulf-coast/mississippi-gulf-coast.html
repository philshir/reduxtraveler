Mississippi Gulf Coast Travel Guide

Area of southern Mississippi along the Gulf of Mexico including the original French settlements in Biloxi and Ocean Springs, as well as Waveland, Bay St. Louis, Pass Christian, Long Beach, Gulfport, D'Iberville, Gautier, and Pascagoula.

Overview

The Mississippi Gulf Coast, also known as Coastal Mississippi or simply The Coast, is the stretch of southern Mississippi along the Mississippi Sound and the Gulf of Mexico, known for its white-sand beaches, historic French Gulf towns, and resort casinos.

Cities

The coast's cities include Biloxi and Ocean Springs, both originally French settlements, along with Bay St. Louis, Waveland, Gulfport, Pass Christian, and Pascagoula.

Since Mississippi legalized casino gambling in the 1990s, the coast, especially Biloxi, has become one of the leading gaming destinations in the South, with resort casinos now sitting alongside the region's long-standing seafood industry and growing tourism scene.
