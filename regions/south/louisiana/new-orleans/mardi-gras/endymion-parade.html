Massive Mardi Gras procession renowned for its enormous illuminated floats, celebrity grand marshals, high-energy atmosphere, and one of the largest crowds of the Carnival season.

General Information
Date:
Usually held on the Saturday before Mardi Gras as one of the premier events of the final Carnival weekend
Location:
Starts at the intersection of City Park Avenue and Orleans Avenue, New Orleans, LA
Ends at Caesars Superdome, New Orleans, LA
Website:
endymion.org

Parade Route

General Route: The parade begins at City Park Avenue and Orleans Avenue, travels along Orleans Avenue to North Carrollton Avenue, continues onto Canal Street through Mid-City and downtown New Orleans, turns onto St. Charles Avenue and circles Harmony Circle, proceeds along Howard Avenue and Loyola Avenue, then follows Girod Street to the Caesars Superdome where the parade concludes and transitions into the Endymion Extravaganza.

Overview

The Krewe of Endymion is one of the most famous and spectacular Mardi Gras parades in New Orleans. Founded in 1967 and elevated to "Super Krewe" status in the 1970s, Endymion has become known for producing some of the largest, most technologically advanced floats in Carnival. Brilliant fiber-optic displays, animated props, elaborate themes, and millions of throws create an unforgettable experience for spectators who gather by the hundreds of thousands along the route. The krewe takes its name from Endymion, a figure from Greek mythology associated with eternal youth and beauty.

What distinguishes Endymion from other parades is its combination of massive scale and entertainment value. The procession features thousands of riders, dozens of floats, marching bands, and celebrity guests, culminating at the Caesars Superdome for the legendary Endymion Extravaganza. The event is preceded by the popular Samedi Gras celebration in Mid-City, where music, food, and festivities fill the streets hours before the first float rolls. For many visitors, Endymion represents the ultimate Mardi Gras spectacle and remains one of the most anticipated events of the entire Carnival season.
