Parade Route

General Route: The parade begins at Napoleon Avenue and Tchoupitoulas Street, travels along Napoleon Avenue to St. Charles Avenue, proceeds through Uptown New Orleans and around Harmony Circle, continues along St. Charles Avenue to Canal Street, follows Canal Street toward the river, turns onto Tchoupitoulas Street, then continues to Convention Center Boulevard and finishes near the Ernest N. Morial Convention Center.

Overview

The Krewe of Bacchus Parade is one of New Orleans' most celebrated Mardi Gras traditions and ranks among the largest parades of the Carnival season. Founded in 1968, the krewe helped transform Mardi Gras by introducing the concept of high-profile celebrity kings known as the "Bacchus Monarch." Each year, enormous themed floats, elaborate costumes, marching bands, dance teams, and hundreds of riders create a spectacle that draws visitors from around the world. The event combines classic Carnival pageantry with a distinctly modern flair, making it a highlight of the city's annual festivities.

Rolling through some of New Orleans' most historic neighborhoods, the parade showcases the energy, creativity, and community spirit that define Mardi Gras. Visitors line St. Charles Avenue hours before the procession begins, creating a festive atmosphere filled with music, food, and anticipation. From dazzling float designs and signature throws to appearances by celebrities and local organizations, Bacchus delivers a memorable Carnival experience while celebrating the culture and traditions that have made New Orleans famous worldwide.
