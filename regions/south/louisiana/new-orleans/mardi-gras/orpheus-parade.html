Renowned Mardi Gras super krewe parade featuring spectacular illuminated floats, musical themes, celebrity appearances, and a grand finale celebration unique to Carnival season.

General Information
Date:
Usually held on Lundi Gras, the Monday before Mardi Gras Day, during the final evening of the Carnival season
Location:
Starts at Napoleon Avenue and Tchoupitoulas Street, New Orleans, LA, Ends near the Ernest N. Morial Convention Center at Convention Center Boulevard and Henderson Street, New Orleans, LA
Website:
kreweoforpheus.com

Parade Route

General Route: The parade begins at Napoleon Avenue and Tchoupitoulas Street, proceeds along Napoleon Avenue to St. Charles Avenue, follows St. Charles through Uptown New Orleans and around Harmony Circle, continues to Canal Street, turns onto Tchoupitoulas Street, then travels to Julia Street and Convention Center Boulevard before ending near the Ernest N. Morial Convention Center.

Overview

The Krewe of Orpheus is one of New Orleans' most celebrated Mardi Gras super krewes. Founded in 1993 by musician and actor Harry Connick Jr., along with his father Harry Connick Sr., Captain Sonny Borey, and Artistic Director Derek Franklin, Orpheus quickly grew into one of the most anticipated events of Carnival. The krewe was notable for becoming the first modern super krewe to welcome both male and female riders, helping redefine Mardi Gras traditions for a new generation. Known for its elaborate floats, signature throws, and celebrity monarchs, Orpheus draws enormous crowds each year on Lundi Gras.

Among the parade's highlights are the massive Orpheus Leviathan, an iconic multi-section float illuminated with extensive fiber-optic lighting, as well as themed floats, marching bands, and costumed riders who fill the streets with music and excitement. The celebration concludes with the famous Orpheuscapade, a large-scale party held at the Ernest N. Morial Convention Center that is open to visitors as well as krewe members. Combining spectacle, music, and New Orleans tradition, the Krewe of Orpheus has become one of the signature experiences of Mardi Gras.
