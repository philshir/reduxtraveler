How to Get There

From Houston (208 mi): Take I‑69/US‑59 S toward Victoria, continue onto US‑77 S or I‑37 S, and follow signs into the city center and bayfront districts.

Overview

Corpus Christi is a port city on the Gulf of Mexico in South Texas, and it is known as the "Sparkling City by the Sea" for its bayfront, its beaches, and its year-round warm weather. It is the gateway to Padre Island National Seashore, the longest stretch of undeveloped barrier island in the world, and the home of the USS Lexington, a World War II aircraft carrier turned museum. The city is also the center of a large oil, gas, and shipping industry, with the Port of Corpus Christi among the busiest in the country.

Visitors come to Corpus Christi for the USS Lexington Museum on the Bay, the Texas State Aquarium, and the beaches of Padre Island and Mustang Island. They also come for fishing, windsurfing, and bird watching, for the Selena Museum and the Selena memorial, and for the Tex-Mex and seafood restaurants along the bay.

History

The Spanish explorer Alonso Álvarez de Pineda is said to have named the bay "Corpus Christi" when he found it in 1519 on the feast day of that name, and the area was home to the Karankawa people. The city was founded in 1839 as a trading post by Henry Kinney, and in 1845 General Zachary Taylor camped his army here before the Mexican–American War. The Texas coast has been shaped by hurricanes, and a major storm in 1919 killed hundreds of people, after which a seawall was built. Oil, shipping, and the naval air station, established in 1941, drove the city's growth in the twentieth century, and in 2017 Hurricane Harvey struck the area.

Sightseeing and Popular Landmarks

The USS Lexington, known as the "Blue Ghost," is a floating museum with a flight deck, a hangar bay, and a flight simulator, and the Texas State Aquarium has exhibits on Gulf of Mexico wildlife, including sharks and sea turtles. The Corpus Christi Bayfront has a seawall promenade and the Water Street Market, and the Selena memorial statue, the Mirador de la Flor, honors the singer. The Art Museum of South Texas and the Texas State Museum of Asian Cultures are in the museum district.

Culture and Museums

The Art Museum of South Texas, designed by Philip Johnson, has a collection of regional and national art, and the Selena Museum honors the Tejano singer Selena Quintanilla-Pérez. The Texas State Museum of Asian Cultures shows art and artifacts from Asia, and the Corpus Christi Museum of Science and History has exhibits on shipwrecks and Spanish treasure. The city also hosts the Bayfest music festival and the Texas Jazz Festival.

Parks

Padre Island National Seashore, south of the city, has 70 miles of undeveloped beach, dunes, and wildlife, including nesting sea turtles, and is popular for camping and fishing. Mustang Island State Park, North Beach, and Corpus Christi Beach offer swimming and sunning, and Oso Bay Wetlands and the Blucher Park nature areas are good for birding. The bay is popular for sailing, windsurfing, and kiteboarding because of the steady winds.

Seasons and Weather

Corpus Christi has a humid subtropical climate, with hot, humid summers and mild winters. Summer highs are in the mid 90s°F, winter highs are in the 60s°F, and snow is very rare. A sea breeze keeps the city a bit cooler than inland Texas, and spring and fall are the most pleasant seasons. Hurricane season runs from June to November.

Things to Do

Relax on Corpus Christi Beaches
Enjoy miles of Gulf Coast shoreline perfect for swimming, sunbathing, beachcombing, and waterfront recreation.

Tour the USS Lexington Museum
Step aboard the historic World War II aircraft carrier known as the "Blue Ghost" and explore flight decks, aircraft exhibits, simulators, theaters, and naval history displays.

Visit the Texas State Aquarium
Explore one of Texas' premier aquariums featuring dolphins, sharks, sea turtles, stingrays, otters, and interactive exhibits focused on Gulf Coast marine life and conservation.

Explore Padre Island and North Beach
Experience coastal scenery, beach activities, wildlife viewing, and water sports along some of the region's most popular shorelines.

Visit the Corpus Christi Museum of Science and History
Learn about South Texas history, archaeology, shipwrecks, and regional culture through engaging exhibits and collections.

Tour the South Texas Botanical Gardens & Nature Center
Discover beautiful gardens, butterflies, birds, orchids, and native coastal plants in a peaceful outdoor setting.

Walk the Downtown Waterfront
Stroll along the bayfront, enjoy public art, coastal views, restaurants, parks, and scenic overlooks of Corpus Christi Bay.

Visit Selena Memorial
Pay tribute to the beloved Tejano music icon at the famous Mirador de la Flor memorial overlooking the bay.

Enjoy Water Sports
Take advantage of excellent conditions for kayaking, paddleboarding, boating, sailing, kiteboarding, and windsurfing.

Explore Local Arts and Culture
Visit the Art Museum of South Texas, Art Center of Corpus Christi, galleries, theaters, and cultural attractions throughout the city.
