Overview

South Congress Avenue, known locally as SoCo, is one of Austin's most popular shopping, dining, and entertainment districts. The avenue runs south from the Colorado River to Ben White Boulevard, bordered by the Travis Heights and Bouldin Creek neighborhoods, and offers a walkable stretch of eclectic boutiques, restaurants, live music venues, and street art. Its most photographed feature is the unobstructed view north to the Texas State Capitol, but the real draw is SoCo's laid-back, distinctly Austin character.

History

South Congress began in the 1850s as the main road linking Austin to San Antonio, but stayed largely rural until a concrete bridge and streetcar line brought steady growth in the early 1900s. Landmarks such as the Austin Motel and Hotel San José opened between the 1930s and 1950s, and the avenue became a magnet for musicians and artists in the 1970s with venues like the Armadillo World Headquarters. After a decline that followed the construction of I-35, cheap rents drew independent retailers back to South Congress in the late 1980s and 1990s, sparking the revival that shaped the shopping and cultural district visitors know today.

Popular Attractions and Activities

Shopping

South Congress is one of Austin's top shopping districts, featuring a mix of local businesses, specialty retailers, and uniquely Austin experiences.

  • Allen's Boots – Iconic Texas boot retailer offering cowboy boots, hats, and western apparel.
  • Locally owned boutiques featuring clothing, jewelry, home décor, and gifts.
  • Specialty shops selling Austin-themed merchandise and souvenirs.
  • Vintage and artisan stores showcasing local creators.

First Thursday

On the first Thursday of every month, shops, galleries, and restaurants between Barton Springs Road and Elizabeth Street stay open late, turning South Congress into a lively evening street festival.

Live Music

Music remains an important part of the South Congress experience, with venues hosting local and touring artists throughout the year.

  • The Continental Club – One of Austin's most famous live music venues.
  • Intimate performance spaces featuring local musicians.
  • Outdoor concerts and special events during select periods.

Public Art and Murals

South Congress doubles as an open-air gallery, with colorful murals painted across storefronts and side walls up and down the avenue. A few have become bona fide Austin landmarks, drawing steady lines of visitors waiting for a photo.

  • "I Love You So Much" – Painted on the side of Jo's Coffee in 2010 by local musician Amy Cook, this is one of the most photographed murals in Austin.
  • "Willie for President" – A tribute to Austin icon Willie Nelson on the side of Stag Provisions, near South Congress and Elizabeth Street.
  • "Greetings from Austin" – A vintage postcard-style mural a short walk off South Congress on S. 1st Street.
  • Rotating street art, storefront designs, and creative displays throughout the district.

Dining

The district offers an impressive variety of dining experiences ranging from casual eateries to Austin institutions.

  • Home Slice Pizza – Popular destination for New York-style pizza.
  • Guero's Taco Bar – Longtime Tex-Mex favorite that hosts live radio broadcasts on Wednesday evenings.
  • Coffee shops, bakeries, and dessert destinations.
  • Food trucks and casual outdoor dining options.

Historic Landmarks

Several notable historic properties contribute to the character of the South Congress district.

  • Hotel San José – Boutique hotel known for its minimalist design and local influence.
  • Historic commercial storefronts dating from earlier periods of Austin's development.
  • Classic neon signs and preserved architectural elements.

Capitol View Experience

One of the most memorable sights along South Congress Avenue is the unobstructed view north toward the Texas State Capitol building.

  • Excellent photography opportunities.
  • Particularly popular at sunrise and sunset.
  • One of Austin's most recognizable urban views.

Why Visit South Congress Avenue?

  • Experience Austin's culture through local businesses, music, and art.
  • Shop unique local retailers not commonly found elsewhere.
  • Enjoy iconic Austin food at popular restaurants and food trucks.
  • Capture memorable photos of murals and Capitol views.
  • Discover live music in one of the city's most celebrated entertainment districts.
