How to Get There

Get on TX-1 Loop N. Follow TX-1 Loop N to Mopac Service Rd. Take the exit toward FM 1325 S/Burnet Rd/Duval Rd from TX-1 Loop N. Take Domain Dr to Esperanza Crossing.

Overview

The Domain is a large, upscale shopping, dining, and entertainment district in Austin's tech corridor in northwest Austin, often nicknamed "Austin's second downtown." Since opening in 2007, it has grown into a walkable, mixed-use development combining high-end retail, restaurants, offices, hotels, and apartments.

Popular Attractions and Activities

Shopping

The Domain is anchored by several major department stores and packed with more than 100 specialty shops.

  • Neiman Marcus, Macy's, and Nordstrom – Anchor department stores.
  • Apple Store, Tiffany & Co., and Burberry – Among the upscale specialty retailers.
  • A wide mix of national and boutique fashion brands.

Dining

Restaurants throughout the Domain range from casual eateries to upscale dining rooms.

  • Sit-down restaurants serving American, steakhouse, and international cuisine.
  • Casual and fast-casual options for a quick bite between shops.

Nightlife and Entertainment

In the evenings, the Domain's bars and lounges draw a lively crowd.

  • Bars and cocktail lounges scattered throughout the district.
  • Live entertainment and seasonal events in the district's public plazas.

Stay and Explore

On-site hotels make the Domain a convenient home base for exploring Austin's tech corridor.

  • Hotels within walking distance of shopping and dining.
  • Nearby offices and tech campuses that give the district its energy on weekdays.

Why Visit The Domain?

  • Shop upscale brands at anchor department stores and specialty retailers.
  • Dine and unwind at a wide range of restaurants and bars.
  • Stay close to the action at one of the district's on-site hotels.
