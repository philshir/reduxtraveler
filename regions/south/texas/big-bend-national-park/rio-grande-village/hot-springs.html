How to Get There

From Study Butte / Terlingua (35 mi): Take TX-118 east into Big Bend National Park, follow the main park road toward Panther Junction, continue east on the Rio Grande Village Road, turn onto Hot Springs Road, and follow the road to the Hot Springs Historic District parking area near the river.

Overview

The Big Bend Hot Springs offer one of the most unique experiences in Big Bend National Park, where visitors can soak in warm mineral-rich waters while looking out across the Rio Grande into Mexico. The springs emerge from the ground at temperatures exceeding 100 degrees Fahrenheit and collect in a stone-lined soaking area along the riverbank. Surrounded by towering canyon walls, desert vegetation, and expansive river views, the site combines natural beauty with a sense of tranquility rarely found in such a remote setting.

Beyond its scenic appeal, the area is rich in history. Indigenous peoples used the springs for centuries before settlers established a small resort community here in the early twentieth century. Remnants of the Hot Springs Historic District, including foundations and interpretive exhibits, remain visible today and tell the story of the region's past. Visitors can explore the short trail to the springs, admire ancient pictographs and historic structures nearby, and enjoy one of the most memorable cultural and natural attractions within Big Bend National Park.
