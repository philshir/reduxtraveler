Linear park featuring a historic 60-mile towpath, canal locks, and scenic trails ideal for hiking, biking, and wildlife observation.

How to Get There

From Philadelphia (45 mi): Take I-95 N to Exit 49 for PA-332 W, continue to PA-413 N, then follow signs to Delaware Canal State Park via PA-32 N.

Overview

Delaware Canal State Park is a nearly 60-mile linear park along the Delaware River, preserving the towpath where mules pulled canal boats. Its original locks, aqueducts and stonework are still visible, and the flat trail links river towns such as New Hope and Easton for walking and biking. The park also has river islands and a 50-acre pond, with interpretive signs and seasonal events.

Access

The park runs from Easton to Bristol along the Delaware River, with access points in New Hope, Lumberville, Upper Black Eddy and Easton. The park office is at 11 Lodi Hill Road in Upper Black Eddy. Visitors drive on PA-32 and follow signs. Parking is free at most access points. There is no public transportation along the whole park.

Seasons

The towpath is open all year from sunrise to sunset. Spring and fall bring the best weather for walking and biking, and fall brings color. Summer is warm and busy in New Hope. Winters are cold, and ice can make the path slippery. Canal boat rides run in warm months in New Hope.

Things to Do

Walk or Bike the Towpath

A flat, shaded path follows the canal for nearly 60 miles.

Ride the Mule-Drawn Boat

Seasonal boat rides run at New Hope.

See Historic Locks

Stone locks and aqueducts remain along the canal.

Visit New Hope

The town has shops, galleries and restaurants.

Fish and Paddle the River

The Delaware River is nearby for boating.

Top Trails

New Hope to Lumberville Section

Length: Approximately 7 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A shaded path past the canal, with river views and a few historic buildings.

Lodi Hill to Uhlerstown Section

Length: Approximately 5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A quieter stretch with stone walls and woods.

Easton to Raubsville Section

Length: Approximately 6 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A towpath beside the Delaware River at the north end of the park.

Visitor Centers

The Delaware Canal State Park office in Upper Black Eddy has maps and information. The Locktender's House in New Hope has exhibits, and the Delaware Canal Visitor Center in Easton and the Delaware & Lehigh visitor offices offer more. Rangers lead walks in season.

Camping and Lodging

The park has no campground. Camping is available at Tinicum County Park and Nockamixon State Park cabins nearby. Hotels, inns and bed-and-breakfasts are in New Hope, Lambertville and Doylestown, and Easton and Philadelphia offer more.
