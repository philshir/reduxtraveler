Ricketts Glen State Park

★5.0
  1. Mid-Atlantic
  2. Pennsylvania
  3. Ricketts Glen State Park

lachrimae72, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

Expansive state park known for its old-growth forest, 22 named waterfalls, and diverse hiking trails.

General Information

Hours:
Open daily from sunrise to sunset
Fees:
Free admission
Pet Policy:
Pets allowed; not permitted in swimming areas
Closest cities with hotels:
Benton, PA (7 mi)
Seasons:
Spring through Fall for hiking and waterfalls; winter access for snowshoeing and ice climbing
Location:
695 State Route 487, Benton, PA 17814
Website:
dcnr.pa.gov

How to Get There

From Scranton, PA (60 mi): Take I-81 S to I-80 W. Exit at PA-487 N toward Benton. Continue north for approximately 20 miles to the park entrance on the left.

Overview

Ricketts Glen State Park is a 13,000-acre National Natural Landmark of old-growth forest famous for its Falls Trail System, which passes 22 named waterfalls, including Ganoga Falls at 94 feet. Towering hemlocks and hardwoods shade the glens, and Lake Jean offers swimming and boating in summer. The park has camping year-round, and winter brings snowshoeing and ice climbing.

Access

The park is at 695 State Route 487 in Benton, about 30 miles northwest of Wilkes-Barre and 60 miles from Scranton. Visitors take PA-487 and follow signs. Roads are paved. Parking is free. There is no public transportation.

Seasons

The park is open all year. Spring brings the strongest water. Summer is warm and good for swimming. Fall brings color and the most visitors. Winter brings snow and ice, which makes the Falls Trail dangerous. Camping is open all year.

Things to Do

Hike the Falls Trail

The trail passes 22 waterfalls in two glens.

See Ganoga Falls

The 94-foot waterfall is the highest of the series.

Swim and Paddle at Lake Jean

A beach and boat rentals are open in summer.

Camp in the Park

The campground and cabins are open year-round.

Snowshoe and Climb Ice

Winter activities are popular.

Top Trails

Falls Trail Loop

Length: Approximately 7.2 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Approximately 1,100 feet
Difficulty: Strenuous

The classic route through both glens past 22 waterfalls, with many steps and slippery rocks.

Ganoga Falls Loop

Length: Approximately 3.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 600 feet
Difficulty: Moderate to Strenuous

A shorter route to the tallest waterfall.

Lake Jean Trail

Length: Approximately 3 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Approximately 80 feet
Difficulty: Easy

A loop around the lake with views.

Visitor Centers

The park office near Lake Jean has maps, trail information and fee details. Rangers lead programs in summer. The Falls Trail begins at the Lake Rose and Lake Jean trailheads.

Camping and Lodging

The park has a campground with tent and RV sites, plus cabins and a lodge-type group area, reserved through Pennsylvania State Parks. Hotels and inns are in Benton, Bloomsburg and Wilkes-Barre. Worlds End State Park is also within an hour.
