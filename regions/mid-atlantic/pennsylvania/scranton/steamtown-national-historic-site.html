How to Get There

From downtown Scranton, take Lackawanna Avenue eastbound, turn right onto Cliff Street, and follow signs to the entrance.

Overview

Steamtown National Historic Site is a 62-acre National Park Service site on the former Delaware, Lackawanna and Western Railroad yards, with a working roundhouse, a big turntable and restored steam locomotives and railcars. Highlights include Union Pacific Big Boy #4012, one of the largest steam locomotives ever built, a museum theater, walk-through vintage cars, live steam demonstrations and seasonal train excursions.

Access

The site is at 350 Cliff Street in Scranton, about 125 miles from New York City and 120 miles from Philadelphia. Visitors take I-81 or I-84 to the Scranton exit and follow signs. Parking is free. Admission is charged for the museum and the site.

Seasons

The site is open daily except for some holidays. Spring and fall bring pleasant weather and the best excursions. Summers are warm. Winters are cold and snowy. Train rides run on a seasonal schedule.

Things to Do

Tour the Locomotives

Restored steam engines stand in the yard and the roundhouse.

See Big Boy #4012

One of the largest steam locomotives ever built.

Ride an Excursion Train

Seasonal rides run along the tracks.

Visit the Museum

Exhibits cover railroading and the people who worked on the rails.

Walk Through the Cars

Vintage cars show how passengers traveled.

Top Trails

Steamtown Yard Walk

Length: Approximately 0.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A walk around the yard, the roundhouse and the turntable.

Lackawanna Heritage Valley Trail

Length: Approximately 3 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A paved trail near the site along the Lackawanna River.

Visitor Centers

The Steamtown visitor center has exhibits, a film, a bookstore and ticket sales for excursions. Rangers lead tours and demonstrations. Hours vary by season.

Camping and Lodging

There is no camping or lodging at the site. Camping is available at Lackawanna State Park and Hickory Run State Park. Hotels are in Scranton, Wilkes-Barre and Clarks Summit, with a train-themed hotel at the Radisson Lackawanna Station.
