How to Get There

Head south on S 15th St toward S Penn Square. S 15th St turns slightly left and becomes S Penn Square. Keep left to stay on S Penn Square. Turn right at John F Kennedy Blvd. Continue onto Filbert St. Turn left onto N 11th St. Turn left onto Arch St.

Overview

Reading Terminal Market at 12th and Arch streets is a large indoor public market in Center City, with more than 100 merchants selling fresh produce, meats, fish, artisan cheese, baked goods, flowers, ice cream, and prepared foods, including Pennsylvania Dutch specialties and the classic Philly cheesesteak. Stalls are arranged in a grid around a central seating area, and it makes an easy lunch stop.
