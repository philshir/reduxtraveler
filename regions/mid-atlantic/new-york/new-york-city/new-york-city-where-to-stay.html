Where to Stay in New York City: Manhattan, the Outer Boroughs, and New Jersey

New York City offers accommodations for every budget and travel style, from luxury hotels overlooking Central Park to affordable stays in neighboring boroughs and New Jersey. Choosing where to stay depends on your budget, preferred attractions, and how much time you want to spend traveling to and from Manhattan.

Understanding New York City's Lodging Options

Visitors typically choose one of three accommodation strategies:

  1. Stay in Manhattan for maximum convenience and access to major attractions.
  2. Stay in the Outer Boroughs for lower prices while remaining within New York City.
  3. Stay in New Jersey for the most affordable hotel options with easy transit access into Manhattan.

Budget Accommodations

Budget-conscious travelers can find affordable lodging throughout New York City and nearby New Jersey.

Common budget accommodation types include:

  • Hostels
  • Budget hotels
  • Limited-service chain hotels
  • Guesthouses
  • Budget-friendly boutique hotels

Benefits of budget accommodations:

  • Lower nightly rates
  • Convenient access to public transportation
  • More budget available for attractions, dining, and entertainment
  • Good options for short stays and sightseeing-focused trips

Mid-Range Accommodations

Mid-range hotels offer a balance between affordability and comfort. These properties are ideal for travelers seeking modern amenities without luxury pricing.

Typical mid-range options include:

  • Business-class hotels
  • Boutique hotels
  • Extended-stay properties
  • Neighborhood hotels
  • Major hotel chain properties

Common amenities may include:

  • Fitness centers
  • Business facilities
  • On-site restaurants
  • Complimentary Wi-Fi
  • Concierge services

Luxury Accommodations

New York City's luxury hotels are among the most prestigious in the world. Many are located in Manhattan's premier neighborhoods and offer exceptional service, fine dining, spa facilities, and iconic city views.

Luxury hotel features often include:

  • Premium suites and penthouses
  • Central Park or skyline views
  • Luxury spas
  • Fine dining restaurants
  • Valet services
  • Personalized concierge assistance
  • Exclusive guest experiences

Staying in Manhattan

Manhattan is the most popular choice for first-time visitors because it places travelers near many of New York City's most famous attractions.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Midtown Manhattan
  • Times Square
  • Central Park South
  • Upper West Side
  • Upper East Side
  • Chelsea
  • Greenwich Village
  • Financial District
  • SoHo
  • Tribeca

Budget options in Manhattan:

  • Hostels
  • Budget hotels in Midtown and Upper Manhattan
  • Smaller boutique properties

Mid-range options in Manhattan:

  • Business-class hotels
  • Boutique hotels
  • Major hotel chains near Midtown and Downtown

Luxury options in Manhattan:

  • Central Park luxury hotels
  • Five-star Midtown properties
  • Luxury hotels in Tribeca and SoHo

Advantages of staying in Manhattan:

  • Close to major attractions
  • Extensive subway access
  • Walkable neighborhoods
  • Excellent restaurant and nightlife options

Best for:

  • First-time visitors
  • Short stays
  • Business travelers
  • Luxury travelers

Staying in Brooklyn

Brooklyn has become one of the most popular alternatives to Manhattan. It offers vibrant neighborhoods, excellent food, and easy subway access.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Downtown Brooklyn
  • Williamsburg
  • DUMBO
  • Brooklyn Heights
  • Park Slope

Budget options in Brooklyn:

  • Hostels
  • Budget hotels near subway stations
  • Guesthouses

Mid-range options in Brooklyn:

  • Boutique hotels
  • Neighborhood hotels
  • Modern chain hotels

Luxury options in Brooklyn:

  • Waterfront hotels
  • Upscale boutique properties
  • Luxury accommodations with Manhattan skyline views

Transportation to Manhattan:

  • Subway service typically ranging from 10 to 30 minutes depending on location
  • East River ferry service in select neighborhoods
  • Taxi and rideshare services

Staying in Queens

Queens offers some of the best lodging values within New York City and is particularly convenient for travelers using LaGuardia Airport or John F. Kennedy International Airport.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Long Island City
  • Astoria
  • Flushing
  • Jamaica

Budget options in Queens:

  • Budget chain hotels
  • Airport hotels
  • Economy lodging

Mid-range options in Queens:

  • Modern hotels in Long Island City
  • Boutique properties
  • Business hotels

Luxury options in Queens:

  • Limited upscale boutique hotels
  • Premium Long Island City properties

Transportation to Manhattan:

  • Subway travel often ranging from 10 to 40 minutes
  • Long Island Rail Road service from select locations
  • Taxi and rideshare options

Best for:

  • Budget-conscious visitors
  • Airport access
  • Longer stays

Staying in the Bronx

The Bronx provides a quieter alternative to Manhattan and offers convenient access to attractions such as Yankee Stadium, the New York Botanical Garden, and the Bronx Zoo.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Yankee Stadium area
  • Fordham
  • Riverdale

Transportation to Manhattan:

  • Subway service
  • Metro-North Railroad service
  • Bus routes and taxis

Best for:

  • Budget travelers
  • Sports fans
  • Visitors exploring northern New York City

Staying on Staten Island

Staten Island typically offers lower hotel rates but involves longer travel times to Manhattan.

Transportation to Manhattan:

  • Staten Island Ferry
  • Bus connections
  • Rideshare and taxi services

Best for:

  • Budget-conscious travelers
  • Repeat visitors
  • Travelers with flexible schedules

Staying in New Jersey

New Jersey can offer excellent hotel values while maintaining convenient access to Manhattan. Many visitors choose New Jersey to obtain larger rooms and lower rates than comparable Manhattan properties.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Jersey City
  • Hoboken
  • Weehawken
  • Newark
  • Secaucus

Budget options in New Jersey:

  • Highway hotels
  • Airport hotels
  • Budget chain properties

Mid-range options in New Jersey:

  • Jersey City hotels
  • Hoboken hotels
  • Business-oriented accommodations

Luxury options in New Jersey:

  • Waterfront hotels with Manhattan views
  • Upscale boutique properties
  • Premium business hotels

Transportation to Manhattan:

  • PATH trains from Jersey City and Hoboken
  • NJ Transit commuter rail service
  • Bus service through the Port Authority Bus Terminal
  • NY Waterway ferry services
  • Taxi and rideshare services

Travel times:

  • Jersey City: often 10 to 20 minutes to Lower Manhattan
  • Hoboken: often 10 to 20 minutes to Midtown or Lower Manhattan
  • Newark: often 20 to 40 minutes depending on destination
  • Secaucus: often 15 to 30 minutes via NJ Transit connections

Best for:

  • Families
  • Budget-conscious visitors
  • Travelers seeking larger hotel rooms
  • Visitors staying for multiple days

Transportation Options Throughout the New York Area

Most common transportation methods include:

  • New York City Subway
  • MTA Bus System
  • Long Island Rail Road (LIRR)
  • Metro-North Railroad
  • NJ Transit
  • PATH Train
  • NYC Ferry
  • Taxis
  • Rideshare services
  • Walking

For most visitors, public transportation is faster and more economical than driving. Parking costs can be significant, particularly in Manhattan.

Choosing the Right Location

  1. Stay in Manhattan for maximum convenience, sightseeing, and first-time visits.
  2. Stay in Brooklyn for trendy neighborhoods and easy subway access.
  3. Stay in Queens for excellent value and airport convenience.
  4. Stay in the Bronx for budget-friendly options and northern attractions.
  5. Stay in Staten Island for lower lodging costs and a quieter environment.
  6. Stay in New Jersey for larger rooms, lower hotel rates, and good transit connections.

Bottom Line

Visitors willing to pay premium rates for convenience should stay in Manhattan, particularly near Midtown, Times Square, Central Park, or Downtown neighborhoods. Travelers seeking better value should consider Brooklyn or Queens, while those looking for the most affordable accommodations often find excellent options in New Jersey. Thanks to extensive subway, rail, ferry, and bus networks, visitors can easily reach Manhattan from nearly every major lodging area in the New York metropolitan region.
