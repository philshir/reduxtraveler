How to Get There

From Midtown Manhattan: Head west toward Hudson Yards and walk to West 30th Street, where The Shed sits next to the High Line and Public Square.

Overview

The Shed at Hudson Yards, opened in 2019, is a multi-arts venue defined by a movable outer shell that can extend to create a large performance hall. Inside are galleries, a theater, creative labs, and event spaces for exhibitions, concerts, theater, dance, and multimedia work, with a focus on commissioning new work.
