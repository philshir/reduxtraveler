Rock Creek Park

★5.0

Expansive urban park offering wooded trails, historic sites, equestrian paths, and nature centers, providing a peaceful retreat for recreation and wildlife observation.

How to Get There

From Van Ness–UDC Metro Station, take Connecticut Avenue NW north, then turn onto Tilden Street NW and follow signs to the Nature Center or Peirce Mill entrances.

Overview

Rock Creek Park is a 1,754-acre natural park in the heart of Washington, D.C., established in 1890 and one of the oldest and largest urban parks in the United States, with forests, meadows and streams. More than 32 miles of trails serve hikers, bikers and horseback riders, and it has ranger-led walks, historic landmarks, a nature center, a planetarium and a golf course. The park is open year-round.

Access

The park runs from the Potomac River north to the Maryland line, with main access at the Nature Center at 5200 Glover Road NW. Visitors take the Metro to Woodley Park or Van Ness and a bus, or drive on Rock Creek Parkway. Parking is free. Beach Drive is closed to cars on weekends in sections.

Seasons

The park is open all year. Spring brings wildflowers and fall brings color. Summers are hot and humid, with mosquitoes. Winters are cold, and some trails are muddy or icy. The Nature Center is open Wednesday through Sunday.

Things to Do

Hike the Trails

Forests, ridges and creeks are close to the city.

Visit the Nature Center

Exhibits, a planetarium and ranger programs are open Wednesday to Sunday.

See Peirce Mill

An 1820s grist mill shows historic milling.

Bike Beach Drive

The road is closed to cars on weekends in places.

Ride Horses

Stables offer rides in the park.

Top Trails

Valley Trail

Length: Approximately 10 miles one way
Elevation Gain: Approximately 400 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

A long route along Rock Creek through woods and past historic sites.

Western Ridge Trail

Length: Approximately 5 miles one way
Elevation Gain: Approximately 300 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

A ridge walk through forest from Military Road.

Boulder Bridge Loop

Length: Approximately 3 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 200 feet
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

A loop on the creek with rocks and stone bridges.

Visitor Centers

The Rock Creek Park Nature Center and Planetarium has exhibits, a bookstore, programs and staff who give trail advice. Other facilities include Peirce Mill, Fort DeRussy and the Rock Creek Horse Center.

Camping and Lodging

There is no camping in the park. Hotels are throughout Woodley Park, Dupont Circle, Chevy Chase and Bethesda. Camping is available at Greenbelt Park about 25 minutes away.
