Springfield

★5.0

Vibrant Ozarks community recognized for its cultural attractions, historic Route 66 heritage, outdoor recreation opportunities, and diverse arts and entertainment scene.

General Information
Seasons:
All year, with spring and fall providing the best combination of mild weather, festivals, and outdoor activities
Location:
840 N Boonville Ave, Springfield, MO 65802
Website:
springfieldmo.org

How to Get There

From University Plaza Hotel Springfield (about 1 mi): Head north toward downtown Springfield, follow local streets to Boonville Avenue, and continue to the municipal complex at 840 N Boonville Ave in the city center.

Overview

Springfield, Missouri, is the third-largest city in Missouri and the largest in the Ozarks, in the southwest part of the state. It is known as the "Birthplace of Route 66," because the highway's name was chosen here in 1926, and as the home of Bass Pro Shops, founded by Johnny Morris, and its enormous flagship store and nearby Wonders of Wildlife National Museum & Aquarium. The city is also known for Springfield-style cashew chicken and its Civil War history.

Visitors come to Springfield for Wonders of Wildlife, for the Fantastic Caverns, a ride-through cave that visitors tour by a jeep-drawn tram, and for Route 66 sites such as the Birthplace of Route 66 marker. They also come for Wilson's Creek National Battlefield and the Springfield Botanical Gardens, and it is a convenient and affordable base for trips to Branson, about 45 minutes south.

History

John Polk Campbell settled here in 1829 and the city was incorporated in 1838, and it grew as a hub on the Wire Road and later the railroads. The Battle of Wilson's Creek was fought nearby on August 10, 1861, the first major Civil War battle west of the Mississippi, and Springfield remained a Union stronghold for the rest of the war. In 1865 Wild Bill Hickok shot Davis Tutt in a duel on the public square, which is often called the first "quick draw" shootout. In 1926 representatives of Missouri and Oklahoma met in Springfield to name and design the new highway Route 66, and the city has celebrated its Route 66 heritage ever since.

Sightseeing and Popular Landmarks

The Bass Pro Shops flagship store is among the biggest attractions in Missouri, with waterfalls, an aquarium, and a shooting range, and Wonders of Wildlife next door is one of the largest wildlife museums and aquariums in the country. Fantastic Caverns is a large cave that visitors tour in a tram, and Pythian Castle is a Moorish-style building that has been a lodge, a military hospital, and a POW camp. The Springfield Art Museum, the Missouri Sports Hall of Fame, and the historic Park Central Square downtown are other attractions.

Culture and Museums

Wonders of Wildlife has more than 35,000 live animals and exhibits on conservation, and the Wilson's Creek National Battlefield preserves the site of the Civil War battle with a visitor center and a driving tour. The Springfield Art Museum has a free collection of American art, and the History Museum on the Square tells the story of the city. The Route 66 heritage is celebrated at the Birthplace of Route 66 monument and the Route 66 Car Museum, and the Springfield Little Theatre and Springfield Symphony Orchestra provide entertainment.

Parks

Springfield has a large park system. The Springfield Botanical Gardens at Nathanael Greene/Close Memorial Park has themed gardens, a butterfly house, and a Japanese stroll garden, and the Dickerson Park Zoo is one of the largest in Missouri. The Springfield Conservation Nature Center has trails and exhibits, and the Ozark Greenways trail system connects the city with nearby parks. Table Rock Lake and the Ozark National Scenic Riverways are within a short drive.

Seasons and Weather

Springfield has a humid subtropical climate. Summers are hot and humid, with highs in the upper 80s°F, and winters are cool, with highs in the 40s°F and about 15 inches of snow. Spring and fall are mild, and severe thunderstorms and ice storms are possible. The fall color in the Ozarks is a highlight in October.
