General Information
Seasons:
All year, with spring and fall offering the most comfortable conditions for walking tours, festivals, and outdoor attractions
Location:

Website:
stjomo.com

How to Get There

From Stoney Creek Hotel St. Joseph (about 3 mi): Head south toward downtown via Frederick Avenue, continue east through the central business district, and follow Frederick Avenue to City Hall at 1100 Frederick Ave.

Overview

St. Joseph is a city in northwest Missouri on the Missouri River, and it is famous as the eastern terminus of the Pony Express, the mail service that ran by horse relay between St. Joseph and Sacramento, California, in 1860 and 1861. It is also where outlaw Jesse James was killed in 1882, and the home where he died is now a museum. St. Joseph was once a gateway to the West, with thousands of emigrants crossing the Missouri here on their way to California and Oregon.

Visitors come to St. Joseph for the Pony Express National Museum, the Patee House Museum, and the Jesse James Home Museum. They also come for the unique Glore Psychiatric Museum, the Albrecht-Kemper Museum of Art, and the city's historic neighborhoods, and it is a convenient stop between Kansas City, about an hour south, and Omaha.

History

The trader Joseph Robidoux established a fur trading post here in the 1820s, and the town was platted in 1843 and named for him. When the California Gold Rush began in 1849, St. Joseph became one of the main jumping-off points for travelers heading west, and the city was a boomtown with banks, outfitters, and hotels. In 1860, the Pony Express began here from the Patee House, where the company had its headquarters, and carried mail the 1,900 miles to Sacramento in about ten days before the telegraph ended the service in 1861. The city was a major railroad and meatpacking center, and in 1882 Jesse James was shot by Robert Ford in his rented home on the hill.

Sightseeing and Popular Landmarks

The Pony Express National Museum is in the original stables from which the first rider left in 1860, and the Patee House, the former hotel and Pony Express headquarters, has a museum with railroad cars and Old West exhibits. The Jesse James Home shows the bullet hole in the wall, and Robidoux Row is a row of historic townhouses from 1840s. The Mount Mora Cemetery and the Wyeth-Tootle Mansion are among historic sites, and Krug Park is a popular local park.

Culture and Museums

The Pony Express National Museum has interactive exhibits on the history of the riders, and the Patee House Museum includes a collection of historic buildings, and a carousel. The Glore Psychiatric Museum has exhibits on the history of mental health treatment, and the Albrecht-Kemper Museum of Art has a collection of American art. The St. Joseph Museum, in a former mansion, covers local natural and cultural history, and the Missouri Theater and the Missouri Western State University provide events.

Parks

St. Joseph has a large park system. Krug Park has trails and picnic areas, and the Remington Nature Center has natural history exhibits and trails. The Missouri River offers views and fishing, and the Wyeth Hills area has trails for hiking and biking.

Seasons and Weather

St. Joseph has a humid continental to humid subtropical climate. Summers are hot and humid, with highs in the upper 80s°F, and winters are cold, with highs in the 30s°F and about 20 inches of snow. Spring and fall are mild, and severe thunderstorms are possible from spring through summer.

Albrecht-Kemper Museum of Art

Regional fine arts museum featuring American art from the 18th century to the present, housed in a historic mansion with rotating exhibitions and educational programs.

Glore Psychiatric Museum
★ 5.0

Extensive museum chronicling the history of mental health treatment through artifacts, dioramas, and exhibits housed in a former psychiatric hospital.

Jesse James Home and Museum
★ 5.0

Historic house museum preserving the site of an infamous outlaw’s death, featuring personal artifacts, period furnishings, and exhibits on 19th-century frontier life.

Patee House Museum
★ 5.0

National Historic Landmark museum featuring exhibits on the Pony Express, 19th-century transportation, and frontier life within a restored 1858 hotel.

Pony Express National Museum
★ 5.0

Interactive museum commemorating the legendary mail service with immersive exhibits, historic artifacts, and educational displays housed in original 1860 stables.

Robidoux Row Museum and St. Joseph Historical Society
★ 5.0

Historic frontier-era house museum preserving the oldest surviving building in the city and interpreting the founding, settlement, and early development of the American West.
