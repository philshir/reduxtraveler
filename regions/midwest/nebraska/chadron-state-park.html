How to Get There

From Chadron (9 mi): Take US-385 south from Chadron, continue through the Nebraska National Forest area, and follow signs to the park entrance at 15951 Highway 385.

Overview

Chadron State Park is a public recreation area located within the Nebraska National Forest, nine miles (14 km) south of Chadron, Nebraska, in the northwestern portion of the state. The park's 974 acres (394 ha) include a portion of the Pine Ridge escarpment and Chadron Creek. The park is wooded with ponderosa pine throughout and cottonwood trees near the creek and lagoon.

Activities and amenities

Activities include swimming, horseback riding, archery, disc golf, hiking, tennis, sand volleyball, and fishing. The park has rental paddleboats, hiking trails, campground, and cabins.

Things to Do

Discover Nebraska's First State Park

Chadron State Park, established in 1921 as Nebraska's first state park, is located in the scenic Pine Ridge region of northwestern Nebraska near the city of Chadron. The park encompasses more than 970 acres of pine-covered ridges, meadows, canyons, and rolling hills, offering visitors a unique blend of Great Plains and Rocky Mountain landscapes. It is one of Nebraska's premier destinations for hiking, horseback riding, wildlife viewing, camping, and outdoor recreation.

Explore the Pine Ridge Landscape

The park is situated within the Pine Ridge escarpment, a region characterized by ponderosa pine forests, rugged buttes, and dramatic overlooks. Visitors can enjoy sweeping views of northwestern Nebraska's distinctive terrain while exploring one of the state's most scenic environments.

Experience Horseback Riding

Chadron State Park is well known for its horseback riding opportunities. Guided trail rides and extensive equestrian trails allow visitors to explore forests, grasslands, and scenic ridges while experiencing the natural beauty of the Pine Ridge region.

Enjoy Outdoor Recreation

The park offers hiking, mountain biking, fishing, archery, camping, wildlife viewing, and seasonal educational programs. Its family-friendly facilities and beautiful surroundings make it a popular destination for both day trips and extended stays.

Observe Wildlife

Chadron State Park supports a variety of wildlife, including mule deer, white-tailed deer, wild turkeys, elk, foxes, songbirds, hawks, and other species native to the Pine Ridge ecosystem. Wildlife viewing and photography opportunities are available throughout the year.

Top Trails

Steamboat Trail

Length: Approximately 3 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Moderate

This scenic trail traverses pine forests and ridgelines while providing beautiful views of the surrounding Pine Ridge landscape. It is one of the park's most popular hiking routes.

Pinecone Trail

Length: Approximately 1 mile loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal to Moderate
Difficulty: Easy

This family-friendly trail offers a pleasant walk through ponderosa pine forests and is ideal for wildlife viewing, nature study, and short hikes.

Blackhills Overlook Trail

Length: Approximately 2 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Moderate

The trail climbs to scenic overlook points with expansive views of the Pine Ridge and surrounding High Plains. It is especially popular for photography and sunset viewing.
