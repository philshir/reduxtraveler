How to Get There

From Cedar Rapids, IA (45 mi): Take I-380 N to IA-150 N, then follow signs to Cedar Rock State Park near Independence.

Overview

Cedar Rock State Park is home to one of the best-preserved examples of Frank Lloyd Wright’s Usonian architecture. The park’s centerpiece is the Walter House, designed by Wright in 1948 and perched above the Wapsipinicon River. Visitors can tour the home, which remains fully furnished with original Wright-designed pieces and offers insight into his philosophy of organic architecture.

Surrounding the house are wooded trails and picnic areas that make Cedar Rock a peaceful retreat for nature lovers and architecture enthusiasts alike. The park is open seasonally and offers guided tours that delve into Wright’s design principles and the history of the site. It’s a hidden gem for those seeking inspiration in both nature and design.

Things to Do

Visit a Frank Lloyd Wright Architectural Masterpiece

Cedar Rock State Park preserves the Walter House, one of the finest examples of Usonian architecture designed by renowned American architect Frank Lloyd Wright. Located near Quasqueton, Iowa, along the Wapsipinicon River, the park blends architectural significance with natural beauty, making it a unique destination for both history enthusiasts and outdoor recreation visitors.

Tour the Walter House (Cedar Rock)

Completed in 1950 for Lowell and Agnes Walter, Cedar Rock is considered one of the most complete Frank Lloyd Wright-designed properties in existence. Visitors can take guided tours of the home and grounds to experience Wright's vision of integrating architecture with the surrounding landscape. Much of the original furniture and design elements remain intact.

Explore the Historic Estate Grounds

The Cedar Rock property includes beautifully maintained grounds, gardens, terraces, a riverfront setting, and several structures designed by Wright. The site offers insight into Wright's philosophy of organic architecture, where buildings harmonize with their natural surroundings.

Enjoy Scenic Views of the Wapsipinicon River

The park overlooks the Wapsipinicon River and provides peaceful views of wooded riverbanks, rolling hills, and native vegetation. The setting offers excellent opportunities for photography, picnicking, and enjoying Iowa's natural beauty.

Learn About Architecture and Design

Interpretive programs and guided tours focus on Frank Lloyd Wright's design principles, the history of the Walter family, and the significance of Cedar Rock within Wright's body of work. Architectural enthusiasts often consider the site one of Iowa's most important cultural landmarks.

Top Trails

Cedar Rock Nature Trail

Length: Approximately 1 mile loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

This scenic trail winds through woodlands and riverfront areas surrounding the Cedar Rock estate. Visitors can enjoy views of the Wapsipinicon River, native plants, and wildlife while exploring the park's natural setting.

Wapsipinicon River Walking Paths

Length: Variable distances available
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

Short walking routes throughout the park provide access to overlooks, picnic areas, and riverside scenery. These paths offer a relaxing complement to a tour of the historic Walter House.

Historic Estate Grounds Walk

Length: Approximately 0.5 miles
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

Visitors can stroll around the Cedar Rock property to appreciate the architectural design, carefully planned landscaping, and outdoor features that contribute to the estate's National Historic Landmark significance.
