Adams National Historical Park

★5.0

National Historical Park preserves the home of United States presidents John Adams and John Quincy Adams

Closed Mondays and Tuesdays
Park grounds open daily from sunrise to sunset
Fees:
Adults (16+): $15
Children 15 and under: Free
Pet Policy:

nps.gov/adam

How to Get There

From Boston (8 mi): Take I-93 S toward Quincy, then take exit 12 for MA-3A S/Hancock St, and continue south on Hancock Street until you reach the Adams National Historical Park Visitor Center.

Overview

Adams National Historical Park in Quincy preserves the homes of presidents John Adams and John Quincy Adams and of several generations of the Adams family, including diplomat Charles Francis Adams and historians Henry and Brooks Adams. The National Park Service acquired the estate in 1947. The park includes the John Adams and John Quincy Adams birthplaces, Peacefield (the Old House), the Stone Library and the United First Parish Church, where both presidents and their wives are buried.

Access

The visitor center is at 1250 Hancock Street in Quincy, across from the Quincy Center MBTA station on the Red Line, about 8 miles south of Boston. The historic houses are about a mile away and visited by guided tour only, with a trolley from the visitor center. Visitors drive on I-93 and the Southeast Expressway, and parking is in nearby garages. A fee is charged for tours.

Seasons

The houses are open for guided tours from mid-April to early November, and the visitor center is open year-round. Spring and fall bring pleasant weather and fall color. Summer is warm and busier. Winter closes the houses.

Things to Do

Tour Peacefield

Rangers lead a tour of the house where four generations of the family lived.

See the Birthplaces

The two homes where John Adams and John Quincy Adams were born are open.

Visit the Stone Library

The 1870 library holds more than 14,000 books and papers.

Visit the United First Parish Church

The Church of the Presidents holds the Adams Crypt.

Walk Quincy Center

Shops and restaurants are around the visitor center.

Top Trails

Presidents Birthplace Walk

Length: Approximately 0.8 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A walk between the two birthplace houses.

Peacefield Grounds Walk

Length: Approximately 0.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A walk through the garden and orchard at the Old House.

Visitor Centers

The Adams National Historical Park Visitor Center has exhibits, a film, a bookstore and ranger information, and sells tickets for the trolley and tours. The center is open daily in tour season.

Camping and Lodging

There is no camping or lodging in the park. Camping is available at Wompatuck State Park in Hingham, about 20 minutes away. Hotels and inns are in Quincy, Braintree and Boston.
