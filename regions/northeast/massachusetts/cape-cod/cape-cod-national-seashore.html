How to Get There

From Provincetown, MA: Follow Route 6 south or north depending on access area, enter Cape Cod National Seashore through designated park entrances, and follow posted signs to beaches, trails, lighthouses, or visitor centers.

Overview

Cape Cod National Seashore was created by President John F. Kennedy in 1961 and protects 43,607 acres and nearly 40 miles of Atlantic-facing shoreline on the Outer Cape, from Chatham to Provincetown. Ponds, woods, dunes and beaches make it the Cape's top outdoor destination. Highlights include Nauset Light, Coast Guard Beach, Marconi Station, the Great Island and the Province Lands Dunes. The National Park Service manages it.

Access

The seashore runs along the Outer Cape, with the Salt Pond Visitor Center on US-6 in Eastham. Visitors take US-6 from the Sagamore Bridge, about 1.5 hours from Boston. Parking fees apply at beaches in summer. The Cape Cod Regional Transit shuttles run in season, and the Cape Cod Rail Trail passes through.

Seasons

The seashore is open all year. Summer is busy at beaches, with lifeguards from late June to Labor Day. Fall is quieter, with warm water and fewer crowds. Winter is cold and windy with wild surf. Spring brings wildflowers and birds. Ticks and greenhead flies are active in summer.

Things to Do

Swim at the Beaches

Coast Guard Beach, Nauset Light Beach and Race Point have lifeguards.

Bike the Trails

Paved trails run through the dunes and marshes.

Climb the Dunes

Province Lands has shifting dunes.

Visit Nauset Light

The red-and-white lighthouse is a landmark.

See Whales

Boat tours leave from Provincetown to see humpbacks.

Top Trails

Nauset Marsh Trail

Length: Approximately 1.5 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A walk through the marsh with views of the salt pond.

Great Island Trail

Length: Approximately 8 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Moderate

A long trail across dunes and a peninsula, reached across tidal flats.

Province Lands Bike Trail

Length: Approximately 5 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Approximately 100 feet
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

A paved path through the dunes and ponds near Provincetown.

Cedar Swamp Trail

Length: Approximately 1 mile loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A boardwalk through a swamp of cedar and holly.

Visitor Centers

The Salt Pond Visitor Center in Eastham and the Province Lands Visitor Center in Provincetown have exhibits, films, a bookstore and rangers. Rangers lead beach walks and talks in season. The Salt Pond center is open year-round.

Camping and Lodging

There is no camping in the seashore, but camping is available at Nickerson State Park in Brewster and private campgrounds near Eastham and Truro. Hotels, inns, cottages and motels are in Eastham, Wellfleet, Truro and Provincetown, and reservations fill early in summer.
