Lubec, ME and Quoddy Head State Park

★5.0

Coastal park featuring rocky cliffs, forested trails, peat bog habitats, sweeping ocean vistas, and a distinctive striped lighthouse.

Overview

Quoddy Head State Park in Lubec is a 541-acre park four miles off Route 189 at West Quoddy Head, the easternmost point of land in the contiguous United States. Sea cliffs, a rocky shore and a candy-striped lighthouse, built in 1808 and the easternmost lighthouse in the country, make it a postcard destination. Around the equinoxes this is among the first places in the United States to see the sunrise. Lubec itself is the easternmost town in the contiguous U.S., connected to Campobello Island, New Brunswick, by the Franklin D. Roosevelt Bridge.

Access

The park is at 973 South Lubec Road in Lubec, about 4 miles from town and 3.5 hours from Portland. Visitors take US-1 to ME-189 and follow signs. Roads are paved. A day-use fee is charged. There is no public transportation. A border crossing to Canada is nearby.

Seasons

The park is open from mid-May to mid-October, with the lighthouse museum open in summer. Summer is cool and foggy, with the best chance of whale sightings. Fall brings color and crisp days. Winter closes the road. Mosquitoes and blackflies are active in late spring.

Things to Do

Visit the West Quoddy Head Light

The red-and-white striped lighthouse is the easternmost in the country.

Walk the Cliff Trail

A path follows the sea cliffs with views of the Bay of Fundy.

Watch for Whales

Humpbacks, minke whales and seals pass offshore in summer.

See the Sunrise

The first sunrise in the United States is visible around the equinoxes.

Cross to Campobello

Roosevelt Campobello International Park is across the bridge.

Top Trails

Coastal Trail

Length: Approximately 4.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 300 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

A path along the cliffs with views of the sea, the lighthouse and the bogs.

Bog Trail

Length: Approximately 1 mile loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A boardwalk through a coastal bog with pitcher plants.

Lighthouse Path

Length: Approximately 0.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A short walk from the parking area to the lighthouse and the shore.

Visitor Centers

The Visitor Center in the former keeper's house has exhibits on the light and the area, a gift shop and information. It is open in summer. The park office at the entrance has maps and fees.

Camping and Lodging

The park has no campground, but camping is available at Cobscook Bay State Park, about 30 minutes west. Inns, motels and bed-and-breakfasts are in Lubec, Eastport and Machias, with cottages on Campobello Island.
