How to Get There

From Lincoln, NH: Take I‑93 North for approximately 5 miles and exit directly into the main park access areas along the Franconia Notch Parkway.

Overview

Franconia Notch State Park spans eight miles of I-93 through Franconia Notch, a 1,950-foot mountain pass between the Kinsman Range and the Franconia Range in the White Mountains. Cannon Mountain and Mount Lafayette overlook the notch. It is one of the best scenic stops in the state, with a gorge, a granite pothole, lakes and a tramway. Top sights include Flume Gorge, the Basin, the Cannon Mountain Aerial Tram, the Old Man of the Mountain memorial and Echo Lake.

Access

The park is on I-93 near Lincoln and Franconia, about 2.5 hours north of Boston. Visitors take I-93 to exits 33 to 34A and follow signs. Roads are paved. Parking is free at most sites, and a fee is charged at Flume Gorge. There is no public transportation.

Seasons

The park is open all year, and Flume Gorge is open from mid-May to late October. Summer is warm and busy. Fall is the most popular season for the foliage. Winters are cold and snowy, with skiing at Cannon Mountain. Spring has strong streams.

Things to Do

Walk Flume Gorge

A boardwalk passes through a narrow granite gorge with waterfalls.

See the Basin

A 20-foot glacial pothole sits beneath a waterfall.

Ride the Aerial Tram

The Cannon Mountain Aerial Tram was the first in North America (1938).

Visit the Old Man Memorial

Profile Plaza shows the outline of the lost rock face.

Hike Franconia Ridge

A big alpine route has views across the White Mountains.

Top Trails

Flume Gorge Trail

Length: Approximately 2 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Approximately 300 feet
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

A loop on boardwalks and paths past the gorge, covered bridges and falls.

Lonesome Lake Trail

Length: Approximately 3 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 1,000 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

A climb from Lafayette Place to an alpine lake and an AMC hut.

Franconia Ridge Loop

Length: Approximately 8.9 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Approximately 3,900 feet
Difficulty: Strenuous

An alpine ridge hike over Little Haystack, Lincoln and Lafayette, with exposed sections.

Artist's Bluff Trail

Length: Approximately 1.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 200 feet
Difficulty: Easy

A short path to a rocky ledge with a view of Echo Lake.

Visitor Centers

The Flume Gorge Visitor Center has exhibits, a film, a gift shop, food and staff who sell tickets. The Old Man of the Mountain Profile Plaza and the Lafayette Place Campground have information. Rangers lead programs in summer.

Camping and Lodging

The park has the Lafayette Place Campground, with tent and RV sites, reserved through New Hampshire State Parks. Hotels, inns and cabins are in Lincoln, Franconia and Woodstock, and the AMC huts are along the trails. Littleton is about 25 minutes north.
