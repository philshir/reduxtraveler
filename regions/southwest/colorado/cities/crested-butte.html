How to Get There

From Gunnison (28 mi): Take CO-135 N from downtown Gunnison and follow it north for about 28 miles directly into the town and resort base area.

Overview

Crested Butte is a former coal mining town on Coal Creek in the Slate River valley of the Elk Mountains, at nearly 9,000 feet. Now called "the last great Colorado ski town" and "the wildflower capital of Colorado," it combines colorful Victorian buildings along Elk Avenue with serious skiing, mountain biking, and hiking, and a laid-back, independent spirit.

Visitors come to ski the steep terrain at Crested Butte Mountain Resort, to see the summer wildflower displays that peak in mid-July, and to ride the trails that helped make the town one of the birthplaces of mountain biking. With a short distance from the airport at Gunnison and few crowds, it is a quieter alternative to better-known resort towns.

Crested Butte was founded in 1880 as a supply point for coal and silver mines, and coal became its mainstay when the Colorado Fuel and Iron Company opened mines nearby. The last big coal mine closed in 1952, and the town struggled until the ski area opened in 1961, and a new generation of skiers, artists, and cyclists revived it. The town is a National Historic District, and its mining-era buildings give Elk Avenue its character.

Elk Avenue, with its false-front buildings, galleries, restaurants, and historic saloons, is the center of town. Just north, the old mining townsite of Gothic and the Rocky Mountain Biological Laboratory sit beneath the peaks, and the drive over Kebler Pass passes through one of the largest aspen forests in North America. Crystal Mill, a photogenic old mill above the Crystal River, is also reachable from Crested Butte by high-clearance vehicle.

The Crested Butte Mountain Heritage Museum, in a former hardware store, tells the story of the town's coal mining and skiing past, and the Center for the Arts hosts theater, concerts, and gallery exhibitions. Local festivals such as the Crested Butte Wildflower Festival, Vinotok in autumn, and summer music series make cultural life active for a town this size.

Crested Butte is surrounded by public land, including the Gunnison National Forest and the Maroon Bells–Snowmass Wilderness. Popular outings include the 401 Trail and Trail 403 for mountain biking, Judd Falls and the trails around Gothic, and hikes along the Slate River. In town, Town Park and the Crested Butte Nordic Center provide recreation year-round.

Crested Butte has cold, snowy winters and cool, sunny summers. Winter temperatures are among the coldest in Colorado, and the ski mountain gets about 300 inches of snow a year. Summer highs are generally in the 70s °F with cool nights, wildflowers peak in July, and fall color follows in September. Spring, often called mud season, is a quiet time between ski and bike seasons.

Things to Do

Discover the Wildflower Capital of Colorado

Crested Butte is famous for its vibrant summer wildflower displays and scenic mountain landscapes. Surrounded by the Elk Mountains and Gunnison National Forest, the town offers breathtaking alpine scenery, colorful meadows, and abundant outdoor recreation opportunities throughout the year.

Explore Historic Downtown Crested Butte

The town's historic district features colorful Victorian-era buildings, locally owned shops, art galleries, restaurants, and cafés. Visitors can stroll along Elk Avenue to experience Crested Butte's unique mountain-town character and rich mining heritage.

Experience Crested Butte Mountain Resort

Crested Butte Mountain Resort is a premier destination for skiing and snowboarding during winter and transforms into a hub for hiking, mountain biking, scenic chairlift rides, and alpine activities during the summer. The resort offers spectacular views of the surrounding peaks and valleys.

Go Mountain Biking on World-Class Trails

Often referred to as one of the birthplaces of modern mountain biking, Crested Butte features hundreds of miles of trails ranging from beginner-friendly routes to challenging alpine singletrack. Riders come from around the world to experience the area's renowned biking terrain.

Enjoy Scenic Drives and Alpine Adventures

Visitors can explore nearby mountain passes, including Kebler Pass, which is home to one of the largest aspen groves in North America. During autumn, the area becomes a popular destination for vibrant fall foliage and photography.

Fish, Paddle, and Explore the Outdoors

The rivers, streams, and alpine lakes surrounding Crested Butte provide excellent fishing, kayaking, paddleboarding, camping, wildlife viewing, and photography opportunities. Outdoor enthusiasts can enjoy year-round access to some of Colorado's most spectacular backcountry landscapes.

Top Trails

Emerald Lake Trail

Length: Approximately 3.8 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 700 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

One of the area's most popular hikes, this scenic trail leads to a beautiful alpine lake surrounded by rugged mountain peaks and seasonal wildflowers. The route provides excellent opportunities for photography and wildlife viewing.

Snodgrass Mountain Trail

Length: Approximately 5 to 6 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 1,000 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

This trail offers expansive views of Mount Crested Butte, Gothic Valley, and the surrounding Elk Mountains. It is popular with both hikers and mountain bikers and is especially scenic during wildflower season.

Lower Loop Trail

Length: Approximately 4 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A favorite trail close to town, the Lower Loop follows sections of the Slate River and passes through open meadows filled with wildflowers. The trail is ideal for families, casual hikers, and cyclists.

Judd Falls Trail

Length: Approximately 2 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 450 feet
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Located near the historic townsite of Gothic, this short trail leads to a beautiful waterfall and offers views of alpine forests, mountain peaks, and seasonal wildflowers. It is one of the most accessible hikes in the area.
