Overview

Nevada is home to one of the world's most recognizable UFO tourism regions, where vast desert landscapes, military legends, and extraterrestrial folklore combine to create an experience unlike any other. At the center of this fascination is Area 51, the secretive military installation long associated with reports of unidentified flying objects and experimental aircraft. Although the base itself is inaccessible to the public, its mystique has inspired a network of attractions that draw curious travelers, amateur astronomers, photographers, and science-fiction enthusiasts from around the globe.

The journey typically begins along the Extraterrestrial Highway, a remote stretch of road famous for its UFO connections and dramatic scenery. Along the route, visitors encounter iconic stops such as the Little A'Le'Inn, an alien-themed motel and restaurant that has become a gathering place for UFO enthusiasts, and the Alien Research Center, a roadside attraction known for its massive extraterrestrial sculpture and collection of themed souvenirs. These destinations embrace the region's mythology while providing memorable opportunities to explore the stories and speculation surrounding Nevada's most famous mystery.

Adding to the adventure are legendary landmarks like the Black Mailbox and countless viewing areas where travelers scan the skies beneath some of North America's darkest night skies. Together, these attractions create a distinctive tourism experience that blends history, folklore, desert exploration, and popular culture. Whether visitors are serious UFO researchers or simply seeking a unique road trip, Nevada's extraterrestrial attractions offer an intriguing glimpse into the enduring fascination with the unknown.

Things to Do

Drive the Extraterrestrial Highway

One of Nevada's most famous road trips follows the Extraterrestrial Highway, a remote desert route closely associated with UFO sightings and Area 51 folklore. The highway passes through expansive basins, mountain ranges, and open landscapes that create a sense of adventure and mystery while providing excellent opportunities for sightseeing and photography.

Explore the Town of Rachel

Rachel is the small desert community most closely associated with Area 51 tourism. The town serves as a gateway for visitors exploring Nevada's extraterrestrial attractions and offers a unique glimpse into the culture, stories, and legends that surround the region's UFO phenomenon.

Visit the Little A'Le'Inn

The Little A'Le'Inn is one of the region's best-known UFO attractions. Visitors can enjoy themed dining, browse alien-inspired souvenirs, view displays dedicated to extraterrestrial legends, and connect with fellow travelers interested in Area 51 and unexplained phenomena.

Stop at the Alien Research Center

Recognizable by its giant alien sculpture, the Alien Research Center is a popular roadside attraction celebrating Nevada's extraterrestrial culture. Guests can shop for unique memorabilia, take photographs with the iconic alien figure, and explore exhibits inspired by UFO reports and science-fiction themes.

Visit ET Fresh Jerky

ET Fresh Jerky combines a traditional roadside market with quirky alien-themed attractions. Travelers can sample locally produced snacks, browse UFO merchandise, and pose for photographs with colorful extraterrestrial displays that celebrate the area's connection to Area 51 lore.

See the Legendary Black Mailbox

Long associated with UFO enthusiasts and skywatchers, the Black Mailbox has become a symbol of the area's extraterrestrial folklore. Travelers often stop to photograph the landmark and learn about the stories, rumors, and sightings that helped make it famous among Area 51 visitors.

Hike Tikaboo Peak

Tikaboo Peak is one of the most popular destinations for adventurous visitors seeking distant views toward the Area 51 region. The challenging hike rewards experienced hikers with panoramic desert scenery and one of the most famous viewpoints associated with Nevada's UFO and aviation mysteries.

Experience Area 51 from Public Viewing Areas

While access to the military installation is prohibited, visitors can explore surrounding public lands and designated viewpoints near the restricted boundary. These locations offer a chance to appreciate the vast desert environment and learn about the aviation history and mysteries that continue to surround the facility.

Discover the Tonopah Historic Mining Park

The Tonopah Historic Mining Park provides a fascinating look at Nevada's mining heritage while serving as a popular stop for travelers exploring the state's central desert. Historic structures, mining equipment, and interpretive exhibits offer insights into the boomtown era that helped shape the region's history.

Popular Attractions

Area 51

Type: Military installation and UFO folklore destination
Access: Public viewpoints only
Experience: Scenic exploration and mystery tourism

Known worldwide for its secrecy and association with experimental aircraft testing, Area 51 remains the centerpiece of Nevada's UFO tourism industry and continues to attract visitors fascinated by the unknown.

Extraterrestrial Highway

Type: Scenic highway
Length: Approximately 98 miles
Experience: Road trip, sightseeing, and photography

This legendary route connects many of Nevada's most famous UFO attractions while showcasing spectacular desert scenery and dark skies.

Rachel

Type: Desert community
Experience: UFO tourism and local culture

Often considered the unofficial hometown of Area 51 tourism, Rachel welcomes visitors seeking to experience the unique atmosphere surrounding Nevada's extraterrestrial legends.

Little A'Le'Inn

Type: Restaurant, motel, and gift shop
Experience: Dining, lodging, and alien-themed exhibits

A favorite stop for visitors interested in UFO lore, the Little A'Le'Inn serves as both a tourist attraction and gathering place for enthusiasts from around the world.

Alien Research Center

Type: Roadside attraction and gift shop
Experience: Souvenirs, exhibits, and photo opportunities

The center's giant alien statue has become one of the most photographed landmarks along Nevada's extraterrestrial tourism corridor.

ET Fresh Jerky

Type: Specialty market and roadside attraction
Experience: Local snacks, souvenirs, and photography

This popular stop combines Nevada-made products with playful extraterrestrial themes, making it a favorite destination for road-trippers.

Tikaboo Peak

Type: Hiking destination and viewpoint
Experience: Backcountry hiking and desert panoramas

The peak is renowned among outdoor enthusiasts for its sweeping views of the surrounding desert and its association with Area 51 sightseeing.

Black Mailbox

Type: Historic UFO landmark
Experience: Photography and sightseeing

Though simple in appearance, this mailbox achieved international recognition through its connection to UFO culture and Area 51 mythology.

Tonopah Historic Mining Park

Type: Historic mining attraction
Experience: History, exhibits, and self-guided exploration

This preserved mining complex showcases Nevada's rich silver-mining heritage and provides an interesting historical complement to the region's extraterrestrial attractions.
