ARIA Resort & Casino

★ 5.0

Luxury resort and casino, consisting of two curved glass and steel highrise towers adjoined at the center

Luxurious, upmarket resort with an Italian theme. Overview

The Las Vegas Strip is a four-mile stretch of South Las Vegas Boulevard known for its huge resort hotels and casinos, most of which are outside the city limits in unincorporated Paradise. The first resort opened here in 1941, and it grew into the world's best-known entertainment strip, with themed hotels, shows, fountains, and shopping.

Resorts from South to North

  • Mandalay Bay, Luxor, Excalibur, New York-New York, and MGM Grand
  • ARIA, Cosmopolitan, Bellagio, and Planet Hollywood
  • Caesars Palace, Flamingo, The LINQ and High Roller
  • Venetian, Palazzo, Treasure Island, Wynn, and Encore
  • Circus Circus, Resorts World, The Strat, and Sahara

Good to Know

  • Walking: distances are longer than they look, so wear comfortable shoes, and pedestrian bridges cross the busiest intersections
  • Getting around: the Las Vegas Monorail, trams between resorts, and rideshares
