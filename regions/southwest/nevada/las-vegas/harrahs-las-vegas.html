A lively casino resort offering gaming, entertainment, dining, and accommodations in a vibrant atmosphere with a central Strip location.

General Information

Average Room Rates with Resort Fee:
Roughly $140–$210 per night on weeknights, including the resort fee of about $56.63 per night (with tax); rates vary widely by season, room type and events.
Average Room Rates Weekends and Holidays with Resort Fee:
Roughly $220–$330 per night on Friday–Saturday nights, holidays and major events, including the resort fee of about $56.63 per night (with tax).
Parking Rates:
Self-parking is $20 per day Monday–Thursday and $25 per day Friday–Sunday (hotel guests $20 per day), and valet is $40 per day; free self-parking for Caesars Rewards Platinum tier and above, and Nevada license holders get 3 free hours (not on event nights).
Pet Policy:
Dogs only (no cats), up to two per room of 50 pounds or less each, in designated Pet Stay rooms arranged in advance; reported fee of $75–$100 per night plus a refundable deposit. Dogs must be crated when left alone and are not allowed in the casino, restaurants or pools. Service animals are welcome.
Smoking Policy:
Smoking is allowed on the casino floor, as Nevada law exempts casinos from its indoor smoking ban. Guest rooms are predominantly non-smoking; confirm when booking.
Budget:
Average
Hours:
Casino and hotel open 24 hours daily; entertainment and dining venues vary by schedule
Seasons:
All year

Overview

Harrah's Las Vegas opened in 1973 as the riverboat-themed Holiday Casino and was rebranded as Harrah's in 1992. It is a bustling, centrally located resort with a full-service casino, multiple dining options, live entertainment, and easy access to nearby attractions.

Hotel Design and Rooms

The resort offers a mix of classic Vegas charm and modern amenities, with about 2,500 rooms in its hotel towers. Rooms are mid-range and comfortable, and many have been renovated with a more modern look.

Special Attractions

One of its distinctive features is its PetStay program, which welcomes pets, and its central location next to the LINQ Promenade.

Dining Options

Harrah's offers diverse restaurants, from casual cafes to sit-down dining, at moderate prices, including a Ruth's Chris Steak House. Check current listings.

Las Vegas Shows

The resort is known for comedy shows and music performances, and entertainment varies, so check the schedule.

Pool Options

The pool area is a seasonal pool complex.

Nightlife Options

Carnaval Court, an open-air bar on the Strip side of the resort, is known for its flair bartenders and live bands. Nightlife also includes the casino's bars and lounges, with major clubs a short walk away at the LINQ and Caesars Palace.

Shopping Options

The adjacent LINQ Promenade offers shopping and dining, and Harrah's has a few of its own shops.

Special Amenities

The resort has a fitness center and a spa, with moderate prices.

Nearby Attractions

Harrah's is next to the LINQ Promenade and High Roller, and near Caesars Palace, the Venetian, Treasure Island.
