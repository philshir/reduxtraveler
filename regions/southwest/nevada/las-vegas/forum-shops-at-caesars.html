Overview

The Forum Shops at Caesars Palace is one of Las Vegas’s premier retail destinations, offering over 160 specialty stores and luxury brands such as Gucci, Louis Vuitton, and Versace, all set within a lavish Roman-inspired environment complete with marble columns, fountains, and painted sky ceilings.

Beyond shopping, visitors can enjoy fine dining, casual eateries, and unique attractions like the Fall of Atlantis animatronic show, making the Forum Shops a must-see blend of retail therapy and themed entertainment in the heart of the Strip.
