ARIA Resort & Casino

★5.0

ARIA Resort & Casino, Las Vegas, Nevada
Steven Lek, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons

Luxury resort and casino, consisting of two curved glass and steel highrise towers adjoined at the center

General Information
Average Room Rates with Resort Fee:
Roughly $190–$280 per night on weeknights, including the resort fee of about $62.36 per night (with tax); rates vary widely by season, room type and events.
Average Room Rates Weekends and Holidays with Resort Fee:
Roughly $300–$470 per night on Friday–Saturday nights, holidays and major events, including the resort fee of about $62.36 per night (with tax).
Parking Rates:
Self-parking is $20 per day and valet is $40 per day; free self-parking for MGM Rewards Pearl tier and above, and Nevada residents get 3 free hours with ID.
Pet Policy:
Dogs only, up to two per room (about 100 pounds combined), in designated pet-friendly rooms; reported fee of about $150 per night. Pets are not allowed on the casino floor, in restaurants or at the pools. Service animals are welcome.
Smoking Policy:
Smoking is allowed on the casino floor, as Nevada law exempts casinos from its indoor smoking ban. Guest rooms are predominantly non-smoking; confirm when booking.
Budget:
Expensive
Location:
3730 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89158
Website:
aria.mgmresorts.com

Overview

ARIA Resort & Casino is the centerpiece of the CityCenter complex on the Strip and one of the most modern luxury resorts in Las Vegas. It opened on December 16, 2009, and draws visitors with its 150,000-square-foot casino, upscale restaurants, art collection, spa, and nightlife, in a more sleek, contemporary setting than the themed resorts nearby.

Hotel Design and Rooms

ARIA's two curved glass towers, of 61 and 51 stories, hold 4,004 rooms and suites. The design is modern and upscale, with clean lines, floor-to-ceiling windows, and in-room technology that controls lighting and curtains. The resort is smoke-conscious in feel and quieter than many Strip casinos, and it connects by tram to Bellagio and Park MGM.

Special Attractions

The lobby is the main attraction, with the Lumia water feature and works by Maya Lin and other artists on display throughout the public spaces. ARIA is also part of CityCenter's public art collection, which makes a walk through the complex a free art tour.

Dining Options

With 16 restaurants, ARIA covers fine dining to casual meals. Carbone serves Italian-American classics, Bardot Brasserie is a French brasserie, CATCH focuses on seafood and sushi, and Jean Georges Steakhouse and Mastro's Ocean Club offer steak and seafood. Casual cafes, a food court, and late-night options fill out the lower end of the price range.

Las Vegas Shows

ARIA focuses more on dining, gaming, and nightlife than on a large resident production show. Its theater opened with Cirque du Soleil's Viva Elvis, which ran from 2009 to 2012, followed by Zarkana until 2016. Today the resort hosts concerts and special events, and the big Strip theaters at Bellagio and Park MGM are a short tram ride or walk away. Check the current schedule before you visit.

Pool Options

The large pool deck has several pools, cabanas, and a Liquid Pool Lounge area for guests who want a more social, adults-oriented scene. Some pools are seasonal, so confirm which are open if you visit in winter.

Nightlife Options

Jewel Nightclub is ARIA's main club, with big-name DJs, a modern, stylish design, and a more intimate size than the Strip's largest clubs. For a quieter evening, the casino has several bars and lounges, including the Lobby Bar and Alibi Ultra Lounge.

Shopping Options

Crystals, the luxury shopping center in CityCenter, connects directly to ARIA. Its angular building was designed by architect Daniel Libeskind, and it holds high-end designer boutiques such as Louis Vuitton, Prada, and Gucci, along with galleries and restaurants. ARIA also has its own gift and specialty shops.

Special Amenities

The 80,000-square-foot spa has an infinity pool and a full range of treatments, and the resort also has a fitness center, salon, and luxury suites and sky suites for high-end stays.

Nearby Attractions

ARIA is a short walk or tram ride from Bellagio and its fountains, the Cosmopolitan, Park MGM and T-Mobile Arena, and the Crystals shops. Caesars Palace, the LINQ, and the High Roller are reachable by foot or a short ride.
