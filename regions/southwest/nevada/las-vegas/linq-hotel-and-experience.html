General Information
Average Room Rates with Resort Fee:
Roughly $150–$220 per night on weeknights, including the resort fee of about $56.63 per night (with tax); rates vary widely by season, room type and events.
Average Room Rates Weekends and Holidays with Resort Fee:
Roughly $240–$350 per night on Friday–Saturday nights, holidays and major events, including the resort fee of about $56.63 per night (with tax).
Parking Rates:
Self-parking is $20 per day Monday–Thursday and $25 per day Friday–Sunday (hotel guests $20 per day), and valet is $40 per day; free self-parking for Caesars Rewards Platinum tier and above, and Nevada license holders get 3 free hours (not on event nights).
Pet Policy:
Dogs only (no cats), up to two per room of 50 pounds or less each, in designated Pet Stay rooms arranged in advance; reported fee of $75–$100 per night plus a refundable deposit. Dogs must be crated when left alone and are not allowed in the casino, restaurants or pools. Service animals are welcome.
Smoking Policy:
Smoking is allowed on the casino floor, as Nevada law exempts casinos from its indoor smoking ban. Guest rooms are predominantly non-smoking; confirm when booking.

Overview

The LINQ Hotel + Experience is a moderately priced hotel and casino in the center of the Strip, between Flamingo and Harrah's and across from Caesars Palace. It opened in 1959 as the Flamingo Capri, became the Imperial Palace in 1979 and The Quad in 2012, and was rebuilt and renamed The LINQ in 2014. People come for its central location, modern rooms at a midrange price, and easy access to the LINQ Promenade and the High Roller.

Hotel Design and Rooms

The 2014 renovation replaced the old Imperial Palace's Asian theme with a bright, contemporary look. The hotel has about 2,250 rooms, which are on the smaller side but modern, with colorful decor and a practical layout, and the casino is compact and easy to get around compared with the larger resorts nearby.

Special Attractions

The LINQ Promenade is an open-air shopping, dining, and entertainment street that Caesars opened in 2013 along the resort's south edge, with the High Roller observation wheel at its end. At 550 feet, the High Roller was the world's tallest observation wheel when it opened in 2014, and each cabin holds up to 40 people for a 30-minute ride. The Fly LINQ zip line launches from a tower at the Strip end of the Promenade and flies riders more than 1,000 feet toward the wheel.

Dining Options

The hotel and the LINQ Promenade have a wide range of mostly casual restaurants, including Hash House A Go Go for oversized breakfasts, In-N-Out Burger, Virgil's Real BBQ, Yard House, and Gordon Ramsay Fish & Chips. Restaurants change often, so check current listings.

Las Vegas Shows

Brooklyn Bowl on the Promenade is a combined bowling alley, restaurant, and concert venue that hosts live music most weeks. The LINQ also has a showroom for comedy and variety acts, and the Colosseum at Caesars Palace and the shows at Harrah's and Flamingo are a short walk away. Check the current schedule.

Pool Options

The LINQ Pool is an adults-only (21+) pool with DJs, cabanas, and a party atmosphere. The pool is seasonal, so confirm availability in cooler months.

Nightlife Options

Nightlife centers on the LINQ Promenade's bars and Brooklyn Bowl, along with O'Sheas, a casual casino-within-a-casino known for beer pong and cheap drinks. The bigger clubs at Caesars Palace are across the street.

Shopping Options

The LINQ Promenade has souvenir, fashion, and specialty shops along its walkway, and the Forum Shops at Caesars are across the Strip.

Special Amenities

The hotel has a fitness center, self-check-in kiosks, and dog-friendly Pet Stay rooms, and stays count toward Caesars Rewards. The Harrah's & The LINQ station on the Las Vegas Monorail is behind the resort.

Nearby Attractions

The LINQ is next to Flamingo and its Wildlife Habitat and Harrah's, and across from Caesars Palace and the Forum Shops. Bellagio and its fountains, the Venetian, and Paris Las Vegas are within walking distance.
