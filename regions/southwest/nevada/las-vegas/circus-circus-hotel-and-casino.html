Circus Circus Hotel and Casino

Casino features circus and trapeze acts, as well as carnival games, at its Carnival Midway and the Adventuredome, an indoor amusement park

General Information
Average Room Rates with Resort Fee:
Roughly $85–$140 per night on weeknights, including the resort fee of about $51.02 per night (with tax); rates vary widely by season, room type and events.
Average Room Rates Weekends and Holidays with Resort Fee:
Roughly $150–$230 per night on Friday–Saturday nights, holidays and major events, including the resort fee of about $51.02 per night (with tax).
Parking Rates:
Self-parking is free for all guests and visitors, and valet is about $12–$16 depending on length of stay.
Pet Policy:
Pets are not allowed in the hotel rooms; only service animals are permitted. The on-site RV park accepts pets.
Smoking Policy:
Smoking is allowed on the casino floor, as Nevada law exempts casinos from its indoor smoking ban. Guest rooms are predominantly non-smoking; confirm when booking.
Budget:
Inexpensive
Location:
2880 S Las Vegas Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89109
Website:
circuscircus.com

Overview

Circus Circus on the north Strip opened without a hotel on October 18, 1968, as a casino with a real circus. It was created by Jay Sarno, who had built Caesars Palace two years earlier, and a hotel tower was added in 1972. It remains one of the best-known family-friendly resorts in Las Vegas, with free circus acts, a large arcade and midway, and the Adventuredome indoor theme park.

Hotel Design and Rooms

The resort has a carnival and circus theme, with a big-top feel in the casino and midway. It is an older and lower-priced resort with about 3,700 rooms spread across several towers, and its rooms are known for being large, though the decor is dated compared with newer resorts. Some towers have been renovated, so it is worth checking which one you are booking.

Special Attractions

The Midway offers free circus acts, carnival games, and a big arcade. The Adventuredome, a five-acre indoor theme park under a pink dome that opened in 1993, has roller coasters and rides for kids and adults, and because it is indoors it stays open in any weather.

Dining Options

Dining at Circus Circus is mostly casual and affordable, with buffets, food court options, pizza, and snack counters, plus the long-standing Steak House at Circus Circus for a sit-down dinner.

Las Vegas Shows

Circus Circus is better known for its free circus acts than for a headline production show. Acrobats, trapeze artists, jugglers, and other performers have appeared on the Midway stage since the casino opened in 1968, and the acts run through the day. Check the current schedule.

Pool Options

The resort has an outdoor pool area that is seasonal, so confirm availability if you visit in cooler months.

Nightlife Options

The Horse-a-Round Bar, a rotating carousel bar that appears in Hunter S. Thompson's Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas, is a classic Circus Circus stop, and the casino has other bars and lounges.

Shopping Options

Circus Circus has gift and souvenir shops, candy stores, and the shops around the Midway, rather than a large shopping complex.

Special Amenities

The resort has a wedding chapel and an RV park, which is rare on the Strip, and is known for lower prices.

Nearby Attractions

Nearby attractions include the Strat, Resorts World, Sahara Las Vegas, the Fashion Show mall, and Wynn and Encore.
