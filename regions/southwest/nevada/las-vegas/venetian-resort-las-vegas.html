The Venetian Resort Las Vegas

★5.0

Hotel uses Venice, Italy, as its design inspiration and features architectural replicas of various Venetian landmarks

General Information
Average Room Rates with Resort Fee:
Roughly $190–$280 per night on weeknights, including the resort fee of about $62.36 per night (with tax); rates vary widely by season, room type and events.
Average Room Rates Weekends and Holidays with Resort Fee:
Roughly $300–$460 per night on Friday–Saturday nights, holidays and major events, including the resort fee of about $62.36 per night (with tax).
Parking Rates:
Self-parking is $20 per day for hotel guests (non-guests $20 Monday–Thursday, $25 Friday–Sunday), and valet is $40 per day; free self-parking for Venetian Rewards Sapphire tier and above, and Nevada residents get 3 free hours with ID.
Pet Policy:
Pet policy could not be confirmed; contact the resort before booking. Service animals are welcome.
Smoking Policy:
Smoking is allowed on most of the casino floor, with a designated non-smoking section. Nevada law exempts casinos from its indoor smoking ban. Guest rooms are predominantly non-smoking; confirm when booking.
Budget:
Expensive

Overview

The Venetian Las Vegas is a 36-story, 475-foot luxury hotel and casino on the site of the old Sands, inspired by Venice. It opened on May 3, 1999, and together with the Palazzo it forms one of the largest hotel complexes in the world. People come for the canals and gondolas, the shops, and fine dining.

Hotel Design and Rooms

The resort has replicas of the Doge's Palace, St. Mark's Square, the Campanile bell tower, and the Rialto Bridge, with painted ceilings and marble in the public spaces. All rooms are suites, with a sunken living area and more space than a standard Strip hotel room.

Special Attractions

The Grand Canal Shoppes is an indoor mall with a canal where gondolas ply, singing gondoliers, and painted-sky ceilings. The complex also includes the Palazzo, the Sands Expo, and the Sphere.

Dining Options

CUT by Wolfgang Puck, Bouchon, SUSHISAMBA, Mott 32, and LAVO are among the top restaurants in the complex, and Emeril Lagasse's Delmonico Steakhouse has been a Venetian favorite for years. Casual options and cafes line the Grand Canal Shoppes.

Las Vegas Shows

The Venetian Theatre hosts concerts and residencies, and Voltaire, an intimate cabaret-style venue, opened in 2023 with a Kylie Minogue residency. The Grand Canal Shoppes are home to Spiegelworld's Atomic Saloon Show. Check the schedule.

Pool Options

The pool deck has several pools and cabanas, and some are seasonal.

Nightlife Options

TAO Nightclub, which opened in 2005, is an Asian-themed club with a 20-foot Buddha, and TAO Beach is its daytime pool club. The casino and the Grand Canal Shoppes have many bars and lounges.

Shopping Options

The Grand Canal Shoppes have more than 100 stores and restaurants along the indoor canal, connecting the Venetian and the Palazzo. Madame Tussauds wax museum is at the front of the resort on the Strip.

Special Amenities

Amenities include a spa and fitness center.

Nearby Attractions

Nearby are the Palazzo, the Sphere, which connects to the resort by a pedestrian bridge, Treasure Island, and Wynn and Encore.
