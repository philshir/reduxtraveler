The STRAT Hotel, Casino & Tower

General Information
Average Room Rates with Resort Fee:
Roughly $110–$170 per night on weeknights, including the resort fee of about $56.63 per night (with tax); rates vary widely by season, room type and events.
Average Room Rates Weekends and Holidays with Resort Fee:
Roughly $180–$270 per night on Friday–Saturday nights, holidays and major events, including the resort fee of about $56.63 per night (with tax).
Parking Rates:
Self-parking is a flat $22 per 24 hours for hotel guests and out-of-state plates; free for Nevada-plated vehicles and for higher True Rewards tiers.
Pet Policy:
Pet policy could not be confirmed; contact the resort before booking. Service animals are welcome.
Smoking Policy:
Smoking is allowed on the casino floor, as Nevada law exempts casinos from its indoor smoking ban. Guest rooms are predominantly non-smoking; confirm when booking.

Overview

The Strat Hotel, Casino and SkyPod (formerly the Stratosphere) is at the north end of the Strip with a 1,149-foot observation tower, the tallest freestanding observation tower in the U.S. It opened as the Stratosphere in 1996 and was renamed the Strat in 2020. People come for the views and the thrill rides.

Hotel Design and Rooms

The tower dominates the design. The hotel's roughly 2,400 rooms are affordable and many have been renovated, and the casino is spacious.

Special Attractions

The attractions are the indoor and outdoor observation decks, a revolving restaurant, and the thrill rides on top of the tower: the Big Shot, which launches riders straight up the mast, X-Scream, Insanity, and the SkyJump, a controlled 855-foot jump.

Dining Options

Top of the World, a revolving restaurant more than 800 feet up the tower, offers fine dining with views as it slowly turns, and the resort also has casual restaurants, including McCall's Heartland Grill and a café.

Las Vegas Shows

The resort has hosted shows over the years. Check current listings.

Pool Options

The pool area is seasonal.

Nightlife Options

The 107 SkyLounge near the top of the tower serves cocktails with views of the Strip, and the casino has other bars and lounges.

Shopping Options

Shopping is limited.

Special Amenities

Amenities include a fitness center.

Nearby Attractions

Nearby are Resorts World, the Sahara, and the Sphere, and the Las Vegas Arts District, with galleries, vintage shops, and bars, is just south of the tower.
