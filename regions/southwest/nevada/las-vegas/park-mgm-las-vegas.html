A modern resort and casino featuring contemporary accommodations, diverse dining options, and entertainment venues.

General Information

Average Room Rates with Resort Fee:
Roughly $150–$230 per night on weeknights, including the resort fee of about $56.69 per night (with tax); rates vary widely by season, room type and events.
Average Room Rates Weekends and Holidays with Resort Fee:
Roughly $250–$370 per night on Friday–Saturday nights, holidays and major events, including the resort fee of about $56.69 per night (with tax).
Parking Rates:
Self-parking is $20 per day after the first free hour and valet is $40 per day; free self-parking for MGM Rewards Pearl tier and above, and Nevada residents get 3 free hours with ID.
Pet Policy:
Dogs only, up to two per room (about 100 pounds combined), in designated pet-friendly rooms; reported fee of about $100 per night. Pets are not allowed on the casino floor, in restaurants or at the pools. Service animals are welcome.
Smoking Policy:
Park MGM is a fully smoke-free resort, including the casino floor and guest rooms, with designated outdoor smoking areas.
Budget:
Average
Hours:
Hotel and casino open 24 hours daily; restaurants and entertainment venues operate on individual schedules
Seasons:
All year

Overview

Park MGM opened in 1996 as Monte Carlo and was rebranded in 2018. In 2020 it became the first resort on the Strip to go entirely smoke-free. People come for the smoke-free casino, modern rooms, acclaimed dining, and the Dolby Live theater.

Hotel Design and Rooms

Park MGM blends contemporary design with convenience, offering a stylish, upscale experience. The top floors are home to NoMad Las Vegas, a separate boutique hotel with its own entrance, residential-style rooms, and a pool. The resort sits next to T-Mobile Arena and The Park outdoor promenade.

Special Attractions

The Dolby Live theater hosts major concerts and residencies, and The Park promenade offers dining and entertainment.

Dining Options

Bavette's Steakhouse & Bar is a Chicago-style steakhouse, Primrose is a French-inspired cafe, Eataly Las Vegas is an Italian marketplace, Best Friend is Korean-Mexican fusion from Chef Roy Choi, and NoMad Library offers refined American cuisine.

Las Vegas Shows

Dolby Live, which opened in 2016 as the Park Theater, hosts major concerts and residencies, with past headliners including Lady Gaga, Bruno Mars, and Aerosmith. Check the current schedule.

Pool Options

The resort has a pool area that is seasonal.

Nightlife Options

Park MGM has lounges and bars, and the nearby promenade has more nightlife.

Shopping Options

Shopping is limited inside the resort, with larger options at nearby ARIA and the Crystals.

Special Amenities

Amenities include a spa and fitness center, and the smoke-free casino is a draw.

Nearby Attractions

Nearby are T-Mobile Arena, home of the Vegas Golden Knights, New York-New York, ARIA, Bellagio, and the Cosmopolitan.
