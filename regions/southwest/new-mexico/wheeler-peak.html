How to Get There

From Taos (20 mi): Take NM-150 north from Taos toward Taos Ski Valley, continue through Taos Ski Valley to the upper parking area near Twining Campground and Williams Lake Trailhead, then follow signs to the Wheeler Peak Trail trailhead.

Overview

Wheeler Peak (13,161 feet) is the highest point in New Mexico, in the Sangre de Cristo Mountains northeast of Taos, south of Red River, and two miles from Taos Ski Valley. It's a non-technical hike for fit hikers, and the summit has views over the Rockies. The Wheeler Peak Wilderness protects it.
