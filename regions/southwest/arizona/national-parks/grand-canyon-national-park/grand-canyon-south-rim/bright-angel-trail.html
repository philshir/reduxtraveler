Bright Angel Trail, Grand Canyon South Rim

Trail descending 4380 feet to the Colorado River is the most common method used to access Phantom Ranch by hikers and mules

General Information

Length:
12.2 miles round trip to Plateau Point
Elevation Gain:
Approximately 3,200 ft
Difficulty:
Strenuous
Hours:
Open 24 hours a day, year-round, subject to weather, heat advisories, and National Park Service closures.
Fees:
Grand Canyon National Park entrance fee required.
No permit required for day hiking.
Backcountry permit required for overnight camping.
Pet Policy:
Pets are not allowed on the Bright Angel Trail.
Closest cities with hotels:
Tusayan, AZ (7 mi)
Seasons:
Best visited in fall, winter, and spring. Summer hiking can be dangerous due to extreme inner-canyon temperatures.
Location:
Bright Angel Trailhead, Grand Canyon Village, AZ
Website:
nps.gov

How to Get There

From Tusayan, AZ (7 mi): Take AZ-64 North into Grand Canyon National Park, continue to Grand Canyon Village, follow signs to Bright Angel Lodge, and walk a short distance to the Bright Angel Trailhead located along the South Rim.

Overview

Bright Angel Trail is the Grand Canyon’s most popular corridor trail, starting at Grand Canyon Village on the South Rim and descending 4,380 feet to the Colorado River. The River Trail then continues 1.9 miles to Bright Angel Campground and Phantom Ranch, and together they are the most common route to Phantom Ranch for hikers and mules. Rangers maintain and patrol it regularly.

Water

Water is available at the Mile-and-a-Half Resthouse, Three-Mile Resthouse and Indian Garden. The two upper resthouses are usually shut off from about October to April to keep the pipeline from freezing.
