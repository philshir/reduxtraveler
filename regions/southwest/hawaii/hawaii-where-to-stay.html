Where to Stay in Hawaii: Oahu, Maui, Hawaii Island, and Kauai

The Hawaiian Islands offer accommodations for every type of traveler, from budget-conscious visitors looking for affordable lodging to travelers seeking world-class luxury resorts. Whether you are visiting Oahu, Maui, Hawaii Island (the Big Island), or Kauai, choosing the right area and style of accommodation can greatly enhance your vacation experience.

Budget Accommodations

Budget travelers can find comfortable and affordable lodging options throughout Hawaii, particularly in larger communities and near major tourist destinations.

Common budget accommodation types include:

  • Hostels
  • Budget hotels and motels
  • Guesthouses
  • Vacation rentals
  • Budget-friendly inns

Benefits of budget accommodations:

  • Lower nightly rates
  • Good locations near attractions and beaches
  • Access to kitchens or shared facilities
  • More money available for activities and dining

Mid-Range Accommodations

Mid-range properties offer a balance of comfort, convenience, and affordability. These accommodations often include resort amenities without the premium prices associated with luxury resorts.

Typical mid-range options include:

  • Chain hotels
  • Boutique hotels
  • Condominium resorts
  • Family-friendly resorts
  • Vacation rental communities

Common amenities may include:

  • Swimming pools
  • Beach access
  • Restaurants and bars
  • Fitness centers
  • Free parking or Wi-Fi

Luxury Accommodations

Hawaii is home to some of the world's most renowned luxury resorts. These properties feature exceptional service, oceanfront locations, high-end dining, spa facilities, golf courses, and exclusive guest experiences.

Luxury resort features often include:

  • Oceanfront suites and villas
  • Private beaches
  • Luxury spas
  • Championship golf courses
  • Fine dining restaurants
  • Personalized concierge services
  • Exclusive cultural and outdoor activities

Staying on Oahu

Oahu is Hawaii's most populated island and combines urban attractions, historic sites, surf beaches, nightlife, and luxury resorts.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Waikiki
  • Honolulu
  • Ko Olina
  • North Shore
  • Kailua

Budget options on Oahu:

  • Hostels in Waikiki
  • Budget hotels near Honolulu
  • Vacation rentals outside major resort areas

Mid-range options on Oahu:

  • Waikiki beach hotels
  • Boutique properties
  • Condo-style resorts

Luxury options on Oahu:

  • Oceanfront resorts in Waikiki
  • Luxury resorts in Ko Olina
  • Upscale beachfront hotels with spa and golf amenities

Best for:

  • First-time visitors
  • Nightlife
  • Shopping
  • Historical attractions
  • Surf culture

Staying on Maui

Maui is known for its scenic coastline, luxury resorts, whale watching, beaches, and the famous Road to Hana.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Lahaina area
  • Ka'anapali
  • Kapalua
  • Wailea
  • Kihei

Budget options on Maui:

  • Condominium rentals
  • Guesthouses
  • Budget-friendly hotels in Kihei

Mid-range options on Maui:

  • Beachfront condominiums
  • Resort hotels in Ka'anapali
  • Boutique lodging properties

Luxury options on Maui:

  • Wailea luxury resorts
  • Oceanfront villas
  • High-end golf and spa resorts

Best for:

  • Romantic getaways
  • Beach vacations
  • Scenic drives
  • Whale watching
  • Luxury travelers

Staying on Hawaii Island (The Big Island)

Hawaii Island, commonly known as the Big Island, is the largest Hawaiian island and offers diverse landscapes that include volcanoes, black-sand beaches, rainforests, and lava fields.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Kailua-Kona
  • Kohala Coast
  • Waikoloa
  • Hilo
  • Volcano Village

Budget options on the Big Island:

  • Guesthouses in Hilo
  • Volcano-area lodges
  • Vacation rentals

Mid-range options on the Big Island:

  • Hotels in Kona
  • Condominium resorts in Waikoloa
  • Boutique inns

Luxury options on the Big Island:

  • Kohala Coast luxury resorts
  • Oceanfront villas
  • Golf and spa destinations

Best for:

  • Volcano exploration
  • Snorkeling and diving
  • Stargazing
  • Nature enthusiasts
  • Longer stays

Staying on Kauai

Kauai, often called the "Garden Isle," is known for its lush scenery, dramatic cliffs, waterfalls, and relaxed atmosphere.

Popular areas to stay:

  • Princeville
  • Hanalei
  • Poipu
  • Lihue
  • Kapaa

Budget options on Kauai:

  • Vacation rentals
  • Small inns
  • Budget-friendly hotels near Kapaa

Mid-range options on Kauai:

  • Resort condominiums
  • Boutique hotels
  • Beachfront inns

Luxury options on Kauai:

  • Princeville resorts
  • Poipu oceanfront resorts
  • Luxury villas and spa properties

Best for:

  • Hiking
  • Nature photography
  • Relaxation
  • Scenic coastal views
  • Outdoor adventures

Choosing the Right Island

  1. Choose Oahu for nightlife, shopping, surfing, and first-time visits.
  2. Choose Maui for beaches, luxury resorts, romance, and scenic drives.
  3. Choose the Big Island for volcanoes, diverse landscapes, and outdoor exploration.
  4. Choose Kauai for lush scenery, hiking, and a more relaxed island experience.

Recommended Stay Lengths

  • Oahu: 4 to 7 days
  • Maui: 4 to 7 days
  • Big Island: 5 to 8 days
  • Kauai: 4 to 6 days

Bottom Line

Visitors to Hawaii can find accommodations ranging from budget-friendly hostels and vacation rentals to world-class luxury resorts. Oahu offers the widest variety of lodging and entertainment, Maui is ideal for resort vacations and romance, the Big Island provides unique geological and outdoor experiences, and Kauai delivers spectacular natural beauty and a laid-back atmosphere. Selecting accommodations based on budget, location, and travel style will help create the best possible Hawaiian vacation experience.
