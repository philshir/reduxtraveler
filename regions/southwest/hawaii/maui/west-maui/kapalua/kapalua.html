How to Get There

From Kahului, HI (33 mi): Take HI-30 W past Lahaina, then turn left onto Office Road and follow signs to Kapalua Resort.

Overview

Kapalua is an upscale resort area on Maui's northwest coast, along Honoapiilani Highway, known for golf, quiet bays and a more secluded feel than nearby Kaanapali. It was developed from pineapple land by the Maui Land and Pineapple Company, and the Hawaiian name for the area was Honokahua.

People come to Kapalua for its beaches and snorkeling, its championship golf, its restaurants and spa resorts, and for hiking and ocean views. Kapalua Bay has been named among the best beaches in the country, and the area hosts a major PGA Tour event at the start of each year.

History

The Honolua and Honokahua area was farmed and settled by Hawaiians for centuries, and a burial ground at Honokahua was protected after a 1989 controversy. The land was a pineapple plantation from 1913 until the 1970s and 80s, and the Maui Land and Pineapple Company opened the resort in the mid-1970s, keeping the pineapple fields and adding the Bay Course.

Sightseeing and Popular Landmarks

The beaches of Kapalua Bay, Oneloa Bay and Napili Bay, along with the coastal trail that connects them, are the main attractions. Honolua Bay is a marine life conservation district known for snorkeling in summer and surf in winter, and the scenic road north leads to Nakalele Point and Kahakuloa.

Culture and Museums

The Kapalua Resort hosts the Kapalua Wine and Food Festival, along with concerts and galleries. The Kapalua Coastal Trail passes Hawaiian cultural sites, and tours at the Honolua Ridge explain Maui's agricultural history.

Parks

The Puu Kukui Watershed Preserve, a nature reserve of rainforest in the West Maui Mountains, protects endangered native species and offers guided hikes. The Kapalua Coastal Trail and Dragon's Teeth rock formations at Makaluapuna Point give scenic walks, and the resort's Bay and Plantation golf courses are open to visitors.

Seasons and Weather

Kapalua is sunny and dry, with highs in the low to mid 80s°F year-round and about 25 to 30 inches of rain a year, a little more than Lahaina because of the nearby mountains. Winter brings large surf to the north-facing bays and humpback whale sightings, and summer has calm water ideal for snorkeling.
