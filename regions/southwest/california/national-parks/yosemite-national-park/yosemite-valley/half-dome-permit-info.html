Half Dome Permit Info

  6. Half Dome Permit Info

Overview

A Half Dome permit is required for all day hikers who wish to hike beyond the base of the Subdome and ascend the cables to the summit of Half Dome in Yosemite National Park while the cables are in place. The permit system is designed to protect the wilderness character of the area, reduce crowding, safeguard natural and cultural resources, and improve visitor safety. When the cables are installed, a maximum of 300 hikers per day are allowed beyond the base of the Subdome, including approximately 225 day hikers and 75 backpackers.

The cables are typically installed on the Friday before Memorial Day and remain available until the day after the second Monday in October, although operational dates may change depending on weather and trail conditions. Half Dome permits for day hikers do not include camping, lodging, or other overnight accommodations. Permit holders should carry a valid photo ID along with their permit confirmation during their hike.

Permit Types

  • Preseason Lottery: Provides access to most available day-hiking permits before the hiking season begins.
  • Daily Lottery: Offers additional permits based on cancellations and estimated underuse during the hiking season.

Preseason Lottery

The preseason lottery allocates up to 225 day-hiker permits for each available hiking date. Applications are accepted throughout March, and applicants are notified of their results in mid-April.

  • Application Period: March 1 through March 31.
  • Permits Available: Up to 225 day-hiker permits per day.
  • Group Size: Applications may request up to six permits.
  • Date Selection: Applicants may request specific dates or a range of dates.
  • Priority Processing: If multiple requested dates are available, permits are awarded based on the highest-priority date selected.

Important Application Details

  • Permit Holder Required: Applicants must designate a permit holder.
  • Alternate Holder Recommended: An alternate permit holder may also be listed.
  • Permit Validation: Either the permit holder or designated alternate must be present for the permit to be valid.
  • Identification: Permit holders should bring a photo ID and permit confirmation email.

Daily Lottery

Additional permits are released throughout the hiking season through a daily lottery program. These permits are made available based on cancellations and projected permit underuse.

  • Application Timing: Applications are submitted two days before the intended hiking date.
  • Application Window: Midnight to 4:00 PM Pacific Time.
  • Notification: Lottery results are provided later that evening.
  • No Alternate Allowed: Daily lottery applications only permit a single permit holder.
  • Better Availability: Success rates are generally higher on weekdays, especially later in the season.

How to Apply

Applications for both the preseason and daily lotteries are managed through Recreation.gov. Applicants can submit requests online during the applicable lottery period or apply by phone through the Recreation.gov call center.

  • Online: Recreation.gov
  • Phone: 877-444-6777
  • Call Center Hours: 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM Pacific Time

Fees

Two separate fees apply to Half Dome permit applications. One fee covers application processing, while the second fee is charged only when a permit is successfully awarded.

  • Application Fee: $10 per application. This fee is non-refundable and charged when the application is submitted.
  • Permit Fee: $10 per person. This fee is charged only after a permit is awarded.
  • Refund Policy: The permit fee is fully refundable if the permit is cancelled by 11:59 PM Pacific Time on the day before the hike or if the cables are not installed on the permitted date.
