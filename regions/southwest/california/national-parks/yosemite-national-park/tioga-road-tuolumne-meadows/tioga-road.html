How to Get There

From Yosemite Valley El Portal Entrance (19 mi): Drive along El Portal Rd and Big Oak Flat Rd. Drive to Tiogo Rd

Overview

Tioga Road (State Route 120) is Yosemite’s high country scenic drive, crossing the park from Crane Flat to Tioga Pass at 9,945 feet, the highest paved through road in California, and continuing to Lee Vining and Mono Lake. Its 47 miles inside the park pass forests, meadows, lakes, granite domes and Tuolumne Meadows, with many turnouts and trailheads. The road is normally open from Memorial Day weekend to about November, and is closed by snow in winter.

Beyond the park, SR 120 runs west to Interstate 5 through Oakdale and the Sierra foothills, and east past Mono Lake’s South Tufa and Mono Mills.
