Shuttle System Overview

The Yosemite National Park Shuttle System provides free transportation throughout Yosemite Valley and selected seasonal destinations within the park. The shuttle network helps reduce traffic congestion, minimize parking challenges, improve visitor access, and protect Yosemite's natural resources. Visitors are encouraged to park their vehicles upon arrival and use the shuttle system to reach major attractions, lodging areas, campgrounds, trailheads, visitor centers, and other services.

The transportation system includes the Valleywide Shuttle, the East Valley Shuttle, and several seasonal shuttle services operating in different areas of the park. Most shuttle routes are free and provide convenient access to Yosemite's most popular destinations.

Yosemite Valley Shuttle System

The Yosemite Valley Shuttle System operates throughout Yosemite Valley and serves many of the park's most visited locations. The service allows visitors to explore the valley without needing to move their vehicles between destinations.

  • Free Service: No separate shuttle ticket is required.
  • Frequent Operations: Shuttles run throughout the day.
  • Valley Access: Connects visitor centers, lodging, restaurants, campgrounds, trailheads, and scenic viewpoints.
  • Convenient Transportation: Visitors can park once and use the shuttle to reach multiple destinations.

Valleywide Shuttle

The Valleywide Shuttle serves all major stops throughout Yosemite Valley and is the primary transportation route for most visitors.

  • Comprehensive Coverage: Serves all shuttle stops within Yosemite Valley.
  • Major Destinations: Includes lodges, food services, campgrounds, visitor facilities, and trailheads.
  • Operating Hours: Approximately 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
  • Regular Service: Buses generally arrive every 12 to 22 minutes.

East Valley Shuttle

The East Valley Shuttle focuses on destinations in eastern Yosemite Valley and provides convenient service to several popular hiking and camping locations.

  • Service Area: Yosemite Village, Curry Village, Pines Campgrounds, and eastern valley trailheads.
  • Operating Hours: Approximately 7:00 AM to 10:00 PM.
  • Frequent Service: Buses generally arrive every 8 to 12 minutes.
  • Trailhead Access: Provides transportation to popular hiking destinations.

Popular Destinations Served

  • Yosemite Village: Visitor information, exhibits, dining, and park services.
  • Yosemite Valley Lodge: Lodging, dining, and recreation services.
  • Curry Village: Lodging, food service, and outdoor activities.
  • Lower Yosemite Fall: Access to one of Yosemite's most famous waterfall viewpoints.
  • Happy Isles: Trailhead access for the Mist Trail and numerous wilderness routes.
  • The Ahwahnee: Historic lodging and dining destination.

Seasonal Shuttle Services

Additional transportation services operate seasonally based on weather conditions, road accessibility, and visitor demand.

  • Mariposa Grove Shuttle: Connects the Mariposa Grove Welcome Plaza with the giant sequoia grove.
  • Badger Pass Shuttle: Provides transportation between Yosemite Valley and Badger Pass Ski Area during winter operations.
  • Glacier Point Transportation: Seasonal transportation may be available when Glacier Point Road is open.
  • Tuolumne Meadows Service: Limited seasonal transportation may operate during summer months.
