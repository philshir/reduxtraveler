How to Get There

From Foothills Visitor Center (2 mi): Drive east on Mineral King Road from Highway 198 in Three Rivers. Follow the steep, winding road for approximately 25 miles to reach the Eagle Lake Trailhead. Hike 3.6 miles with 2,200 ft elevation gain to reach Eagle Sinkhole.

Overview

The Eagle Sinkholes are a fascinating natural feature in the Mineral King area of Sequoia National Park, where Eagle Creek disappears into the ground through holes in the marble bedrock and flows underground. The short hike leads to the sinkholes along the trail to Eagle Lake, offering a glimpse into the area's unusual geology. Hikers come for the sinkholes, the creek, and the views of the surrounding peaks.

Getting to the Trailhead

Mineral King is a remote, high valley in the southern part of Sequoia National Park, reached by Mineral King Road, which leaves CA-198 just east of Three Rivers and climbs about 25 miles to the valley. The road is narrow, steep, winding, and partly unpaved, takes about 1.5 hours to drive, and is not suitable for trailers or RVs. It is open seasonally, typically from late May through October. Marmots in Mineral King are known to chew on radiator hoses and wiring in early summer, so many visitors wrap the underside of their vehicles with tarps. The hike begins at the Eagle/Mosquito Trailhead near the end of Mineral King Road. Parking is limited.

Trail Description

From the trailhead, the trail climbs steadily along Eagle Creek, passing a junction with the trail to Mosquito Lakes, to the Eagle Sinkholes, about 0.9 mile from the trailhead. Hikers can view where the creek vanishes underground before returning the same way, for a total of about 1.8 miles round trip, or continue to Eagle Lake.

Trail Conditions and Difficulty

The hike is moderate, with a steady climb on a rocky trail. No scrambling is required. Stay back from the edges of the sinkholes, which can be unstable. At high elevations, the thin air makes climbing harder than many visitors expect, and afternoon thunderstorms are common in summer, so start early.
