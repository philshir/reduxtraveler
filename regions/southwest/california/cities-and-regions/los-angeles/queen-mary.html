Overview

The Queen Mary is a retired British ocean liner permanently docked in Long Beach, now a hotel, museum and event venue. Built in Scotland and launched in 1936 for the Cunard-White Star Line, she sailed the North Atlantic until 1967, when she moved to Long Beach. Visitors tour the decks, engine room and exhibits, and she is on the National Register of Historic Places and a Historic Hotel of America.
