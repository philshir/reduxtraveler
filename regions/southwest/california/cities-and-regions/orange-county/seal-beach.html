Seal Beach

★5.0

City located in the westernmost corner of Orange County featuring surfing and the Seal Beach Pier, the second longest wooden pier in California

General Information

Pet Policy:
Seal Beach is pet-friendly, with dog-friendly parks, walking trails, outdoor dining patios, and pet-friendly lodging. Leashed dogs are welcome in parks such as Gum Grove Nature Park and along several local trails. Dogs are not permitted on the city beach.
Seasons:
All year. Spring and fall offer mild temperatures and fewer crowds, summer is popular for beach activities and the pier area, and winter provides pleasant coastal weather for walking and sightseeing.
Location:
Seal Beach, CA 90740
Website:
sealbeachca.gov

How to Get There

From Long Beach, CA (8 mi): Take CA-1 South (Pacific Coast Highway) or I-405 South to Seal Beach Boulevard, then continue west toward Main Street, the Seal Beach Pier, and the oceanfront district.

Overview

Seal Beach is a laid-back beach town in the westernmost corner of Orange County, near Long Beach, known for its wide sandy beach, a historic 1,865-foot wooden pier, walkable Main Street with cafés and shops, and old-time small-town charm. It is also home to the Seal Beach National Wildlife Refuge and the Naval Weapons Station.

Things to Do

Explore a Classic Southern California Beach Town

Seal Beach is a charming coastal community in northwestern Orange County known for its relaxed atmosphere, wide sandy beaches, historic pier, and walkable Main Street. The city offers visitors a quieter beach experience while still providing easy access to Southern California's coastal attractions.

Visit the Seal Beach Pier

The Seal Beach Pier is one of the longest wooden piers in California and serves as the city's most iconic landmark. Visitors can enjoy fishing, sightseeing, photography, and panoramic views of the Pacific Ocean while watching surfers, seabirds, and passing marine life.

Stroll Along Main Street

Main Street features a variety of local restaurants, coffee shops, boutiques, galleries, and specialty stores. Its small-town atmosphere and pedestrian-friendly layout make it a popular destination for dining, shopping, and exploring the heart of the community.

Relax on Seal Beach

The city's wide sandy shoreline offers excellent opportunities for swimming, sunbathing, surfing, beachcombing, volleyball, and shoreline walks. The beach is generally less crowded than many nearby Southern California beaches, making it a favorite destination for families and visitors seeking a more relaxed coastal setting.

Discover the Seal Beach National Wildlife Refuge

Located within Anaheim Bay, the Seal Beach National Wildlife Refuge protects important coastal wetland habitats for migratory birds and native wildlife. Guided tours and special access events provide opportunities to observe birds, wetlands, and conservation efforts within this protected ecosystem.

Explore Alamitos Bay and Water Activities

Adjacent to Seal Beach, Alamitos Bay provides opportunities for kayaking, paddleboarding, sailing, boating, and wildlife observation. The calm waters make it a popular location for recreational water sports and waterfront exploration.

Top Trails

Seal Beach Pier and Oceanfront Walk

Length: Approximately 1.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

This scenic walk combines the historic pier with the beachfront promenade, offering beautiful ocean views, excellent people-watching opportunities, and easy access to restaurants and shops near Main Street.

San Gabriel River Trail

Length: Up to 38 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

Accessible from Seal Beach, this paved multi-use trail follows the San Gabriel River and is popular for walking, running, cycling, and long-distance recreation. The route connects coastal areas with inland communities.

Bolsa Chica Wetlands Trail

Length: Approximately 5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

Located just southeast of Seal Beach, this trail system traverses protected wetland habitats and provides outstanding opportunities for birdwatching, wildlife photography, and exploring one of Southern California's most significant coastal ecosystems.

Anaheim Bay Overlook Trail

Length: Approximately 2 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

This easy route offers views of Anaheim Bay, coastal wetlands, the harbor area, and seasonal migratory birds. It is a peaceful option for visitors interested in nature observation and photography.
