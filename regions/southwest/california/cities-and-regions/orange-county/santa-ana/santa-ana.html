All year

Santa Ana, CA

How to Get There

From Anaheim (10 mi): Take I-5 S toward Santa Ana and exit onto CA-55 S, then follow signs toward downtown and Civic Center Drive.

Overview

Santa Ana is the county seat of Orange County and one of its largest cities, with about 310,000 residents, in the Southern California coastal plain about 35 miles southeast of Los Angeles. It is the governmental and cultural center of the county, with a dense downtown of historic buildings, murals, courthouses and galleries.

Visitors come to Santa Ana for its museums, its lively downtown, its Latino culture and its food, especially the Mexican restaurants and markets of the Fourth Street area. The city is close to Disneyland, the Orange County beaches and John Wayne Airport, and it is a more local, authentic alternative to the resort areas nearby.

History

The Tongva and Juaneno peoples lived here, and the Spanish named the Santa Ana River in 1769. William Spurgeon bought the land in 1869 and founded the town, which incorporated in 1886 and became the county seat when Orange County split from Los Angeles County in 1889. Citrus, walnuts and sugar beets drove the early economy, and many of the downtown's brick buildings date from that era.

Sightseeing and Popular Landmarks

The Old Orange County Courthouse (1901), the Santa Ana Historic Core and the Artists Village, a downtown arts district, are the main landmarks, and the 4th Street Market is a food hall in a former market building. The Santa Ana Zoo at Prentice Park and the Discovery Cube Orange County, a hands-on science center, are family attractions.

Culture and Museums

The Bowers Museum, founded in 1936, shows Native American, Pre-Columbian and Pacific art and hosts traveling exhibits, and the Lyon Air Museum at John Wayne Airport displays vintage airplanes and vehicles. The Artists Village galleries and the Day of the Dead celebrations reflect the city's Latino heritage.

Parks

Santa Ana has more than 30 parks, including Centennial Regional Park, Santiago Park, Prentice Park with the Santa Ana Zoo, and Willowick Park. The Santa Ana River Trail provides a long path for biking and walking, and Irvine Regional Park and the beaches of Newport and Huntington are a short drive away.

Seasons and Weather

Santa Ana has a mild Mediterranean climate, with summer highs in the 80s°F, winter highs in the upper 60s, and about 13 inches of rain a year, mostly from November to March. Because it is inland, it is warmer than the coast in summer, with less fog, and fall can bring hot Santa Ana winds.
