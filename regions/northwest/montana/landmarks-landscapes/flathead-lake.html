How to Get There

From Missoula (83 miles): Continue to I-90 W. Take US-93 N to Big Arm State Park Rd/Meadowlark Ln in Lake County. Continue on Meadowlark Ln. Drive to Big Arm State Park Rd.

Overview

Flathead Lake in northwest Montana, seven miles south of Kalispell, is the largest natural freshwater lake in the western U.S. by area, about 30 miles long and 16 miles wide, covering 197 square miles. Mission Mountains rise along the east shore and the Salish Mountains to the west. It is a remnant of the giant glacial Lake Missoula era, with a raised level since Kerr Dam was built in 1930. Its mild climate makes it a summer destination for boating and swimming, and cherries are grown along the shore.

Highlights

  • Wild Horse Island State Park: a wild island reached by boat, with bighorn sheep and wild horses
  • Flathead Lake State Park: beaches and boat launches, including West Shore and Yellow Bay
  • Towns: Polson, Bigfork, and Lakeside

Things to Do

Visit the Largest Natural Freshwater Lake West of the Mississippi

Flathead Lake is one of Montana's most treasured natural attractions and the largest natural freshwater lake in the western United States outside Alaska. Surrounded by mountains, orchards, charming lakeside communities, and scenic state parks, the lake offers outstanding opportunities for recreation and sightseeing throughout the year.

Enjoy Boating and Water Recreation

Flathead Lake is renowned for boating, sailing, kayaking, canoeing, paddleboarding, and jet skiing. Its clear waters and expansive shoreline make it a favorite destination for both casual visitors and outdoor enthusiasts looking to spend time on the water.

Explore Wild Horse Island State Park

Wild Horse Island is the largest island on Flathead Lake and is accessible by boat. The island is known for its scenic hiking trails, wildlife viewing opportunities, and resident population of wild horses, bighorn sheep, mule deer, and bald eagles.

Visit Lakeside Communities

Towns surrounding Flathead Lake, including Bigfork, Polson, Lakeside, and Somers, offer shopping, dining, art galleries, local events, and waterfront parks. Each community provides its own unique character and access to the lake's scenic shoreline.

Tour the Flathead Cherry Orchards

The region surrounding Flathead Lake is famous for cherry production. During the summer harvest season, visitors can stop at orchard stands and local markets to sample fresh cherries and other locally grown produce.

Fish the Waters of Flathead Lake

Anglers can pursue lake trout, yellow perch, whitefish, and several other fish species. Fishing is popular both from shore and by boat, and the lake attracts visitors from throughout the region.

Top Trails

Wild Horse Island Trail System

Length: Multiple interconnected trails
Elevation Gain: Varies
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

The island's trail network offers opportunities to explore forests, grasslands, lakeshore viewpoints, and wildlife habitat. Hikers can enjoy panoramic views of Flathead Lake while watching for wild horses and other animals.

Bear Dance Trail

Length: Approximately 3.8 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Moderate

Located near Bigfork, this scenic trail provides excellent views of Flathead Lake, forested hillsides, and the surrounding mountains while passing through a variety of natural habitats.

Jewel Basin Hiking Area Trails

Length: Various routes available
Elevation Gain: Moderate to Significant
Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging

A short drive from Flathead Lake, the Jewel Basin Hiking Area features dozens of alpine lakes, mountain trails, and spectacular viewpoints. It is one of northwest Montana's premier hiking destinations.

Blacktail Mountain Trail

Length: Approximately 4 to 8 miles depending on route
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Moderate

This trail system offers panoramic views of Flathead Lake, the Flathead Valley, and nearby mountain ranges. It is popular for hiking, wildlife viewing, and photography.

Wayfarers State Park Trails

Length: Multiple short trails
Elevation Gain: Minimal to Moderate
Difficulty: Easy

Located in Bigfork along the northeastern shore of Flathead Lake, Wayfarers State Park features easy walking trails, picnic areas, lakeshore access, and scenic viewpoints overlooking the water.
