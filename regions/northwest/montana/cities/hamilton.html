How to Get There

From Missoula (47 mi): Take US-93 S through the Bitterroot Valley directly into downtown Hamilton.

Overview

Hamilton is a small city in the Bitterroot Valley of western Montana, at the base of the Bitterroot Mountains, and it is the county seat of Ravalli County. It is known for the Daly Mansion, the home of copper king Marcus Daly, for the Rocky Mountain Laboratories, a federal research facility, and for its outdoor recreation in the nearby wilderness. The city of about 5,000 has a pleasant small-town atmosphere with a historic downtown and views of the jagged peaks of the Bitterroots.

Visitors come to Hamilton for the Daly Mansion, for the Ravalli County Museum, and for fly fishing on the Bitterroot River. They also come for hiking and camping in the Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness, for hot springs at Lost Trail and Lolo, and for the Bitterroot Valley's apple orchards and farms.

History

The Salish people lived in the Bitterroot Valley for thousands of years, and the Jesuit St. Mary's Mission, established in 1841, was Montana's first permanent European settlement. In 1890, Marcus Daly, a copper magnate from Butte, founded the town and named it for his friend James Hamilton, and developed the Bitterroot Stock Farm, a famous horse-breeding operation. Hamilton became the county seat in 1894, and in 1928 the Rocky Mountain Laboratory was established to study Rocky Mountain spotted fever, spread by ticks. The town has since grown as a retirement and recreation community.

Sightseeing and Popular Landmarks

The Daly Mansion, a 42-room Georgian Revival mansion built in 1910, is a historic house museum on the outskirts of town, and the Ravalli County Museum is in the old courthouse. The Rocky Mountain Laboratories, a NIH facility, studies infectious diseases, and the Bitterroot Valley has farm stands, orchards, and wineries. Hamilton's historic downtown has shops, cafes, and the Bitterroot Public Library.

Culture and Museums

The Ravalli County Museum has exhibits on the history of the Bitterroot Valley, the Salish, and Marcus Daly, and the Daly Mansion offers tours and events. The Hamilton Players and the Bitterroot Performing Arts Council present concerts and plays, and the Bitterroot Valley's St. Mary's Mission, in nearby Stevensville, is Montana's oldest church. The Ravalli County Fair in August is a popular event.

Parks

The Bitterroot National Forest and the Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness provide trails, campgrounds, and fishing, and Lake Como is a favorite for swimming and hiking. The Bitterroot River is known for trout fishing, and Lost Trail Pass, with a ski area, and the Lost Trail Hot Springs are to the south. Blodgett Canyon, a gorge with a trail, is a short drive.

Seasons and Weather

Hamilton has a semi-arid continental climate. Summers are warm and dry, with highs in the 80s°F, and winters are cold, with highs in the 30s°F and about 35 inches of snow. The valley is relatively mild by Montana standards, and summer smoke from wildfires can occur.

Things to Do

Explore the Heart of Montana's Bitterroot Valley

Hamilton is the largest community in Montana's scenic Bitterroot Valley and serves as a gateway to the Bitterroot Mountains, Sapphire Mountains, and Bitterroot National Forest. Visitors enjoy a combination of outdoor adventure, historic attractions, western culture, and spectacular mountain scenery.

Discover Bitterroot National Forest

Hamilton provides direct access to Bitterroot National Forest, which offers extensive opportunities for hiking, backpacking, camping, fishing, wildlife viewing, horseback riding, and scenic driving. The forest stretches across millions of acres of mountains, rivers, and wilderness areas.

Visit Lake Como

Just south of Hamilton, Lake Como is a popular destination for boating, kayaking, paddleboarding, fishing, swimming, picnicking, and hiking. The lake is surrounded by mountain scenery and serves as a gateway to numerous Bitterroot Range trails.

Explore Historic Downtown Hamilton

Downtown Hamilton features locally owned shops, restaurants, breweries, galleries, and historic buildings that reflect the area's rich western heritage. Walking through downtown provides a glimpse into the history and culture of the Bitterroot Valley.

Tour the Daly Mansion

The Daly Mansion was once the summer home of copper baron Marcus Daly and is one of Montana's most significant historic sites. Visitors can tour the mansion, explore the grounds, and learn about the history of Montana's mining industry and early development.

Visit Nearby Waterfalls

Several beautiful waterfalls are located near Hamilton, including Skalkaho Falls, Mill Creek Falls, Snowshoe Falls, and Canyon Falls. These destinations offer excellent opportunities for photography, picnicking, and short hikes.

Top Trails

Blodgett Canyon Trail

Length: Approximately 4 to 12 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Moderate

One of the most iconic hikes in western Montana, Blodgett Canyon follows Blodgett Creek through a dramatic granite canyon with towering cliffs, waterfalls, and access to the Selway-Bitterroot Wilderness. Visitors can choose shorter hikes or continue deeper into the canyon.

Mill Creek Falls Trail

Length: Approximately 6 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 650 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

This scenic trail follows Mill Creek through a forested canyon before reaching a beautiful waterfall. The route features mountain views, creek crossings, and opportunities to enjoy the natural beauty of the Bitterroot National Forest.

Blodgett Overlook Trail

Length: Approximately 3 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 400 to 500 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

A shorter alternative to the full Blodgett Canyon hike, this trail climbs to an overlook with sweeping views of the canyon, surrounding cliffs, and the Bitterroot Valley below.

Canyon Creek Trail to Canyon Lake

Length: Approximately 9 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 2,400 feet
Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging

This beautiful mountain trail passes waterfalls, forests, and alpine scenery before reaching Canyon Lake, a picturesque alpine lake nestled among the Bitterroot Mountains.

Ward Mountain Trail

Length: Approximately 8 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Significant
Difficulty: Challenging

Hikers are rewarded with spectacular views of the Bitterroot Valley, nearby mountain ranges, and surrounding wilderness. This trail is best suited for experienced hikers seeking a strenuous adventure.
