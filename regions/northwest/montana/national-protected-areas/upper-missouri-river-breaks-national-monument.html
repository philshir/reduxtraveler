How to Get There

From Billings (223 miles): Follow Montana Ave and I-90BL E/1st Ave N to US-87 N/Hwy 312/Main St. Follow US-87 N to MT-19 N in Fergus County. Continue straight onto MT-19 N. Continue onto US-191 N. Continue on MT-66 N. Take Cow Island Trail to Ervine Ridge Rd in Blaine County.

From Great Falls (148 miles): Take 4th St N and River Dr N to US-87 N. Turn left onto US-87 N. Continue on State Hwy 236/Judith Landing Rd. Take Cow Island Trail to Ervine Ridge Rd in Blaine County.

Overview

Upper Missouri River Breaks National Monument protects about 377,000 acres of wild country in north-central Montana, including 149 miles of the Wild and Scenic Missouri River. The monument was designated in 2001 and is one of the few places where the landscape looks much as it did when Lewis and Clark passed through in 1805. Sandstone cliffs, white-walled canyons, badlands and prairie surround the river, and the area holds wildlife, homestead ruins and historic sites. Visitors come for multi-day canoe and kayak trips, scenic drives, fishing, hunting and solitude.

Access

The monument stretches from Fort Benton downstream to the Charles M. Russell National Wildlife Refuge, with boat launches at Coal Banks Landing, Judith Landing and others. Most visitors reach it by driving to Fort Benton, about 45 miles northeast of Great Falls, or to Lewistown and Winifred in the south. Many roads are gravel or dirt and become impassable when wet. Services are very limited, and river trips often use shuttles from Fort Benton or Virgelle.

Seasons

The river is best floated from late May through September, with warm days and cooler nights. Spring brings high water and wildflowers, and fall offers cool weather, golden cottonwoods and fewer people. Summer can be hot, with strong sun and sudden storms. Roads are slick and often closed after rain, snow or thaw, and winter access is limited. Bring plenty of water and plan for mosquitoes.

Things to Do

Float the Wild and Scenic Missouri River

Upper Missouri River Breaks National Monument is best experienced by canoe, kayak or raft along a stretch that has barely changed. Trips range from a day to a week.

See the White Cliffs

The river passes towering white sandstone cliffs carved into hoodoos and spires. This is the most scenic section of the float.

Follow Lewis and Clark's Route

Campsites and landmarks mentioned in the explorers' journals can be found along the river. Interpretive signs describe their travels.

Visit Fort Benton

The historic town has the Missouri Breaks Interpretive Center, the Grand Union Hotel and old riverfront buildings. It is the gateway to the monument.

Watch Wildlife and Stars

Elk, bighorn sheep, eagles and prairie birds live in the breaks. The night sky is clear and dark.

Top Trails

Coal Banks to Judith Landing Float

Length: Approximately 46 miles one way
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

This popular 3- to 4-day float passes the White Cliffs and historic sites with calm water. Plan for camping and a shuttle.

Eagle Creek and Hole-in-the-Wall Hikes

Length: Varies; short hikes from river camps
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

Informal hikes from river campsites lead to side canyons and rock formations. They are best done with a map and care in the heat.

Visitor Centers

The Missouri Breaks Interpretive Center in Fort Benton is the main visitor facility, with exhibits on the monument and the river, a theater, a store and staff who provide maps and permits. The Bureau of Land Management also has a field office in Lewistown, and outfitters in Fort Benton and Virgelle provide shuttles and rentals. Check the BLM website for hours and river conditions.

Camping and Lodging

Primitive camping is available at river campsites and boat landings, with limited facilities, and visitors must carry out waste. Fort Benton has the historic Grand Union Hotel and several motels, and Great Falls, about 45 minutes southwest, has a full range of chain hotels. Lewistown, on the southern side, has lodging such as the Yogo Inn, and Havre offers more options. Virgelle has a small bed-and-breakfast. Check each property's website for current availability.
