How to Get There

Overview

Shore Acres State Park is famous for its formal botanical gardens set on a dramatic sandstone headland above the Pacific, once the estate of timber baron Louis Simpson. The gardens include a Japanese garden, a rose garden, a pond and seasonal displays of tulips, rhododendrons and dahlias, with an observation building overlooking crashing waves. The park is one of the best places in Oregon for storm watching, as winter swells burst against the cliffs, and in December the gardens are lit for a popular holiday light show. Visitors come for the gardens, the surf, the trails and views near Charleston.

Access

The park is on the Cape Arago Highway, about 13 miles southwest of Coos Bay and North Bend. The route is paved and well signed, and the drive from Coos Bay takes about 25 minutes. Most visitors drive, and the park has a large parking lot near the gardens and observation building. It is about 2.5 hours from Eugene and 4.5 hours from Portland.

Seasons

The park is open year-round, and each season offers something different. Spring and summer bring colorful gardens, from daffodils and tulips to roses and dahlias. Fall has chrysanthemums and fewer crowds. Winter is the dramatic season, with storm watching in the observation building and the Holiday Lights display in December. Waves can be dangerous, so stay behind the rails and off wet rocks.

Things to Do

Stroll the Formal Gardens

Shore Acres State Park's gardens are a highlight, with flowering beds, a lily pond and a Japanese garden. Something is in bloom in most seasons.

Watch Winter Storm Waves

The headland is a leading storm-watching spot, where waves crash into the cliffs and send spray high. The glass-enclosed observation building provides a warm and safe spot.

See the Holiday Lights

In December, hundreds of thousands of lights decorate the gardens. The display is a regional tradition and draws large crowds.

Explore Simpson Beach

A trail leads to a sheltered cove with a beach and tidepools. It is a quiet escape.

Hike the Oregon Coast Trail

The trail connects Shore Acres with Sunset Bay and Cape Arago. It runs along cliffs with views.

Top Trails

Shore Acres Garden Loop

Length: Approximately 0.5 miles loop
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A paved path winds through the formal gardens and past the pond and Japanese garden. It is accessible for most visitors.

Simpson Beach Trail

Length: Approximately 0.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 100 feet
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

A short trail descends to a protected beach and cove. The climb back is moderate.

Oregon Coast Trail to Sunset Bay

Length: Approximately 1 mile one way
Elevation Gain: Moderate
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

This trail follows the cliffs between the two parks, with views of the sea. Watch for slippery sections.

Visitor Centers

The park's observation building and garden entrance provide the main visitor services, with a gift shop, restrooms and information about blooms and events. The site is staffed during open hours, and the Oregon State Parks website lists current events, including the holiday lights. The Charleston and Coos Bay visitor centers can supply regional maps and advice.

Camping and Lodging

There is no camping at Shore Acres, but Sunset Bay State Park, 3 miles away, has a large campground with yurts, and Bastendorff Beach County Park offers camping near Charleston. Charleston, 10 minutes away, has inns and waterfront lodging, and Coos Bay and North Bend offer chain hotels and the Mill Casino Hotel. Bandon, about an hour south, has resorts and inns. Check each property's website for current availability.
