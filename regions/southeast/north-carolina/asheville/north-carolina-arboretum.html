North Carolina Arboretum

★5.0
  1. Southeast
  2. North Carolina
  3. Asheville
  4. North Carolina Arboretum

JST Tx, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

Expansive public garden and nature preserve featuring cultivated botanical collections, forest trails, rotating exhibits, and one of the region’s premier bonsai displays.

General Information
Hours:
April to October: 8am to 9pm daily
November to March: 8am to 7pm daily
Closed Christmas Day
Entrance gate closes one hour before property closing time
Fees:
Personal vehicle parking: $25
Large vehicles (21–29 ft): $60
Buses and oversized vehicles (30+ ft): $125
Admission to gardens and facilities is otherwise free except for special ticketed events
Pet Policy:
Leashed pets are welcome on the grounds and trails but are not permitted inside buildings or the Bonsai Exhibition Garden
Closest cities with hotels:
Asheville, NC
Seasons:
All year; spring for blooming gardens, summer for lush mountain scenery, fall for vibrant foliage, and winter for seasonal displays
Location:
100 Frederick Law Olmsted Way, Asheville, NC 28806
Website:
ncarboretum.org

How to Get There

From Downtown Asheville (8 mi): Take I-240 W to I-26 E, continue to Exit 33 for NC-191/Brevard Road, then follow signs to the Blue Ridge Parkway access road and the entrance to the North Carolina Arboretum.

Overview

The North Carolina Arboretum is a gateway to the Southern Appalachians, where cultivated gardens blend into native forest across hundreds of acres. Its nationally recognized Bonsai Exhibition Garden, seasonal plantings, and art installations are the main draws, and more than ten miles of hiking and biking trails connect to the Bent Creek Experimental Forest.

Spring blooms and autumn color are especially good; interactive programs and seasonal events add options for families.
