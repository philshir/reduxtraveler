Fort Pulaski National Monument

★5.0
  1. Southeast
  2. Georgia
  3. Savannah
  4. Fort Pulaski National Monument

National monument preserves fort located on Cockspur Island between Savannah and Tybee Island

General Information

Hours:
Fort Pulaski National Monument is open year round from 9am to 5pm, except for New Years, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Days.
The Visitor Center is open from 9am to 5pm and historic Fort Pulaski is open 9am to 4:45pm
Fees:
Per Person: $10 valid for 7 consecutive days. Youth 15 and under are admitted free of charge
Accepts America The Beautiful Pass
Pet Policy:
Pets allowed
Seasons:
All year. Weather is hot and humid during the summer

How to Get There

Head east on E Bay St toward E Upper Factors Walk. Continue onto General McIntosh Blvd. Use the left 2 lanes to turn left onto E President St. Continue straight to stay on E President St. Continue onto Islands Expy. Keep left at the fork, follow signs for US-80 E and merge onto US-80 E/Islands Expy.

Overview

Fort Pulaski National Monument sits on Cockspur Island between Savannah and Tybee Island, guarding the mouth of the Savannah River. Construction began in 1829, with a young Second Lieutenant Robert E. Lee among the engineers, and the fort was named for Casimir Pulaski, the Polish cavalry commander of the American Revolution. In 1862 Union forces used rifled cannon to breach its walls in a single day, a test that made brick forts obsolete. The fort later served as a prisoner-of-war camp. It was set aside as a national monument in 1924 and is now managed by the National Park Service, which preserves one of the nearly 30 Third System coastal fortifications still standing along the Atlantic and Gulf coasts. The monument covers most of Cockspur Island and all of neighboring McQueens Island, and visitors come for the fort, its Civil War history and the surrounding salt marsh.

Access

The monument is on US-80 East, about 15 miles east of downtown Savannah, on the road to Tybee Island, and the drive takes around 20 minutes. Most visitors arrive by car, and the Savannah/Hilton Head International Airport is about 30 minutes away. Cockspur Island is reached by a short bridge across the marsh, and parking is available near the Visitor Center. The fort is across the moat from the entrance, so expect a short walk on uneven surfaces, with some exhibit areas that are not fully accessible.

Seasons

Spring and fall are the most comfortable seasons, with mild temperatures that make exploring the fort and walking the marsh trails pleasant. Summers are hot and humid, with strong sun on the open fort grounds and plenty of gnats and mosquitoes near the marsh, so bring water and repellent. Winters are mild, and winter mornings can be cool and quiet. Hurricane season runs June through November, and storms or high tides can affect the island and its low-lying trails. The monument is open year-round except for a few holidays.

Things to Do

Tour the Fort
Cross the drawbridge and walk the casemates, parade ground, moat, and ramparts, with exhibits on military life, engineering, and the 1862 siege. Self-guided tours let you set your own pace.

Learn the Civil War Story
Exhibits and programs cover the siege, the Union and Confederate forces, prisoners of war, and enslaved people who sought freedom during the conflict.

Bike or Walk the Marsh Trails
Causeways and trails cross salt marshes and tidal creeks with wide views of the surrounding waterways.

Visitor Centers

The Visitor Center near the parking area is the place to start. It offers exhibits on the fort's construction, the 1862 siege and Civil War history, and park rangers can provide maps and information on guided programs and living history demonstrations. Hours are 9am to 5pm, while the fort itself closes at 4:45pm, so allow at least two hours to see both.

Camping and Lodging

There is no camping or lodging within the monument. Hotels, inns and vacation rentals are plentiful in Savannah, about 15 miles away, and especially in its Historic District. Tybee Island, just a few miles east, offers beach hotels, motels and rentals, and Pooler has chain hotels near I-95. For camping, Skidaway Island State Park near Savannah and Fort McAllister State Park near Richmond Hill both have campgrounds within about 30 to 45 minutes. Hilton Head Island, South Carolina, is about an hour away.
