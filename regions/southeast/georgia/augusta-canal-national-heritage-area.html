Augusta Canal National Heritage Area

How to Get There

From Augusta: Travel to downtown Augusta via Broad Street or Greene Street and continue to the Augusta Canal Discovery Center at Enterprise Mill, located at 1450 Greene Street along the canal.

Overview

Augusta Canal National Heritage Area protects a still-working canal dug in 1845 to harness the Savannah River to power textile mills in Augusta. The canal once drove Enterprise Mill, Sibley Mill and other factories, and it helped Augusta become a leading cotton-manufacturing city of the South. Today a National Historic Landmark and National Heritage Area, it offers a towpath for walking and biking, canal boat tours, paddling, wildlife watching and the Augusta Canal Discovery Center in the restored Enterprise Mill.

Access

The Discovery Center is at 1450 Greene Street in downtown Augusta, and the towpath can be reached from several trailheads, including the Headgates near Evans to Locks Road and Lake Olmstead. Visitors arrive by I-20 and I-520, and parking is free at most access points. Augusta Transit buses run to downtown, and rental bikes are in the city.

Seasons

The area is open all year, and the towpath is open from dawn to dusk. Spring and fall bring the most comfortable temperatures and the best views of wildflowers and birds. Summers are hot and humid, so walk early or take a boat tour. Winters are mild with occasional cold snaps. The Masters Tournament in April fills hotels.

Things to Do

Tour the Discovery Center

Exhibits in Enterprise Mill describe how the canal worked and what life was like for mill workers.

Ride a Canal Boat

Guided tours aboard a replica petersburg boat explain the canal history and wildlife.

Walk or Bike the Towpath

A nearly level path runs beside the canal for miles, passing mills, locks and the river.

Paddle the Canal

Canoe and kayak rentals provide a quiet way to see herons, turtles and the old mill buildings.

Visit Riverwalk Augusta

The riverfront park in downtown links shops, restaurants and the Savannah River.

Top Trails

Augusta Canal Towpath

Length: Approximately 7 miles one way
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A wide, flat trail follows the canal from downtown to the Headgates and Lock, passing mills and wetlands.

Savannah Rapids Pavilion Loop

Length: Approximately 1.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Minimal
Difficulty: Easy

A short walk beside the canal and river near the Headgates, with views of rapids and the historic lock.

Visitor Centers

The Augusta Canal Discovery Center at Enterprise Mill is open Tuesday through Saturday from 10 a.m. to 5 p.m. It has exhibits, a theater, a gift shop and ticket sales for canal boat tours. Admission to the center is free. The Savannah Rapids Pavilion near the Headgates is another information point.

Camping and Lodging

There is no camping or lodging within the heritage area. Camping is available at Mistletoe State Park, about 30 minutes northwest, and at Hamburg State Park and Richard B. Russell State Park. Hotels are in Augusta and Evans. Aiken, South Carolina, is about 30 minutes east, and Atlanta is about 2.5 hours west.
