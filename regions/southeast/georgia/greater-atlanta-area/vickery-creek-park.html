Vickery Creek Park

★5.0

How to Get There

From Atlanta, GA (22 mi): Take GA-400 N to exit 7B for Holcomb Bridge Road. Continue west, then turn right onto Mill Street in downtown Roswell. The park entrance is at the end of Mill Street.

Overview

Vickery Creek Park is a wooded riverside park in Roswell and a unit of the Chattahoochee River National Recreation Area. Trails follow Vickery Creek past the stone ruins of the 1850s Roswell Manufacturing Company, over an old covered bridge, and to a man-made waterfall created by a historic dam. Signs describe the industrial and Civil War past, including the 1864 seizure of the mill workers. The park is a quiet escape just north of Atlanta.

Access

The main trailhead is at 95 Mill Street in downtown Roswell, about 20 miles north of Atlanta. Visitors drive on GA-9 or GA-400 and Atlanta Street, and parking is free but fills quickly on weekends. There are also entrances at Allenbrook and from the Chattahoochee River Park. A MARTA bus connects some days.

Seasons

The park is open daily from one hour before sunrise to one hour after sunset. Spring and fall bring the best weather, wildflowers and fall color. Summers are hot, humid and crowded with insects. Winters are mild. Trails can be muddy after rain, and the water access near the falls is closed to protect the stream.

Things to Do

Walk to the Waterfall

A short trail leads to the dam and the man-made waterfall on Vickery Creek.

See the Mill Ruins

Stone walls of the Roswell Manufacturing Company are visible along the creek.

Cross the Covered Bridge

A wooden bridge over the creek is a favorite photo stop.

Read the Interpretive Signs

Panels cover the mill, its workers and Civil War events in Roswell.

Explore Historic Roswell

Roswell Mill, Bulloch Hall and Canton Street shops are a short walk or drive away.

Top Trails

Vickery Creek Falls Trail

Length: Approximately 1.5 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 150 feet
Difficulty: Easy to Moderate

The shortest route to the dam and waterfall, with stone stairs and a bridge.

Vickery Creek Loop

Length: Approximately 4 miles round trip
Elevation Gain: Approximately 400 feet
Difficulty: Moderate

A longer loop along the creek and the ridge, passing the mill ruins and the covered bridge.

Visitor Centers

There is no staffed visitor center at the park. Maps and information are available from the City of Roswell's Visitors Center on Canton Street and from the National Park Service at the Chattahoochee River National Recreation Area headquarters. Restrooms are near the main parking area.

Camping and Lodging

There is no camping or lodging in the park. Camping is available at Don Carter State Park on Lake Lanier and Red Top Mountain State Park, each about an hour away. Hotels, inns and bed-and-breakfasts are in Roswell, Alpharetta and Sandy Springs. Atlanta has the widest choice of lodging.
