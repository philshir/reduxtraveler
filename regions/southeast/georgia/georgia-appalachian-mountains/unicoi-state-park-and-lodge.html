How to Get There

From Helen, GA (2 mi): Take GA-75 N briefly, then turn right onto GA-356 E and continue for 1.5 miles to the park entrance on the left.

Overview

Unicoi State Park & Lodge is a Georgia state park and full-service lodge in the Blue Ridge foothills, set within the Chattahoochee National Forest just outside the Alpine-themed town of Helen. The roughly 1,000-acre park surrounds 53-acre Unicoi Lake and combines a lodge and conference center with a wide range of outdoor recreation. It is best known for its adventure programs, including ziplining, aerial challenge courses and archery, along with mountain biking, a sandy lake beach and boat rentals. The park is also a convenient gateway to Anna Ruby Falls and the attractions of Helen, and seasonal events such as chili cook-offs and craft festivals draw crowds from across the region.

Access

Unicoi sits on GA-356 about 2 miles northeast of downtown Helen, in White County in the north Georgia mountains. Most visitors drive from Atlanta, a trip of roughly 1.5 to 2 hours depending on traffic, typically via Gainesville and Cleveland on US-129 and GA-75 before turning onto GA-356. The mountain roads are winding, and traffic into Helen can be slow on fall weekends. A daily ParkPass is required for day visitors, and the park is open daily until evening, with lodge guests able to come and go as they need.

Seasons

Spring brings dogwoods, wildflowers and full streams, and the weather is cool and comfortable for hiking and biking. Summer is the busiest season for the lake beach, boat rentals and adventure programs, and temperatures are generally milder than in Atlanta. Fall is the most popular time to visit, with peak leaf color usually arriving in mid to late October at the same time as Helen's Oktoberfest, so lodging and weekend traffic fill up early. Winters are cold, with occasional snow or ice, and the park is quieter; some seasonal activities and lake facilities may be limited or closed, so check the schedule before you go.

Things to Do

Try the Adventure Programs
Zipline, take on aerial challenge courses, or shoot archery with guided outdoor experiences for all ages.

Get on Unicoi Lake
Rent a kayak, canoe, or paddleboard, or swim at the sandy beach.

Visit Anna Ruby Falls
The park is a gateway to these twin waterfalls, reached by a paved trail in the national forest.

Walk or Bike to Helen
Trails connect the park to downtown Helen and Hardman Farm Historic Site.

Visitor Centers

The park's visitor center is the place to pick up trail maps and information on programs, events and current conditions. In nearby Helen, the town welcome center offers brochures and local advice, and the U.S. Forest Service runs a visitor center at Anna Ruby Falls, a short drive from the park, where a separate entrance fee applies.

Camping and Lodging

Unicoi offers a range of overnight options inside the park, including lakeside lodge rooms, cabins, glamping safari tents and a campground. Helen, just 2 miles away, has the largest selection of hotels, inns and vacation cabins in the area, and Cleveland and Clarkesville have additional motels and bed and breakfasts within about 20 to 30 minutes. Dahlonega, roughly an hour away, offers more inns and hotels. For other camping, Vogel State Park and Moccasin Creek State Park, both within about an hour, have campgrounds, and the Chattahoochee National Forest has additional campgrounds in the surrounding mountains.
