Ben Lomond Historic Site

★5.0
  1. Southeast
  2. Virginia
  3. Ben Lomond Historic Site

Doug Coldwell, CC BY-SA 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

Restored nineteenth-century plantation home, Civil War hospital site, and historic farm complex featuring original outbuildings, landscaped grounds, and exhibits interpreting wartime and agricultural history.

General Information
Hours:
Friday to Monday: 10am to 4pm
Guided tours offered at 11am and 2pm
Grounds open daily from dawn to dusk
Fees:
Tours: $5 per person
Children under 6: Free
Pet Policy:
Pets are allowed on the grounds when leashed; pets are not permitted inside historic buildings
Closest cities with hotels:
Manassas, Virginia (approximately 3 mi)
Seasons:
Spring through fall are ideal for visiting the gardens and grounds, though the site is open year-round
Location:
10321 Sudley Manor Drive, Manassas, VA 20109
Website:
pwcva.gov/department/historic-preservation/ben-lomond-historic-site

How to Get There

From Manassas (3 mi): Take Sudley Road north from central Manassas, continue toward Sudley Manor Drive, and follow signs to the historic site at 10321 Sudley Manor Drive.

Overview

Ben Lomond Historic Site is a remarkably intact plantation complex from 1832, built for Benjamin Tasker Chinn, with the main house, slave quarters, smokehouse, dairy, rose garden, and landscaped grounds. After the First Battle of Manassas it served as a Confederate field hospital, and soldiers' signatures and inscriptions are still visible on the walls.

Guided tours cover the agricultural, social, and military history of the property, including the enslaved community that lived and worked here.
