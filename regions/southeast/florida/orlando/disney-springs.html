How to Get There

From Lake Buena Vista (1 mi): Follow Buena Vista Drive and the posted Disney Springs signs to the entrance, then proceed to one of the complimentary parking garages or surface lots.

Overview

Disney Springs is Walt Disney World's outdoor shopping, dining, and entertainment district in Lake Buena Vista, split into four areas — Marketplace, The Landing, Town Center, and West Side — and connected to the rest of the resort by Disney Transport buses and water taxis.

Areas

Town Center: A Spanish Revival-style streetscape, complete with the artificial "springs" that give the district its name, lined with well-known retailers. It opened in 2016 on former parking lots.

West Side: Home to AMC Disney Springs 24 and a mix of dining and entertainment venues styled after a 1950s exposition district.

Getting There & Parking

Three parking garages — Orange, Lime, and Grapefruit — and four surface lots serve Disney Springs, with a direct exit from westbound I-4 into the Orange Garage. Ferries connect Disney Springs to the Saratoga Springs, Old Key West, and Port Orleans resorts.
