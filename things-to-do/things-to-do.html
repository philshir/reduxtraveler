Things to Do

Golden Gate Bridge from Baker Beach, San Francisco, CA
Christian Mehlführer, User:Chmehl, CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons

The most popular vacation excursions in the United States span everything from world-class skiing, snowboarding, and scuba diving to skydiving, ziplining, food tours, wildlife encounters, hiking, kayaking, rafting, sailing, whale watching, helicopter tours, and scenic train rides, offering unforgettable experiences for travelers seeking adventure, culture, relaxation, and exploration.

Airboat

★ 5.0

High-speed boating adventure where participants travel across shallow marshes, wetlands, rivers, and waterways aboard a flat-bottom vessel propelled by a large aircraft-style propeller.

ATV

★ 5.0

Off-road motorized recreation where riders operate four-wheeled vehicles across trails, dunes, forests, deserts, and other rugged terrain.

Aurora Chasing

★ 5.0

Observation-focused travel activity where participants seek clear night skies to witness colorful natural light displays produced by solar particles interacting with Earth's atmosphere.

Autocycle

★ 5.0

Three-wheeled motorized vehicle that combines elements of motorcycles and cars, offering a unique driving experience with open-air seating and enhanced maneuverability.

Autocycle

★ 5.0

Three-wheeled motorcycle-style touring activity that combines open-air riding with enhanced stability and comfort for recreational road travel and sightseeing.

Big Wall Climbing

★ 5.0

Technical climbing adventure involving multi-pitch ascents of massive vertical rock faces that require specialized equipment, endurance, and advanced rope systems.

Boating

★ 5.0

Technical climbing adventure involving multi-pitch ascents of massive vertical rock faces that require specialized equipment, endurance, and advanced rope systems.

Boogie Boarding

★ 5.0

Wave-riding beach activity where participants lie or kneel on a small board and use breaking surf to glide toward shore.

Bungie Jumping

★ 5.0

Adventure activity where participants leap from a fixed platform while attached to an elastic cord that safely rebounds them before reaching the ground or water below.

Cliff Jumping

★ 5.0

Adventure activity where participants jump from natural rock ledges into deep bodies of water such as lakes, rivers, quarries, or ocean coves below.

Construction Equipment Operation

★ 5.0

Hands-on heavy machinery experience where participants learn to operate large earthmoving and construction vehicles in controlled outdoor environments.

Cross Country Skiing

★ 5.0

Winter snow sport where participants travel across groomed trails or natural snowy terrain using narrow skis and their own physical endurance for propulsion.

Dirt Bike

★ 5.0

Off-road motorcycling activity where riders navigate trails, tracks, deserts, forests, and rugged terrain using lightweight motorcycles designed for unpaved surfaces.

Dog Sledding

★ 5.0

Winter travel activity where teams of specially trained dogs pull a sled across snow-covered landscapes, trails, and wilderness areas.

Dolphin Experience

★ 5.0

Wildlife-focused marine activity where participants observe, learn about, and sometimes interact with dolphins under supervised and regulated conditions.

Dune Riding

★ 5.0

Off-road recreation activity where participants drive specially equipped vehicles across sand dunes and open desert landscapes for adventure and sightseeing.

E-Bike

★ 5.0

Electric-powered cycling activity that uses a battery-assisted bicycle to make riding easier while exploring roads, trails, parks, and scenic destinations.

eFoil

★ 5.0

Electric-powered hydrofoil board sport where riders glide silently above the water’s surface using a handheld throttle and submerged lifting wing.

Electric Unicycle

★ 5.0

Single-wheel electric personal mobility activity where riders balance and steer using body movements while traveling on a self-balancing motorized wheel.

Exotic Car Driving

★ 5.0

High-performance driving experience that allows participants to operate luxury sports cars and supercars on racetracks, driving courses, or guided road routes.

Fishing

★ 5.0

Outdoor recreational activity where participants catch fish in freshwater or saltwater environments using rods, reels, lines, nets, or other legal angling methods.

Flightseeing

★ 5.0

Scenic aerial sightseeing activity where passengers observe natural landmarks, cities, coastlines, mountains, and other attractions from an aircraft.

Float Trips

★ 5.0

Leisurely river recreation where participants float downstream on natural waterways using tubes, rafts, kayaks, or canoes while enjoying scenic surroundings.

Flyboarding

★ 5.0

Water-propelled board sport where participants are lifted above the water by powerful jets that allow hovering, diving, and aerial maneuvers.

Glass Bottom Boats

★ 5.0

Sightseeing water excursion where passengers observe underwater marine environments through transparent viewing panels built into specially designed vessels.

Gold Mining and Panning

★ 5.0

Recreational prospecting activity where participants search riverbeds, streams, and mineral deposits for small quantities of precious metal using simple hand tools and gravity separation methods.

Hang Gliding

★ 5.0

Aerial adventure sport where participants launch a lightweight glider from elevated terrain and soar through the air using wind currents and pilot-controlled flight techniques.

Horseback Riding

★ 5.0

Outdoor recreational activity where participants ride and control horses along trails, open landscapes, ranches, or designated riding areas while enjoying nature and learning equestrian skills.

Hot Air Balloon

★ 5.0

Aerial sightseeing activity where participants travel through the sky in a large balloon lifted by heated air and guided by prevailing winds.

Hunting

★ 5.0

Outdoor pursuit where participants track and harvest legally permitted wildlife using approved methods while following conservation regulations and seasonal guidelines.

Hydrofoiling

★ 5.0

Water sport where participants ride above the surface on a specialized board lifted by an underwater wing, creating a sensation of gliding across the water with minimal resistance.

Ice Caves

★ 5.0

Natural ice exploration activity where participants enter or view frozen cave formations created by snow, ice, and seasonal freezing conditions in mountainous environments.

Ice Climbing

★ 5.0

Vertical climbing activity where participants ascend frozen waterfalls, ice-covered rock faces, and other natural ice formations using specialized climbing techniques and equipment.

Jetskiing

★ 5.0

High-speed water recreation activity where participants ride a personal watercraft across lakes, rivers, bays, and coastal waters for sightseeing, exploration, and excitement.

Kayaking

★ 5.0

Paddling activity where participants propel a small, narrow watercraft through lakes, rivers, bays, and coastal waters using a double-bladed paddle.

Kite Boarding

★ 5.0

Wind-powered water sport where participants ride a board across the surface while being pulled by a controllable kite that generates speed, lift, and maneuverability.

Manatee Snorkeling

★ 5.0

Wildlife viewing activity where participants swim at the surface in clear spring waters to observe gentle aquatic mammals in their natural habitat.

Mountain Biking

★ 5.0

Off-road cycling activity where participants ride specially designed bicycles over natural terrain, including dirt trails, forest paths, hills, and mountainous landscapes.

Onewheel

★ 5.0

Electric personal mobility activity where participants ride a self-balancing board with a single large wheel to travel on paved paths, streets, and off-road trails.

Paddleboard

★ 5.0

Water recreation activity where participants stand or kneel on a large board and propel themselves across lakes, rivers, bays, and coastal waters using a single-bladed paddle.

Paintball

★ 5.0

Strategic outdoor or indoor team competition where players use compressed-air markers to tag opponents with washable paint-filled projectiles in objective-based games.

Paragliding

★ 5.0

Foot-launched aerial adventure that uses a fabric wing and air currents to glide through the sky while enjoying panoramic views and a sensation of free flight.

Parasailing

★ 5.0

Aerial recreational experience where participants are lifted behind a boat beneath a specially designed canopy to enjoy elevated views over open water.

Party Bus

★ 5.0

Mobile group transportation experience designed for socializing, celebrations, and entertainment while traveling between destinations in a customized vehicle.

Pedal Taverns

★ 5.0

Group-powered sightseeing and social experience where participants collectively pedal a multi-passenger vehicle while visiting entertainment districts, restaurants, and local attractions.

Rappelling

★ 5.0

Controlled descent adventure where participants use ropes and specialized equipment to safely lower themselves down cliffs, rock faces, bridges, or other steep vertical surfaces.

Rock Climbing

★ 5.0

Vertical outdoor adventure that involves ascending natural rock formations or artificial climbing walls using specialized techniques, safety systems, and protective equipment.

Sandboarding

★ 5.0

Desert adventure sport that involves gliding down sand dunes on a specialized board while using balance and control techniques similar to snowboarding.

Schooner

★ 5.0

Traditional sailing experience aboard a multi-masted vessel that combines scenic cruising, maritime history, and wind-powered travel on coastal or inland waterways.

Scoot Coupes

★ 5.0

Traditional sailing experience aboard a multi-masted vessel that combines scenic cruising, maritime history, and wind-powered travel on coastal or inland waterways.

Scooter Rentals

★ 5.0

Convenient personal transportation experience that allows individuals to independently explore cities, waterfronts, parks, and scenic routes using a lightweight motorized two-wheeled vehicle.

Scuba Diving

★ 5.0

Underwater adventure activity that allows participants to breathe beneath the surface using specialized equipment while exploring marine environments, wildlife, and submerged features.

Sea Caves

★ 5.0

Exploration adventure that involves accessing and discovering natural coastal caverns carved by waves, tides, and erosion along ocean shorelines.

Shark Cage

★ 5.0

Thrilling marine wildlife experience that allows participants to observe large predatory fish from inside a protective enclosure while remaining submerged in their natural habitat.

Shooting Range

★ 5.0

Recreational target-shooting activity where participants practice accuracy and firearm handling at supervised facilities designed for controlled shooting.

Skydiving

★ 5.0

Extreme aerial experience where participants jump from an aircraft and experience freefall before descending safely using a parachute system.

Snorkeling

★ 5.0

Recreational water activity where participants float and swim at the surface to observe underwater marine life using a mask and snorkel.

Snow Skiing

★ 5.0

Winter mountain sport that involves gliding over snow on specialized skis while descending groomed trails, natural terrain, and alpine slopes.

Snowboarding

★ 5.0

Motorized winter recreation activity that allows participants to travel across snow-covered trails, forests, mountains, and open terrain using a specialized tracked vehicle.

Snowcoach

★ 5.0

Guided over-snow sightseeing experience aboard a large specialized vehicle designed to transport passengers through winter landscapes and snow-covered terrain.

Snowmobile

★ 5.0

Motorized winter recreation activity that allows participants to travel across snow-covered trails, forests, mountains, and open terrain using a specialized tracked vehicle.

Snowshoeing

★ 5.0

Winter outdoor activity that uses specialized footwear to distribute body weight across deep snow, allowing participants to comfortably hike and explore snowy landscapes.

Sports Fishing

★ 5.0

Recreational outdoor activity where participants use rods, reels, bait, or lures to catch fish in freshwater or saltwater environments for sport, relaxation, and challenge.

Surfing

★ 5.0

Ocean and coastal board-riding activity where participants paddle into waves and ride them toward shore using balance, timing, and wave-reading skills.

Underground Kayaking

★ 5.0

Guided paddling adventure through flooded caves, subterranean waterways, or cavern systems using a kayak to explore unique underground natural environments.

Underwater Fish Observation Submarine

★ 5.0

Underwater sightseeing experience where participants ride in a submerged vessel to observe fish, coral reefs, shipwrecks, and other marine environments without getting wet.

UTV

★ 5.0

Off-road motorized adventure where participants drive all-terrain utility vehicles across trails, dunes, forests, deserts, and rugged outdoor terrain.

Wakeboarding

★ 5.0

Water sport where participants ride a board across the surface of the water while being pulled by a motorboat, combining elements of surfing, snowboarding, and waterskiing.

Water Jetpack

★ 5.0

Personal flight-like water adventure where participants are propelled above the water's surface using high-pressure jets connected to a powered watercraft.

Waterskiing

★ 5.0

Tow-behind water sport where participants glide across the water on skis while being pulled by a motorboat, combining speed, balance, and athletic skill.

Whale Watching

★ 5.0

Marine wildlife viewing excursion where participants observe whales and other ocean animals from boats while learning about their behavior, migration, and habitat.

White Water Rafting

★ 5.0

Guided river adventure where participants navigate fast-moving rapids and turbulent water in an inflatable raft while enjoying teamwork, excitement, and scenic landscapes.

Wind Surfing

Wind-powered board sport where participants use a sail attached to a board to glide across the water using a combination of sailing skill, balance, and athletic control.

Yachting

Recreational boating activity focused on operating, cruising, and enjoying large sailboats or motor yachts on coastal waters, lakes, rivers, and open seas.

Zipline

Zorbing
