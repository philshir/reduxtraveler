Top Twenty Best Museums

  1. Best Of
  2. Top Twenty Best Museums

The finest museums in the United States preserve and showcase world-class collections of art, history, science, culture, and innovation, providing unforgettable educational and cultural experiences.

TomasEE, CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#1 Metropolitan Museum of Art

New York City, New York

World's largest art museum, housing more than two million works spanning 5,000 years of history, from Egyptian antiquities to European masterpieces and American art.

#2 Smithsonian National Museum of Natural History

Washington, DC

Home to the Hope Diamond, a life-size African elephant, and one of the largest natural history collections in the world with over 145 million specimens.

Pedro Szekely from Los Angeles, USA, CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#3 Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum

Washington, DC

Displays the Wright brothers' 1903 Flyer and the Apollo 11 command module, making it one of the most visited museums in the world dedicated to flight and space exploration.

The National Gallery of Art, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#4 National Gallery of Art

Washington, DC

Free national art museum spanning the West and East Buildings, with works ranging from the Middle Ages to the present, including the only Leonardo da Vinci painting in the Americas.

ajay_suresh, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#5 Museum of Modern Art (MoMA)

New York City, New York

Influential modern art museum home to Van Gogh's The Starry Night and Warhol's Campbell's Soup Cans, with a pioneering collection of design, photography, and film.

bryan..., CC BY-SA 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#6 American Museum of Natural History

New York City, New York

Upper West Side museum famous for its dinosaur fossil halls, the Hayden Planetarium, and a life-size blue whale suspended in the Milstein Hall of Ocean Life.

GualdimG, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#7 Art Institute of Chicago

Chicago, Illinois

Guarded by iconic bronze lions, this encyclopedic art museum holds one of the finest collections of Impressionist paintings in the world, including Grant Wood's American Gothic.

#8 Getty Center

Los Angeles, California

Hilltop campus of modern architecture and sweeping gardens with free admission, showcasing European paintings, decorative arts, and panoramic views over Los Angeles.

#9 National Museum of African American History and Culture

Washington, DC

Smithsonian museum devoted to documenting African American life, history, and culture, housed in a striking bronze-latticed building on the National Mall.

#10 National September 11 Memorial & Museum

New York City, New York

Twin reflecting pools mark the footprints of the Twin Towers, with an underground museum preserving artifacts and stories from the September 11, 2001 attacks.

David Wilson from Oak Park, Illinois, USA, CC BY 2.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#11 Field Museum

Chicago, Illinois

Natural history museum famous for SUE, the largest and most complete Tyrannosaurus rex skeleton ever found, alongside vast anthropology and earth science exhibits.

#12 Smithsonian National Museum of American History

Washington, DC

Preserves pieces of American heritage from the original Star-Spangled Banner to Dorothy's ruby slippers, chronicling the nation's cultural, political, and technological history.

#13 Museum of Fine Arts Boston

Boston, Massachusetts

One of the largest and most comprehensive art museums in the country, with an encyclopedic collection spanning Egyptian artifacts, Impressionist masterpieces, and Art of the Americas.

Stilfehler, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#14 California Academy of Sciences

San Francisco, California

All-in-one natural history museum, aquarium, planetarium, and rainforest dome topped with a living, undulating green roof in Golden Gate Park.

Jean-Christophe BENOIST, CC BY 3.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#15 Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum

New York City, New York

Frank Lloyd Wright's spiraling, cylindrical building houses a renowned collection of modern and contemporary art along Fifth Avenue's Museum Mile.

#16 Whitney Museum of American Art

New York City, New York

Renzo Piano-designed museum at the base of the High Line dedicated exclusively to 20th and 21st century American art, from Edward Hopper to contemporary voices.

#17 Los Angeles County Museum of Art

Los Angeles, California

The largest art museum in the western United States, famous for Chris Burden's glowing Urban Light sculpture and an encyclopedic collection spanning ancient to contemporary art.

#18 Houston Museum of Natural Science

Houston, Texas

One of the most visited natural science museums in the country, featuring dinosaur halls, a butterfly center, planetarium, and gem and mineral collections.

Charvex, CC0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

#19 Crystal Bridges Museum of American Art

Bentonville, Arkansas

Moshe Safdie-designed museum set among wooded trails and reflecting pools, tracing American art history from Colonial times to today with free general admission.

#20 The Broad

Los Angeles, California

Contemporary art museum with a distinctive honeycomb "veil and vault" exterior, offering free admission to works by Jeff Koons, Yayoi Kusama, and Kara Walker.
