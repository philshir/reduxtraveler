Northern California Volcanoes & Redwoods: Peaks, Waterfalls, Lava Fields, and Ancient Forests

5 Days

Northern California contains some of the most extraordinary landscapes in North America. Snow-capped volcanoes rise above alpine lakes, geothermal features steam from the earth, waterfalls plunge through forested canyons, and the world's tallest trees tower above fern-covered valleys. This journey combines the volcanic wonders of Lassen Volcanic National Park, the dramatic scenery surrounding Mount Shasta, the lava landscapes of Lava Beds National Monument, and the legendary redwood forests along California's North Coast.

Inspired by the storytelling style of National Geographic and the practical organization of a TripAdvisor itinerary, this route groups destinations geographically to reduce driving while maximizing exploration. From active geothermal basins and cinder cones to hidden beaches and ancient redwood groves, this itinerary showcases the very best of Northern California's natural wonders.

Day 1

Your journey begins in Lassen Volcanic National Park, one of America's most unique volcanic landscapes. Boiling springs, steaming fumaroles, colorful lakes, and alpine scenery reveal a landscape still being shaped by powerful geological forces.

1

Begin your exploration among steaming fumaroles, bubbling mudpots, and sulfur vents that reveal the active volcanic system beneath the park. The scent of sulfur and rising steam create a dramatic introduction to Lassen's geothermal landscape.

2

One of the park's most active geothermal areas, Devils Kitchen contains steaming vents and hydrothermal features tucked into a remote mountain valley.

3

The largest hydrothermal area in the park features boiling pools, colorful mineral deposits, and steaming vents spread across a dramatic volcanic basin.

4

Admire the vivid blue-green waters of Emerald Lake while enjoying views of Lassen Peak, the massive volcano that dominates the park skyline.

Day 2

Today explores Lassen's northern region before continuing west to one of California's most spectacular waterfalls and volcanic landmarks.

1

Massive volcanic rock formations and landslide deposits create one of the park's most fascinating geological landscapes. The rugged terrain showcases the dramatic power of past volcanic activity.

2

One of Lassen's most photographed locations, Manzanita Lake reflects Lassen Peak and surrounding forests in calm waters framed by volcanic scenery.

3

A striking volcanic cone rises above colorful lava fields and painted dunes. The climb provides extraordinary views across one of the park's most unusual landscapes.

4

End the day at one of California's most beautiful waterfalls. Burney Falls cascades from volcanic cliffs into a crystal-clear pool, creating a scene often described as magical.

Day 3

The day focuses on volcanic landscapes surrounding Mount Shasta and Lava Beds National Monument, where lava tubes, volcanic fields, and dramatic peaks dominate the scenery.

1

Rising over 14,000 feet above Northern California, Mount Shasta is among the most iconic mountains in the American West. The volcanic peak dominates the landscape for miles in every direction.

2

Explore a landscape shaped by volcanic eruptions. Lava tubes, cinder cones, rugged lava fields, and remote high-desert scenery combine to create one of California's most unusual national monuments.

Day 4

Today marks your arrival in the legendary redwood forests. Massive trees, lush river valleys, and scenic drives showcase some of the finest old-growth redwoods remaining on Earth.

1

One of the most beautiful drives in California winds through cathedral-like groves of towering redwoods. Massive trunks rise hundreds of feet above the forest floor, creating an unforgettable experience.

2

Walk through some of the finest remaining old-growth forest while standing beside enormous redwoods that have survived for centuries.

3

Frequently considered one of the most beautiful redwood groves in the entire park system, Stout Grove features giant trees growing beside the Smith River.

4

This classic redwood hike travels deeper into pristine forest and leads to one of the park's most famous giant trees.

Day 5

The final day explores the coastal portion of Redwood National and State Parks, where lush forests meet rugged beaches, river valleys, and one of California's most famous natural landscapes.

1

Begin the day in the heart of Redwood National Park where Redwood Creek winds through old-growth forests and broad floodplains.

2

This peaceful trail winds through magnificent old-growth redwoods and offers one of the finest introductions to the ancient forest ecosystem.

3

Descend into one of the most famous redwood groves in the world, where some of the tallest trees ever measured continue to inspire visitors.

4

Enjoy sweeping views of forested mountains, elk habitat, and the Klamath River as it flows toward the Pacific Ocean.

5

End your journey in Fern Canyon, one of the most beautiful and unique landscapes in California. Vertical canyon walls covered in lush green ferns create a prehistoric world that feels untouched by time. Nearby Hidden Beach provides a final opportunity to experience the dramatic Northern California coastline.
