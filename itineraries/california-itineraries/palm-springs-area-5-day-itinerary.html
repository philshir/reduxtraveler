Palm Springs & Joshua Tree: Desert Oases, Otherworldly Landscapes, and Mid-Century California

5 Days

Surrounded by rugged mountains, vast deserts, and iconic palm-filled canyons, Palm Springs serves as the gateway to some of California's most extraordinary landscapes. This journey combines the glamour of Palm Springs, the unique ecosystems of Joshua Tree National Park, the engineering marvel of the Coachella Valley wind farms, and some of the American Southwest's most fascinating geological wonders.

Inspired by the storytelling style of National Geographic and the practical organization of a TripAdvisor-style itinerary, this five-day adventure groups destinations by proximity to minimize driving. From soaring above the desert on the Palm Springs Aerial Tramway to hiking among giant boulder fields in Joshua Tree National Park and exploring the eerie shoreline of the Salton Sea, this itinerary highlights the very best of Southern California's desert landscapes.

Day 1

Your desert adventure begins in Palm Springs, where mid-century modern architecture, mountain scenery, and cultural institutions create one of California's most distinctive destinations. Today combines history, art, aviation, and iconic landmarks while introducing you to the unique character of this desert oasis.

1

Begin your journey exploring the heart of Palm Springs. Lined with palm trees and framed by towering mountains, downtown blends luxury resorts, mid-century architecture, outdoor cafés, and a relaxed desert atmosphere that has attracted visitors for generations.

2

Visit two of Palm Springs' most recognizable attractions. The towering Forever Marilyn statue celebrates Hollywood's golden age, while the Walk of the Stars honors influential entertainers, civic leaders, and celebrities associated with the city.

3

Learn about the history, culture, and traditions of the Agua Caliente Band of Cahuilla Indians whose ancestral homeland encompasses the Palm Springs region.

4

Explore world-class collections of art, architecture, photography, and design while learning about the mid-century modern movement that helped define Palm Springs.

5

End the day among one of the finest collections of operational military aircraft in the United States. Historic aircraft and aviation exhibits provide a fascinating glimpse into twentieth-century history.

Day 2

Today focuses on the spectacular natural landscapes surrounding Palm Springs. Experience desert canyons, mountain forests, and some of the most dramatic elevation changes in California.

1

Begin the morning exploring one of the largest natural palm oases in North America. Towering California fan palms line a scenic canyon where water supports an oasis ecosystem within the surrounding desert.

2

Ride the world's largest rotating tramcar from the desert floor into the cooler forests of Mount San Jacinto State Park. The journey climbs thousands of feet while revealing spectacular views of the Coachella Valley.

3

Explore alpine forests, mountain trails, and panoramic viewpoints overlooking Southern California from one of the region's most impressive mountain environments.

Day 3

Today introduces the western section of Joshua Tree National Park where giant boulder formations, historic sites, and classic Joshua tree landscapes create one of America's most distinctive national parks.

1

Begin at Joshua Tree's historic oasis where Indigenous peoples and early settlers relied upon desert springs for survival. The oasis offers an excellent introduction to the park's ecology and history.

2

Walk among massive granite formations within one of the park's most famous landscapes. Surrounded by towering boulders and Joshua trees, the valley feels isolated from the outside world.

3

This scenic trail combines desert wildlife, historic ranching history, and occasional water features beneath spectacular rock formations.

4

Explore remnants of the region's mining history before entering one of the park's most dramatic boulder landscapes, where giant rock formations create a natural maze across the desert.

5

Enjoy one of Southern California's finest overlooks. The viewpoint offers sweeping panoramas across the Coachella Valley, Salton Sea, and distant mountain ranges.

Day 4

Today's route explores the central and eastern regions of Joshua Tree National Park where cactus gardens, remote valleys, mountain panoramas, and historic ranches reveal the park's incredible diversity.

1

One of the park's premier summit hikes, Ryan Mountain rewards visitors with panoramic views of desert basins, mountain ranges, and vast Joshua tree forests.

2

Explore the remnants of pioneer ranching and mining operations that once supported isolated communities within the harsh desert environment.

3

Discover some of Joshua Tree's most iconic formations. Wind and erosion have sculpted remarkable shapes from giant granite boulders scattered throughout the landscape.

4

Travel into the lower Colorado Desert where dense cactus gardens and colorful desert vegetation create a striking contrast to the higher elevations of Joshua Tree.

5

End the day exploring a remote backcountry route that showcases fascinating geological formations and desert scenery away from the park's more heavily visited areas.

Day 5

Your final day explores the southern reaches of Joshua Tree National Park before visiting several of the Coachella Valley's most unique landmarks and geological curiosities.

1

Discover one of the park's most beautiful oasis environments. Natural springs support lush desert vegetation and attract wildlife from throughout the surrounding region.

2

One of Joshua Tree's most rewarding hikes leads to a hidden palm oasis surrounded by rugged desert mountains and canyon scenery.

3

Spend the afternoon enjoying several of the park's scenic drives and lesser-visited regions where expansive desert vistas, mountain views, and classic Joshua tree landscapes stretch toward the horizon.

4

Stop at one of Southern California's most famous roadside attractions where enormous dinosaur sculptures have welcomed travelers along Interstate 10 for decades.

5

The massive wind farms of the San Gorgonio Pass create one of California's most recognizable landscapes and demonstrate the scale of renewable energy production in the region.

6

End your journey at one of California's most fascinating and surreal landscapes. The Salton Sea's vast shoreline, unique history, and striking desert setting create a memorable and thought-provoking finale to the trip.
