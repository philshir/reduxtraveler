Las Vegas & the Southwest: Icons, Entertainment, and Natural Wonders

7 Days

This 7‑day journey showcases the very best of Las Vegas and the surrounding Southwest, blending world‑famous themed casinos, immersive entertainment, iconic landmarks, and breathtaking natural landscapes. From walking through replicas of ancient Rome and Paris to gazing into the depths of the Grand Canyon, this itinerary balances high‑energy city experiences with unforgettable outdoor adventures. Thoughtfully grouped by location, the trip minimizes unnecessary travel while maximizing cultural, scenic, and entertainment value.

Day 1

Your adventure begins in the heart of the Las Vegas Strip, where elegance, spectacle, and world-renowned landmarks set the tone for the week ahead. Today is all about classic Vegas glamour — strolling between legendary resorts, admiring artistic interiors, indulging in luxury shopping, and witnessing iconic attractions that define the city’s identity. From floral installations and dancing fountains to Roman grandeur and Parisian charm, this first day immerses you fully in the magic of Las Vegas while easing you comfortably into the rhythm of the trip. The evening culminates with an unforgettable dining experience at one of the city’s most celebrated restaurants.

1

The Bellagio is one of Las Vegas’s most iconic resorts, inspired by the elegance of an Italian lakeside village. Inside, the Bellagio Conservatory features massive seasonal displays made entirely from fresh flowers and plants, changing several times a year. Outside, the Bellagio Fountains present choreographed water performances set to music and lights, creating one of the most photographed attractions on the Strip.

2

Caesars Palace brings ancient Rome to life through grand columns, statues, and opulent interiors. The resort is home to the Forum Shops, an upscale shopping mall designed like a Roman marketplace with painted sky ceilings and ornate architecture. Walking through Caesars offers an immersive experience that blends luxury retail, history-inspired décor, and classic Vegas glamour.

3

The Cosmopolitan stands out for its modern, artistic design and vibrant atmosphere. Known for contemporary artwork, stylish lounges, and terraces overlooking the Strip, this resort appeals to travelers seeking a chic, urban vibe. Its open-air balconies and prime views of the Bellagio Fountains make it a favorite stop for photos and dining.

4

Paris Las Vegas recreates the charm of the French capital with cobblestone walkways, Parisian-style cafés, and a half‑scale Eiffel Tower. Visitors can stroll beneath replicas of famous landmarks or ride to the top of the tower for panoramic views of the Strip. The resort’s romantic design and lively streetscape make it one of the most recognizable themed hotels in Las Vegas.

5

Las Vegas is a world‑class dining destination, and this day includes a visit to one of its highly acclaimed restaurants. Options range from Michelin‑recommended fine dining to celebrity‑chef steakhouses and scenic dining rooms overlooking the Strip. These restaurants elevate the Vegas experience by combining exceptional cuisine with elegant settings.

Day 2

Today you’ll explore the bold, high‑energy attractions of the North Strip, where modern mega‑resorts and adrenaline‑filled experiences take center stage. From cutting‑edge technology and breathtaking observation decks to nostalgic casino environments, the day offers a dynamic mix of past and future Las Vegas. As daylight fades, the focus shifts to one of the city’s signature experiences — a jaw‑dropping live show that blends artistry, athleticism, and spectacle in classic Vegas style.

1

The Sphere is one of Las Vegas’s newest and most technologically advanced landmarks. Its massive LED exterior displays dynamic visuals visible from miles away, while the interior features immersive audio and visual technology. The Sphere represents the future of live entertainment and has quickly become one of the city’s most talked‑about attractions.

2

Rising high above the Strip, The STRAT offers sweeping views of Las Vegas from its observation deck. For thrill‑seekers, the tower features adrenaline‑pumping rides that extend over the edge, creating a heart‑stopping experience unlike anywhere else. It’s one of the best places to visually grasp the scale of the city.

3

Circus Circus is a classic Las Vegas resort known for its carnival‑style atmosphere. Inside, guests can find midway games, indoor amusement rides, and circus performances. The resort offers a nostalgic glimpse into old‑school Vegas and remains popular with families and travelers seeking a more playful casino experience.

4

Resorts World is a modern mega‑resort offering sleek design, high‑end shopping, and a diverse collection of global dining options. Its contemporary architecture contrasts sharply with older themed casinos, showcasing the evolution of Las Vegas into a modern luxury destination.

5

Standing over 500 feet tall, the High Roller is one of the world’s tallest observation wheels. The slow rotation allows riders to enjoy unobstructed views of the Strip and surrounding desert. Evening rides are especially popular when the city lights illuminate the skyline.

6

Las Vegas is renowned for its live entertainment, and this itinerary includes at least one top‑tier show. Cirque du Soleil productions such as *O*, *KA*, *Mystère*, and *Michael Jackson ONE* combine acrobatics, music, and storytelling. Alternatives like Blue Man Group or Absinthe offer equally memorable theatrical experiences.

Day 3

This day blends iconic photo stops, desert art, and classic downtown Las Vegas energy. Begin with legendary landmarks at the southern edge of the Strip before venturing into the open desert for a striking art installation. The afternoon transitions into South Strip resorts known for bold architecture and immersive themes. As night falls, downtown Las Vegas comes alive with lights, music, and vintage charm, delivering a lively contrast to the modern Strip experiences earlier in the day.

1

This historic roadside sign has welcomed visitors since 1959 and remains one of the most iconic photo stops in the city. Located at the southern end of the Strip, it’s best visited early in the day to avoid crowds and capture clean, classic photos.

2

Located in the desert just south of Las Vegas, Seven Magic Mountains is a large‑scale public art installation featuring brightly colored boulder stacks. The contrast between neon paint and natural desert surroundings creates a striking visual experience and a unique break from city attractions.

3

The Luxor is instantly recognizable by its massive black pyramid shape and sky‑piercing light beam. Inside, ancient Egyptian themes dominate the décor, from hieroglyphic designs to towering statues. The resort provides a dramatic and immersive atmosphere unlike any other on the Strip.

4

Mandalay Bay features a tropical, beach‑inspired theme with expansive interiors and a relaxed vibe. Known for its wave pool and aquarium, the resort offers a calmer contrast to the high‑energy casinos farther north while still maintaining a luxurious feel.

5

Excalibur embraces medieval fantasy with castle towers, knights, and royal imagery. The playful theming makes it one of the most approachable and recognizable resorts, especially for families or first‑time visitors.

6

Located in downtown Las Vegas, Fremont Street showcases the city’s original casino culture. A massive LED canopy spans the street, hosting nightly light shows synchronized to music. The pedestrian‑only area is filled with live performers, historic casinos, and high‑energy nightlife.

6

SlotZilla allows visitors to zipline directly over Fremont Street, offering a bird’s‑eye view of the crowds and LED canopy below. It’s one of the most thrilling ways to experience downtown Las Vegas and adds an adventurous element to the evening.

Day 4

Today is a journey through recreated worlds and real history. You’ll begin the day surrounded by Old‑World European elegance before diving into Las Vegas’s showbiz history and glamorous past. The afternoon explores cultural attractions off the Strip, followed by an evening among skyline replicas and modern entertainment hubs. This day perfectly captures how Las Vegas blends fantasy, history, and global influence into one unforgettable experience.

1

The Venetian recreates the romantic atmosphere of Venice with indoor canals, singing gondoliers, and ornate Italian architecture. The resort’s grand design makes it one of the most immersive themed casinos in Las Vegas.

2

A gondola ride through the Venetian’s canals offers a peaceful contrast to the bustling casino floor. Riders glide beneath bridges and painted skies while gondoliers sing traditional Italian songs, creating a uniquely romantic Vegas experience.

3

Located inside the Venetian complex, Madame Tussauds features lifelike wax figures of celebrities, historical icons, and entertainment stars. Interactive exhibits allow visitors to pose for photos and explore pop culture moments.

4

Treasure Island combines pirate‑inspired theming with modern entertainment. Its central location and lively atmosphere make it an easy stop while exploring the Strip.

5

The Mob Museum offers a deep dive into the history of organized crime in America and its influence on Las Vegas. Interactive exhibits, historical artifacts, and immersive storytelling provide a fascinating look at law enforcement, crime, and culture.

6

New York‑New York recreates the Manhattan skyline with scaled replicas of famous buildings. Visitors can walk through city‑themed streets or ride the Big Apple Coaster, which loops around the exterior of the resort.

7

Park MGM features a sleek, nature‑inspired design with emphasis on entertainment and dining. Its central location near other major resorts makes it a convenient and stylish stop on the Strip.
