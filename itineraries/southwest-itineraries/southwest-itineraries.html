Southwest travel itineraries showcase a remarkable blend of red-rock canyons, high mountain ranges, desert landscapes, vibrant cultural traditions, and tropical island escapes. From the iconic national parks of Arizona and Utah to Colorado’s alpine scenery, New Mexico’s rich heritage, Nevada’s dramatic desert destinations, and Hawaii’s volcanic landscapes and beaches, these journeys highlight some of America’s most breathtaking natural wonders and distinctive regional experiences.

Overview

The Southwest covers Arizona, Colorado, Nevada, New Mexico, and Utah, plus Hawaii in these itineraries. Red rock canyons, desert landscapes, and Rocky Mountain peaks make this one of the most scenic regions in the country.

Utah's Zion, Bryce Canyon, Capitol Reef, Arches, and Canyonlands, Arizona's Grand Canyon, and Colorado's Rocky Mountain and Mesa Verde are popular park destinations, while Santa Fe, Taos, Sedona, and Las Vegas offer culture and entertainment. In Hawaii, Haleakala and Hawaii Volcanoes National Parks showcase volcanic landscapes.

Summers are extremely hot in desert areas such as Phoenix, so spring and fall are usually the best times to visit. Mountain roads in Colorado close in winter, and some parks may require timed entry or permits during busy periods, so check current requirements before you go.
