Sedona, Arizona: Red Rock Canyons, Sacred Landscapes, and Desert Adventures

5 Days

Surrounded by towering sandstone formations that glow crimson at sunrise and sunset, Sedona is one of the most beautiful destinations in the American Southwest. Red rock cliffs rise above desert valleys, hidden caves overlook ancient landscapes, and scenic trails wind through canyons carved over millions of years. Beyond its famous scenery, Sedona offers a unique blend of outdoor adventure, artistic culture, and spiritual tranquility.

This five-day itinerary combines Sedona's most iconic hikes, scenic overlooks, natural landmarks, and cultural attractions while grouping destinations by location to minimize driving. From the famous summit views of Cathedral Rock and Devil's Bridge to hidden caves, remote overlooks, and the charming streets of Tlaquepaque, this journey showcases the very best of Red Rock Country.

Day 1

Your adventure begins in the heart of Sedona. Today's itinerary focuses on the town's most famous landmarks and scenic overlooks, providing an ideal introduction to the breathtaking red-rock landscapes that surround the city.

1

Begin your journey exploring Sedona's picturesque downtown district. Surrounded by dramatic red cliffs, the town combines outdoor adventure with local art galleries, restaurants, and southwestern culture unlike anywhere else in Arizona.

2

Built directly into the surrounding red rock cliffs, the Chapel of the Holy Cross is one of Sedona's most recognizable landmarks. The dramatic architecture and panoramic views create a memorable blend of spirituality and natural beauty.

3

One of the most accessible viewpoints in Sedona, Airport Mesa offers spectacular panoramic views of the surrounding red-rock formations. The overlook is especially beautiful during late afternoon when the cliffs glow with rich desert color.

4

End the day at Sedona's most famous sunset location. From the overlook, visitors enjoy unobstructed views of Cathedral Rock, Bell Rock, Courthouse Butte, and countless sandstone formations illuminated by the setting sun.

Day 2

Today explores Sedona's most iconic red-rock formations located south of town. These world-famous hikes showcase towering sandstone cliffs, open slickrock terrain, and some of the area's most photographed landscapes.

1

This scenic loop circles two of Sedona's most famous landmarks. Bell Rock rises prominently from the desert floor while Courthouse Butte towers above the surrounding landscape, creating one of the area's most recognizable views.

2

Arguably Sedona's most iconic hike, Cathedral Rock climbs steep slickrock slopes to reach magnificent panoramic views. The towering sandstone spires create one of the most photographed landscapes in Arizona.

3

Explore easier trails through riparian habitats, red-rock scenery, and Oak Creek landscapes. The park provides a quieter experience while offering excellent views of Sedona's surrounding geology.

Day 3

Venture into western Sedona today, where hidden caves, natural arches, and secluded canyon trails reveal a more adventurous side of Red Rock Country.

1

Devil's Bridge is Sedona's largest natural sandstone arch and one of Arizona's most famous hikes. The trail leads through classic desert scenery before reaching the dramatic bridge suspended high above the canyon floor.

2

A relatively gentle hike through a beautiful red-rock canyon, Fay Canyon offers towering cliffs, peaceful scenery, and opportunities to explore hidden rock formations along the route.

3

Hidden within the cliffs above Long Canyon, Birthing Cave has become one of Sedona's most photographed locations. The large sandstone opening frames expansive views across the surrounding desert landscape.

4

One of Sedona's more challenging destinations, Keyhole Cave rewards hikers with a massive sandstone cavern hidden high within the cliffs. The unique opening and sweeping views make it a favorite among adventurous hikers.

Day 4

Today's adventures take you into Sedona's northern canyon country, home to lush creek corridors, hidden arches, and some of the most scenic hiking trails in Arizona.

1

Frequently considered Sedona's most beautiful canyon hike, West Fork follows a stream through a narrow canyon shaded by trees and towering sandstone walls. Multiple creek crossings and colorful cliffs make this a uniquely refreshing desert hike.

2

This short climb leads to a broad mesa top offering spectacular 360-degree views of Sedona's red rocks. The elevated perspective makes it one of the area's finest panoramic overlooks.

3

Less crowded than many of Sedona's famous hikes, Secret Canyon features beautiful red-rock scenery, quiet canyon environments, and expansive views along the upper reaches of the trail.

4

Named after a historic aircraft crash site nearby, Vultee Arch is a striking sandstone formation located in one of Sedona's quieter backcountry areas. The trail provides excellent red-rock scenery throughout.

Day 5

The final day combines Sedona's most adventurous hikes with opportunities to relax and enjoy the town's artistic and cultural side. This balanced conclusion provides an unforgettable finale to your Red Rock Country adventure.

1

One of Sedona's most popular adventures, Soldier Pass traverses fascinating geological features including sinkholes and scenic red-rock terrain before leading to the famous Subway Cave, a distinctive curved sandstone cavern overlooking the surrounding landscape.

2

Boynton Canyon offers one of Sedona's most diverse hiking experiences, winding through forests, towering cliffs, and dramatic canyon scenery while providing panoramic views of the surrounding red-rock wilderness.

3

Cool off along Oak Creek at one of Arizona's most beloved state parks. The smooth sandstone creek bed creates a natural waterslide that has attracted visitors for generations.

4

Inspired by a traditional Mexican village, Tlaquepaque features cobblestone walkways, courtyards, galleries, boutiques, and restaurants. It is one of Sedona's most charming destinations for shopping and dining.

5

Strong hikers looking for a challenge may substitute or add one of Sedona's more demanding adventures. Bear Mountain delivers outstanding summit views, Hangover Trail traverses dramatic slickrock terrain, Robbers Roost offers a lesser-known viewpoint, and A.B. Young Trail near Oak Creek Canyon climbs through forested slopes to exceptional canyon panoramas.
