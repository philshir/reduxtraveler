Explore our curated selection of top itineraries, each designed to offer a unique and unforgettable travel experience. From the vibrant energy of Las Vegas to the serene landscapes of Utah's national parks, these itineraries provide a perfect blend of adventure, culture, and natural beauty. Whether you're seeking a short getaway or an extended journey, our top itineraries are crafted to inspire your next adventure.

Mission Santa Barbara

Las Vegas And Utah 5 National Parks 7 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 7-day odyssey pairs the electric spectacle of the Las Vegas Strip with the silent grandeur of Utah's five national parks, showcasing sandstone arches, canyons of hoodoos, and dramatic canyon rims carved over 200 million years.

Mission Santa Barbara

Las Vegas Zion And Death Valley National Parks 5 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 5-day journey pairs the glitter of the Las Vegas Strip with two of the Southwest's most extreme landscapes, from Zion National Park's towering red walls to the scorched, sea-level-shattering basin of Death Valley.

Mission Santa Barbara

Las Vegas And Grand Canyon National Park 5 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 5-day journey pairs the electric spectacle of the Las Vegas Strip with the South Rim of the Grand Canyon, focusing on its most celebrated overlooks along Desert View Drive, Grand Canyon Village, and Hermit Road.

Pismo Beach, CA

Pacific Coast Highway 5 Day Itinerary★5.0

★ 4.5

This 5-day road trip follows California's Pacific Coast Highway through Big Sur, grouping dramatic cliffs, waterfalls, historic lighthouses, state parks, and wildlife viewing areas along the most spectacular stretch of the state's coastline.

San Francisco 5 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 5-day itinerary combines San Francisco's most iconic attractions, from Alcatraz Island and Fisherman's Wharf to the Golden Gate Bridge and Muir Woods, with the city's historic neighborhoods and coastal scenery.

San Diego 3 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 3-day itinerary explores San Diego's best destinations grouped geographically, from the museums and gardens of Balboa Park to the dramatic coastline of La Jolla and Torrey Pines, historic waterfront landmarks, and legendary beaches.

Downtown Los Angeles sunset with Mount Baldy in the background

Hawaii Oahu Maui Big Island 7 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 7-day itinerary island-hops across Oahu, Maui, and Hawaii Island, pairing each island's most emblematic sights — royal palaces and Pearl Harbor, the Road to Hana and Haleakala, and an active volcano along the historic Kona Coast.

Downtown Los Angeles sunset with Mount Baldy in the background
Downtown Los Angeles, CC BY-SA 4.0, via Wikimedia Commons; Image Size Adjusted

Fall Colors in New England 7 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 7-day itinerary follows the fall foliage from Boston to the White Mountains of New Hampshire, Vermont's Green Mountains, and the Berkshires of western Massachusetts, combining historic towns, scenic drives, and iconic autumn landscapes.

Colorado Springs Denver And Mountain Towns 7 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 7-day itinerary travels from Colorado Springs' 14,000-foot peaks to Denver's gold-domed downtown, Boulder's college-town energy, and a string of gold-rush towns turned ski resorts ending in Aspen.

Flagstaff Area Grand Canyon And Sedona 7 Day Itinerary★5.0

★ 4.0

This 7-day itinerary moves from Flagstaff's volcanic fields and ancient pueblos through two full days on the Grand Canyon's South Rim to four days exploring Sedona's slot canyons, vortex sites, and red rock spires.

Albuquerque Santa Fe And Northern New Mexico 5 Day Itinerary★5.0

★ 4.0

This 5-day itinerary follows a route through Albuquerque, Santa Fe, and the surrounding high desert, combining the adobe streets of Santa Fe, the volcanic panoramas of Valles Caldera, the ancient cliff dwellings of Bandelier, and the Rio Grande Gorge.

New York City 5 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 5-day itinerary follows a geographic route through Manhattan and Brooklyn, combining New York City's iconic landmarks, world-famous museums, vibrant neighborhoods, and unforgettable skyline views.

Washington DC 5 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 5-day itinerary follows a geographic route through Washington, D.C.'s most iconic landmarks, museums, and memorials, including the National Mall, Capitol Hill, Georgetown, and Embassy Row.

Washington DC, Baltimore And Philadelphia 7 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 7-day itinerary moves north along the Mid-Atlantic corridor with three days in Washington D.C.'s monuments and Smithsonian museums, two days in Baltimore's Inner Harbor and Mount Vernon neighborhood, and two days in Philadelphia's Old City and Ben Franklin Parkway.

Great Smoky Mountains Blue Ridge Parkway And Shenandoah National Park 6 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 6-day journey traces the Appalachian spine from Gatlinburg's mountain-resort energy through the Great Smoky Mountains' coves and waterfalls, along the Blue Ridge Parkway, and up onto Shenandoah National Park's wildflower-studded ridgeline.

Savannah And Charleston 4 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 4-day journey moves between Savannah's oak-canopied squares and Charleston's pastel-colored row houses, blending architecture, history, and coastal scenery across more than three centuries of Lowcountry history.

Yellowstone And Grand Teton National Parks 5 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 5-day adventure combines Yellowstone's erupting geysers and colorful hot springs with Grand Teton National Park's rugged granite peaks and alpine lakes across the Greater Yellowstone Ecosystem.

New England Connecticut Rhode Island Massachusetts 7 Day Itinerary★5.0

This 7-day itinerary moves along the New England coast from Mystic's whaling wharves and Newport's Gilded Age mansions to Boston's Freedom Trail, Plymouth, and the lighthouse-studded shores of Cape Cod.
