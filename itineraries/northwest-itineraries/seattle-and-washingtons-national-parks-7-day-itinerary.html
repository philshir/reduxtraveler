Washington's Greatest National Parks: Seattle, Mount Rainier, North Cascades & Olympic National Park

7 Days

This seven-day journey showcases the very best of Washington State, combining the vibrant culture of Seattle with three of America's most spectacular national parks. From the iconic skyline of the Emerald City to the glacier-covered slopes of Mount Rainier, from the rugged wilderness of the North Cascades to the temperate rainforests and wild Pacific coastline of Olympic National Park, this itinerary follows a carefully planned route designed to minimize driving while maximizing unforgettable experiences. Like a National Geographic expedition, each day reveals a different face of the Pacific Northwest: urban innovation, alpine wilderness, ancient forests, turquoise lakes, snow-capped volcanoes, and some of the most pristine landscapes in North America.

Day 1 - Seattle Waterfront & Historic Downtown

Your adventure begins in Seattle, a city shaped by water, commerce, and innovation. Today explores the historic waterfront and downtown core where Seattle's maritime heritage, coffee culture, and modern skyline converge. Throughout the day you'll move between bustling public markets, historic districts, waterfront attractions, and panoramic observation decks that reveal the geography defining the Pacific Northwest.

1

Begin at Seattle's most famous destination, where fishmongers, flower vendors, artists, and local businesses have operated continuously for more than a century. Overlooking Elliott Bay, Pike Place Market offers the perfect introduction to Seattle's culture and character.

2

The original Starbucks location and the colorful Gum Wall sit just steps from the market. Together they represent both Seattle's global influence and its quirky local personality.

3

Explore the marine ecosystems of Puget Sound before riding the Seattle Great Wheel for spectacular waterfront and mountain views.

4

Seattle's oldest neighborhood preserves the city's frontier past through grand brick architecture and fascinating stories from the Klondike Gold Rush era.

5

Conclude the day high above downtown where sweeping views stretch across Puget Sound, Mount Rainier, Elliott Bay, and the Olympic Mountains.

Day 2 - Seattle Center & North Cascades Gateway

Today begins with Seattle's most iconic landmarks before transitioning into the dramatic mountain landscapes of the North Cascades. You'll experience futuristic architecture, artistic expression, and stunning viewpoints before driving one of the most scenic highways in America.

1

Seattle's defining landmark offers magnificent views over the city, Puget Sound, Mount Rainier, and the distant Cascades.

2

Explore brilliant glass artwork, Seattle Center's iconic fountain, and one of America's most innovative museums dedicated to music and popular culture.

3

Capture Seattle's most famous skyline view before visiting the remarkable glass domes that symbolize the city's role as a center of technological innovation.

4

Leave the city behind and enter the rugged North Cascades, where jagged peaks, deep valleys, and alpine forests create one of North America's most dramatic mountain landscapes.

5

The turquoise waters of Diablo Lake rank among Washington's most photographed sights, surrounded by glacier-carved peaks and dense forests.

Day 3 - North Cascades National Park

Today is devoted entirely to the wilderness of North Cascades National Park. Massive mountains, hanging glaciers, rushing rivers, and old-growth forests create an environment often compared to the Alps.

1

Begin the day at Newhalem, the historic gateway to the park, then follow the easy Sterling Munro Trail for panoramic mountain views.

2

Historic engineering achievements blend seamlessly into the surrounding wilderness at these unique North Cascades attractions.

3

Walk beneath towering old-growth trees while learning about the ecology of the Pacific Northwest forest ecosystem.

4

One of the park's premier river valley hikes showcases emerald forests, rushing glacial waters, and magnificent mountain scenery.

5

Often considered the signature hike of the North Cascades, Cascade Pass delivers extraordinary alpine views and access to one of the park's most spectacular landscapes.

Day 4 - Mount Rainier National Park: Paradise Region

Leaving the North Cascades behind, you arrive at Mount Rainier, a 14,410-foot volcano that dominates Washington's skyline. Today explores the famous Paradise region, known for wildflower meadows, glaciers, waterfalls, and some of the best mountain views in the country.

1

Enter the park through the historic Longmire district before exploring one of Rainier's most accessible and scenic trails.

2

Paradise lives up to its name with alpine meadows, glaciers, and sweeping mountain views. The Skyline Trail is widely considered the park's crown jewel hike.

3

Two of Mount Rainier's most famous attractions provide dramatic waterfall scenery and mirror-like reflections of the mountain.

4

This rewarding hike offers outstanding perspectives of Rainier, surrounding valleys, and distant volcanic peaks.

Day 5 - Mount Rainier National Park: Sunrise Region

Explore the higher and wilder side of Mount Rainier. The Sunrise area provides some of the closest views of glaciers, alpine tundra, and the immense bulk of Washington's tallest mountain.

1

Sunrise is the highest vehicle-accessible area in the park and serves as the ideal base for exploring Rainier's alpine world.

2

These hikes bring visitors remarkably close to the mountain, offering breathtaking glacier views and unforgettable alpine scenery.

3

Explore glacial valleys and some of the most dramatic geological landscapes within Mount Rainier National Park.

4

Wildflower meadows, mountain streams, and towering views make Summerland one of Rainier's most beloved backcountry destinations.

Day 6 - Olympic National Park: Mountains & Rain Forests

Cross to the Olympic Peninsula where an entirely different world awaits. Today explores glacier-clad mountains, ancient rain forests, and river valleys that feel untouched by time.

1

Enjoy spectacular views of Mount Olympus, surrounding peaks, and the Strait of Juan de Fuca from Olympic National Park's premier mountain destination.

2

One of the highest roads in Washington unfolds across alpine landscapes rarely seen outside remote wilderness areas.

3

A crystal-clear mountain lake and a beautiful waterfall showcase Olympic's diverse ecosystems.

4

Moss-draped trees, giant ferns, and extraordinary biodiversity make the Hoh one of the finest temperate rain forests on Earth.

5

Ancient forests and one of the park's most photogenic waterfalls provide a fitting conclusion to the day.

Day 7 - Olympic Coast & Return to Seattle

The final day explores Olympic National Park's legendary coastline before returning to Seattle. Rugged beaches, sea stacks, crashing surf, and remote wilderness bring this Pacific Northwest adventure to a memorable conclusion.

1

Dramatic sea stacks, driftwood-covered shorelines, and Pacific Ocean vistas make Ruby Beach one of Washington's most iconic coastal destinations.

2

Massive sea stacks and wild ocean scenery create one of the most dramatic landscapes in the Pacific Northwest.

3

The northwestern-most point of the contiguous United States offers a true wilderness coastline experience.

4

Before returning to Seattle, explore another magnificent rain forest where towering trees and tranquil lakes provide a final reminder of Washington's incredible natural diversity.
