Northwest travel itineraries showcase some of North America’s most dramatic scenery, from Pacific coastlines and volcanic peaks to vast wilderness and iconic national parks. Whether exploring Washington and Oregon’s coastal beauty, the mountain landscapes of Montana, Wyoming, and Idaho, or Alaska’s untamed wilderness, these journeys celebrate adventure and natural wonder on a grand scale.

Overview

The Northwest spans Washington, Oregon, Idaho, Montana, Wyoming, and Alaska, with volcanoes, rainforests, glaciers, and some of the country's largest wilderness areas. Seattle and Portland make easy urban bases, with great food, coffee, and craft beer.

National parks include Mount Rainier, Olympic, North Cascades, Glacier, Yellowstone, Grand Teton, and Redwood, while Alaska adds Denali, Kenai Fjords, Glacier Bay, and remote parks reached only by plane or boat. The Oregon Coast and Columbia River Gorge are among the region's best scenic drives.

Mountain roads and passes often stay closed by snow into early summer, and Alaska's parks are best visited from June through August. Some popular parks may require advance reservations, so check current requirements before your visit.
