Northeast travel itineraries showcase a blend of rugged coastlines, scenic mountains, charming villages, and historic cities. From Maine’s rocky shores and Vermont’s countryside to New Hampshire’s White Mountains and the colonial heritage of Massachusetts, Connecticut, and Rhode Island, these journeys combine natural beauty, maritime culture, and New England tradition into unforgettable regional adventures.

Overview

The Northeast packs colonial history, rocky coastline, and mountain scenery into a compact region covering Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Connecticut, New Hampshire, Vermont, and Maine. Boston, Salem, Plymouth, and Newport trace the story of early America.

Outdoor highlights include Acadia National Park on the Maine coast, the beaches of Cape Cod and Martha's Vineyard, the White Mountains of New Hampshire, and Vermont's Green Mountains. Lakes, covered bridges, and small villages fill the countryside in between.

Fall foliage, usually peaking from late September to mid-October, is the busiest season, so book lodging early. Summer is best for beaches and islands, and some coastal attractions close or reduce hours in winter.
