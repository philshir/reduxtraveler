Multi-State Itineraries

America’s greatest multi-state road trips transform the journey into the destination, crossing dramatic coastlines, mountain ranges, deserts, and small-town main streets that reveal the nation’s remarkable diversity. Whether following the historic path of Route 66, winding along the Pacific Coast, exploring the national parks of the Southwest, or tracing New England’s scenic byways, travelers encounter ever-changing landscapes, regional cultures, and iconic landmarks that showcase the freedom, adventure, and discovery at the heart of the American road trip experience.

Overview

Multi-state itineraries link destinations across state lines, making it easier to build longer road trips around a theme, a region, or a historic route. Options range from loops through New England and the Utah national parks to routes that span much of the country.

Historic routes such as Route 66, the Oregon Trail, and the Lewis and Clark Expedition follow paths that shaped American history, while the Civil Rights Movement itinerary visits key places of the struggle for equality across the South.

Because these trips cover long distances, plan for several driving days, check seasonal road and park conditions, and book lodging early in popular areas such as national parks and fall foliage destinations.
