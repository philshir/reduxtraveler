Southeast travel itineraries lead visitors through mountain vistas, colonial landmarks, coastal escapes, and vibrant southern cities. Whether exploring West Virginia’s rugged landscapes, Virginia’s historic treasures, the Carolinas’ beaches, Georgia’s cultural heritage, or Florida’s world-famous attractions, these journeys showcase the warmth, diversity, and beauty of the American Southeast.

Overview

The Southeast stretches from the Florida Keys north through Georgia, the Carolinas, and Virginia, with beaches, historic cities, and mountain scenery. Savannah, Charleston, Richmond, and Colonial Williamsburg preserve centuries of American history.

Florida offers Orlando's theme parks, Gulf and Atlantic beaches, and national parks such as Everglades, Biscayne, and Dry Tortugas. Further north, Congaree, Shenandoah, and New River Gorge offer forests and mountain hiking, and the Outer Banks offer barrier-island beaches.

Summers are hot and humid and hurricane season runs from June through November, so spring and fall are good times for most destinations. Dry Tortugas National Park is reached only by ferry or seaplane from Key West.
