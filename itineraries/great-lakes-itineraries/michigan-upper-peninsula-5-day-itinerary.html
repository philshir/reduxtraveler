Michigan's Upper Peninsula: Waterfalls, Wild Shores, and Copper Country

5 Days

This 5-day journey explores the very best of Michigan's Upper Peninsula, a rugged region where towering waterfalls, ancient forests, dramatic Lake Superior coastlines, and rich mining heritage create one of North America's most unforgettable road trips. Following a route that minimizes backtracking while maximizing scenic beauty, this itinerary combines the storytelling spirit of a National Geographic expedition with the practical structure of a TripAdvisor travel guide. From the crystal-clear waters of Kitch-iti-Kipi and the colorful cliffs of Pictured Rocks to the wild mountains of the Porcupine Mountains and the historic copper towns of the Keweenaw Peninsula, each day reveals a new chapter in the story of the Upper Peninsula.

Day 1

Your Upper Peninsula adventure begins in the eastern U.P., where freshwater springs, dense forests, and some of Michigan's most famous waterfalls provide the perfect introduction to this wild and beautiful region.

1

Begin your journey among towering pines and peaceful wetlands at Palms Book State Park, one of Michigan's most treasured natural areas. The park protects a unique freshwater ecosystem hidden within the forests of the Upper Peninsula.

2

Known as "The Big Spring," Kitch-iti-Kipi is Michigan's largest freshwater spring. Viewed from a self-operated observation raft, its crystal-clear waters reveal ancient tree trunks, bubbling sand vents, and brilliant shades of blue and emerald beneath the surface.

3

Spend the afternoon exploring one of the Midwest's most spectacular waterfall destinations. The Upper Falls thunder more than 200 feet across as tannin-stained waters create the famous copper-colored appearance that has earned them the nickname "Root Beer Falls."

Day 2

Today follows the southern shore of Lake Superior into one of the most photogenic regions in the Great Lakes. Waterfalls, sandstone cliffs, hidden beaches, and endless views over the world's largest freshwater lake dominate the landscape.

1

The colorful sandstone cliffs of Pictured Rocks rise dramatically above Lake Superior, stained by centuries of mineral-rich groundwater. Sea caves, arches, beaches, waterfalls, and towering overlooks combine to create one of the most iconic landscapes in the Great Lakes region.

2

Hidden within the forests near Munising, Manido Falls rewards visitors with a peaceful setting where water tumbles through a quiet woodland environment far removed from the crowds.

3

Continue exploring the waterfall-rich forests surrounding Pictured Rocks. Nawadaha Falls provides another scenic stop where rushing water, moss-covered rocks, and towering trees create a classic Upper Peninsula landscape.

Day 3

The journey continues west into the legendary Porcupine Mountains, where ancient forests, dramatic valleys, and some of the most stunning wilderness scenery in the Midwest showcase the untamed character of the Upper Peninsula.

1

Begin the day at one of Michigan's most beautiful waterfalls. Broad cascades flow over ancient rock formations, creating a powerful yet elegant display that is easily accessible from a scenic boardwalk.

2

Often called "The Porkies," these mountains contain some of the largest remaining old-growth forests in the Midwest. Winding scenic roads, rugged hiking trails, and remarkable overlooks showcase the wild beauty of northern Michigan.

3

Arguably the most famous viewpoint in Michigan's Upper Peninsula, Lake of the Clouds reveals a spectacular panorama of forested ridges surrounding a shimmering inland lake. Sunrise and sunset here rank among the finest views in the Great Lakes.

4

Follow trails along the Presque Isle River as it races through rocky gorges toward Lake Superior. Rapids, waterfalls, and scenic overlooks highlight the raw power of the river and the rugged character of the western Upper Peninsula.

Day 4

Today enters Michigan's historic Copper Country, where nineteenth-century mining booms transformed remote forests into thriving industrial communities. History, engineering, and culture blend with spectacular Lake Superior scenery.

1

Start the day exploring the nationally significant sites that tell the story of the copper industry which helped shape both Michigan and the nation during the industrial age.

2

Once one of the world's wealthiest mining communities, Calumet preserves remarkable architecture, historic churches, grand civic buildings, and stories of immigration from across Europe.

3

Venture underground into one of the region's historic copper mines and experience firsthand the challenging conditions faced by generations of miners beneath the Keweenaw Peninsula.

4

Spend the afternoon exploring scenic byways and historic landmarks connected by the Copper Country Trail, providing a deeper appreciation of the communities and industries that defined the region.

Day 5

Your final day explores the dramatic northern reaches of the Keweenaw Peninsula, where rugged coastlines, historic forts, scenic mountain drives, and endless Lake Superior views provide a spectacular conclusion to your Upper Peninsula adventure.

1

Begin the day driving through the rugged landscapes of the Keweenaw Peninsula, where forests, rocky shorelines, and Lake Superior vistas define one of Michigan's most remote and scenic regions.

2

Known for its expansive Lake Superior shoreline and beautiful sunsets, McLain State Park offers outstanding views across the world's largest freshwater lake.

3

Explore a restored nineteenth-century military outpost built during Michigan's copper boom. Historic buildings and exhibits transport visitors back to the frontier days of the Upper Peninsula.

4

At the northern tip of the peninsula, Copper Harbor offers rugged shorelines, forested hills, and a remote atmosphere that feels worlds away from modern city life. It serves as one of Michigan's premier outdoor destinations.

5

Finish your journey with a drive along one of the most scenic roads in the Midwest. High above Lake Superior, Brockway Mountain Drive delivers sweeping panoramas of forested ridges, rocky coastlines, and seemingly endless water, providing a fitting finale to five unforgettable days in Michigan's Upper Peninsula.
