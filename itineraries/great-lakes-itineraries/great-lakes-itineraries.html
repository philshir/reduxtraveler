From Chicago’s skyline and Michigan’s coastlines to Minnesota’s lakes and Wisconsin’s small-town charm, these journeys showcase outdoor recreation, regional traditions, and some of the nation’s most distinctive landscapes.

Overview

The Great Lakes region surrounds the largest group of freshwater lakes on Earth. These itineraries cover Illinois, Indiana, Michigan, Minnesota, Ohio, and Wisconsin, combining big-city culture with lakeshore beaches, forests, and quiet small towns.

Cities such as Chicago, Detroit, Cleveland, Cincinnati, Columbus, Indianapolis, and Minneapolis-St. Paul offer museums, architecture, sports, and food. Lakeshore getaways like Door County and Minnesota's North Shore add lighthouses, state parks, and scenic drives, and national parks include Isle Royale, Voyageurs, Cuyahoga Valley, and Indiana Dunes.

Summer is the busiest season on the lakes, and fall brings colorful foliage. Winters are cold and snowy, and remote parks such as Isle Royale are open only seasonally and reached by boat or seaplane, so plan ahead and check current conditions before you go.
