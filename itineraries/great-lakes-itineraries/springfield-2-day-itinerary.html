Springfield, Illinois: Walking in Lincoln's Footsteps

2 Days

Few American cities are as closely connected to a single historical figure as Springfield is to Abraham Lincoln. Before becoming the nation's sixteenth president, Lincoln lived, practiced law, raised a family, and launched his political career in Springfield. Today, visitors can explore a remarkable collection of historic homes, museums, monuments, and government buildings that tell the story of both Lincoln and the state of Illinois.

This two-day itinerary combines the storytelling style of National Geographic with the practical pacing of a TripAdvisor-style journey. Locations are grouped by proximity, allowing travelers to easily explore Springfield's historic downtown, presidential sites, museums, and beautiful parks while experiencing one of America's most important historical destinations.

Day 1

Your first day focuses on the heart of historic Springfield, where Abraham Lincoln built his career and where many of the state's most significant historical landmarks remain preserved. Walking through downtown offers a unique opportunity to experience the city much as Lincoln would have known it during the nineteenth century.

1

Begin your journey exploring the historic heart of Springfield. Government buildings, preserved nineteenth-century architecture, monuments, and cultural attractions create an atmosphere where Illinois history remains highly visible throughout the city center.

2

One of the premier presidential museums in the United States, this immersive attraction brings Lincoln's life and legacy to life through exhibits, artifacts, multimedia presentations, and recreations of pivotal moments in American history.

3

This beautifully restored building served as Illinois' state capitol during Lincoln's political rise. Here he argued cases, delivered speeches, and announced his famous "House Divided" address.

4

Walk through the only home Abraham Lincoln ever owned. Preserved within an entire historic neighborhood, the site offers an intimate look at the family life of Lincoln before his presidency.

5

End the afternoon exploring Union Station and nearby Union Square Park, where restored railroad history, public spaces, and downtown architecture provide a glimpse into Springfield's transportation and civic heritage.

Day 2

Today expands beyond Lincoln's life to explore Springfield's cultural institutions, architectural landmarks, beautiful park system, and the sites associated with Lincoln's final resting place. The day combines history, architecture, and scenic green spaces into a fitting conclusion to your Springfield experience.

1

The current Illinois State Capitol dominates Springfield's skyline with its impressive dome and grand interior spaces. As the center of state government, it represents the continuing political importance of Illinois' capital city.

2

Explore exhibits covering Illinois' natural history, Indigenous cultures, geology, art, and scientific discoveries. The museum provides a broader understanding of the state's rich heritage beyond Springfield itself.

3

Considered one of Frank Lloyd Wright's finest Prairie-style masterpieces, the Dana-Thomas House showcases remarkable architecture, original furnishings, and innovative design concepts that influenced modern architecture.

4

Visit Oak Ridge Cemetery, where Abraham Lincoln, Mary Todd Lincoln, and several members of the Lincoln family are buried. The monumental tomb remains one of the most important historical pilgrimage sites in the United States. Nearby, visitors can also learn about Lincoln's original temporary burial location before his final tomb was completed.

5

Conclude your visit with a relaxing stroll through one of Springfield's most beautiful green spaces. The botanical garden, landscaped grounds, walking paths, and gardens provide a peaceful setting to reflect on the remarkable history explored throughout the trip.
